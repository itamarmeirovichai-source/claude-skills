3. Costs: realistic one-way costs per instrument at 1×, plus a 2× cost run (the fast-market and outside-hours stress) in which net mean > 0 with t ≥ 1.65 is required, not the full bar. Floors unless measured better, per contract, market order, regular hours: ES 0.30 points (one tick) per side plus about $2.50 commission and fees per side; MES $0.80 commission and fees per side plus 0.25 points (one tick) per side; NQ 0.25 points (one tick) per side plus about $2.50 commission and fees, or 0.375 points per unit traded when a unit is a flip; SPY 0.5 bp per unit; CFDs per side 0.5–1.5 bp for FX and indices, 1 bp gold, 2.5 bp WTI, 8 bp natural gas, plus FTMO swaps when held across the close, on gross notional per calendar day held, long and short side taken separately from the symbol page with fetch date, never assumed symmetric, triple-swap day included (indicative long swaps: index about −8% a year US, −6.5% EU, −4.9% JP225; crypto −30% a year both sides; FX markup about 1% a year); crypto perps 7 bp per side plus actual funding. Market orders at 02:00 ET, at opens and at settlements get extra slippage. Nothing passes on 1× cost only.
4. Significance: the sign is fixed in the registry row (one-sided; a variant whose sign was not fixed occupies two counts and is tested at z(0.025/N_total), about 3.85). Compute everything on daily mark-to-market P&L; trade-level series only for strategies flat by day end, with day-clustered standard errors. OOS net mean > 0 with t = min(iid t, Newey-West t at lag max(10, longest holding period in days); day-clustered t for trade-level series) ≥ z(0.05/N_total), about 3.68 at N_total = 430. 5. Multiple testing: criterion 4 is the only multiplicity gate (family-wise error 5% across N_total). Report, do not gate: the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey and López de Prado 2014) with its inputs printed (N_total; variance of trial Sharpes = max(empirical, 1/T_years); skew; kurtosis; T) and, for families with ≥ 8 in-sample configurations, CSCV with 16 splits (PBO, degradation slope, probability of OOS loss of the in-sample-selected configuration); otherwise "n/a (single pre-registered variant)". DSR below 0.50 or a CSCV probability of OOS loss above 0.10 is a material Red Team objection under criterion 10 and must be answered in writing.
6. Null models: alpha t against buy-and-hold of the same basket at the same exposure ≥ the bar (for intraday strategies the benchmark holds the instrument over the same minutes at the same notional); the strategy beats 95% of at least 10,000 random-entry strategies with the same holding-time distribution and trade count; a shuffled-signal permutation test with at least 1,000 × N_family permutations (in 20-day blocks when the signal is autocorrelated) gives p < 0.05/N_family; for event strategies, placebo days show no effect.
7. Stability: a calendar year counts only if the OOS holds ≥ 200 of its trading days; net positive in at least round-half-up(0.7 × years) of them (2 of 3, 3 of 4, 4 of 5, 7 of 10); no single year holds more than 50% of OOS net profit, no single month more than 50%; profitable in two of three terciles of the traded instrument's trailing 20-day realized volatility, boundaries fixed in sample; parameter neighbourhood: each continuous parameter moved ±25% one at a time, each discrete parameter one step each way, run once on OOS, every result printed and net positive (these runs never change the headline and do not add to N); the edge keeps at least half its value when entry is delayed one minute.
8. 11.
