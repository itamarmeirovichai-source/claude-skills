# יום הפקה — 10 סקריפטים

**איך זה עובד:** מצלם את עצמך מדבר את 10 הסקריפטים (2-3 שעות). מעלה ל-Higgsfield, מייצר 5-8 דמויות לכל סקריפט. חותך עם שוטי המוצר הנקיים. **תפוקה: 40-80 קריאייטיבים ייחודיים.**

**כלל הזהב:** המוצר תמיד נקי (בלי restyling), האדם תמיד restyled.

## שוטים נקיים לצלם בנפרד — לפני הסקריפטים

1. הכלי נע לאורך קו הלסת — 8 שניות
2. הכלי נע מתחת לעין כלפי חוץ — 8 שניות

6. הסט כולו על משטח שיש/עץ — 5 שניות
7. הכלי יוצא מהמקרר עם אדים — 5 שניות
8. יד פותחת את האריזה — 6 שניות
9. סרום נמרח והכלי מפזר אותו — 8 שניות
10. הכלי מונח על ערמת מגבות — 4 שניות

## 10 הסקריפטים

### סקריפט 1 — נפיחות בוקר (הראשי)
> This is my face thirty seconds after waking up. Puffy eyes, heavy cheeks, no jawline at all.
> Every morning, fluid settles in your face while you sleep flat.
> Three minutes with this, working from the center of the face outward — and the swelling moves.
> *[cut to after]*
> Same face. Same morning. Three minutes.
> It lasts through the day. Then you do it again tomorrow.

### סקריפט 2 — נשבר לי הקודם
> My third gua sha broke last week. Snapped in half while I was using it.
> Fifteen dollars, three of them, all broken. That's forty-five dollars of stone in my trash.
> So I stopped buying the cheap ones.
> *[cut to pressure shot]*
> This is real stone, thicker through the middle, and the roller doesn't squeak.
> Thirty days. If it breaks, you get a new one.

### סקריפט 3 — מתח בלסת
> I clench my jaw all night and I had no idea until my dentist told me.
> The tension doesn't sit where you think. It sits right here, under the cheekbone and along the jaw.
> You work along the muscle, slow, with pressure.
> It releases. That's the whole thing.
> Three minutes before bed and my jaw stops feeling locked.

### סקריפט 4 — טכניקה נכונה
> Everyone is doing this wrong, and it's the same mistake every time.
> You're pulling down. Down does nothing.
> Always outward and up — center of the face toward the ear, then down the neck.
> Light pressure. Slow. Eight strokes per section.
> That's it. That's the technique.

### סקריפט 5 — 10 דקות לעצמי
> Ten minutes. Before anyone else in the house wakes up.
> This isn't really about my face.
> It's the only part of the day where nobody needs anything from me.
> I make the coffee, I sit down, and I do this.
> If that's the only reason you buy it, that's a good enough reason.

### סקריפט 6 — לפני אירוע
> Twenty minutes before I have to leave, and I look like I haven't slept.
> Cold tool out of the fridge. Three minutes, both sides.
> *[cut to after]*
> Not makeup. Not a filter. Just the swelling moved.
> This is the thing I do before every single time I need to look awake.

### סקריפט 7 — הסקין-קר שלך
> You're spending eighty dollars on a serum and rubbing it in with your fingers.
> Most of it sits on the surface and dries there.
> Apply the serum, then work it in with the stone, outward and up.
> The product goes where you put it instead of onto your hands.
> Same serum. You just stopped wasting it.

### סקריפט 8 — כנות על זמניות
> Nobody tells you this, so I will: the results are temporary.
> You de-puff in the morning, it lasts through the day, and tomorrow you wake up puffy again.
> That's how it works. Anyone telling you otherwise is selling you something.
> I still do it every single morning — because looking awake today is worth three minutes today.

### סקריפט 9 — מתחילה
> If you've never done this, you're probably scared of doing it wrong.
> You can't really. Worst case, nothing happens.
> Start at the chin, go out along the jaw to the ear. Eight times.
> Then cheekbone, out to the ear. Then under the eye, very light.
> Finish down the neck. Done — that's the whole routine.

### סקריפט 10 — מתנה
> I bought one for me and three as gifts, and the gifts went over better than anything else I've given.
> It's the kind of thing people want and never buy for themselves.
> Comes in the box, with the case, ready to hand over.
> My sister texted me a photo of hers in her fridge.
