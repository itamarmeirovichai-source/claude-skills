Questions, answered straight.

Will it break?

Cheap sets snap because stone is brittle and thin through the middle. This is solid 304 stainless — it bends before it breaks, and it won't do either in normal use. If it breaks inside thirty days, we replace it and you keep the old one.

I've never done this. Will I do it wrong?

You can't really. Worst case, nothing happens. The four moves are above — chin to ear, cheekbone to ear, under the eye, then down the neck. Outward and up, always.

Are the results permanent?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. You de-puff in the morning and it lasts through the day. Most people do it daily for exactly that reason.

Does the roller squeak?

No. Squeaking is an open-bearing problem on cheap rollers. Ours is sealed.

Can I use it with my serum?

Yes, and it's the better way. Apply first, then work it in with the flat edge. The product goes into your skin instead of onto your fingers.
