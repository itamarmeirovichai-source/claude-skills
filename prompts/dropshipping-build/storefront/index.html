Questions

Answered straight.

Will it break?

Cheap sets snap because stone is brittle and thin through the middle. This is solid steel. If it does break inside thirty days we replace it, and you keep the old one.

I've never done this. Will I do it wrong?

You can't really. The worst case is that nothing happens. The four moves are above — chin to ear, cheekbone to ear, under the eye, then down the neck. Outward and up, always.

Are the results permanent?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. You de-puff in the morning and it holds through the day. Most people do it daily for exactly that reason.

Does the roller squeak?

No. Squeaking comes from an open bearing, which is what cheap rollers use. Ours is sealed.

Can I use it with my serum?

Yes, and it's the better way. Apply first, then work it in with the flat edge — the product goes into your skin instead of onto your fingers.

Why $42 when Amazon sells sets for $15?
