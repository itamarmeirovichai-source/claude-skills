## Safe list

- **FTMO** (CFDs). Bots and EAs allowed in writing. Swing accounts allow overnight and weekends, are exempt from the ±2-minute news ban, and run at 1:30 leverage against 1:100 on Standard. 5% daily loss measured from the balance at 00:00 CE(S)T including floating P&L and swaps; 10% static maximum loss. $400,000 cap per trader or per strategy, so many accounts do not raise the cap. VPS allowed. Under 2,000 server requests a day. Forex commission about $3 per lot round trip, indices commission-free with the cost in the spread, crypto 0.0325% per side; swaps nightly with triple swap on Wednesday. The Scaling Plan (+25% allocation every four months with a 90% split, conditions apply) is the one route past the $400k cap. A 30-day inactivity rule matters for low-frequency sleeves. An "FTMO Futures" product was reported in Nov 2026. T&C 7.8 lets one forbidden practice on one account end all agreements; 13.2.4 allows termination without cause on five business days' notice; the funded-stage agreement was never read by the previous program. Verify all of it on ftmo.com.
- **MyFundedFutures** (futures). Own-settings automation and own-account copying allowed in the firm's text; a third-party source says fully autonomous bots without human oversight are prohibited and the firm's text is silent on "unattended", so this needs a written answer. 5 sim-funded accounts if all are 25K/50K, 3 if any 100K/150K, with per-plan caps such as Rapid EOD 50K: 3 and Builder 50K: 1. Rapid funded uses an intraday trailing drawdown from the equity high-water mark, Rapid EOD an end-of-day one. 7-day inactivity rule. Rapid/Pro funded: no positions or resting orders within ±2 minutes of the firm's own Tier-1 list. No tight brackets. Flat by 4:10 PM ET. Profit split cut from 90% to 80% on Flex/Pro in Feb 2026. Live promotion merges all accounts into one.
- **Lucid Trading** (futures). Automated systems and trade copiers permitted. Five per household. Hedging across firms banned ("different firms or funded platforms"). Micro-scalping flagged when more than half the profit comes from holds of five seconds or less. Lucid Daily treats red-folder news as a hard breach. Accounts move live after 5 payouts and the sim accounts close with a $0 restart. Flat by 4:45 PM ET. Young firm; owner and entity not verified.
- **Topstep** (futures). Bots allowed on Express Funded accounts via the TopstepX/ProjectX API. The bot must run on the trader's own computer; VPS, VPN and remote servers banned ("your server can watch and record, but it cannot trade"). Live Funded has no API and a Live call-up closes every Express account. The maximum loss limit trails the end-of-day balance but is enforced in real time including unrealized P&L, locks at +$2,000 and resets to the initial balance after the first payout. 50K Express payouts: 50% of balance per request, capped at $2,000 on the Standard policy or $3,000 on the Consistency policy; the April 2026 cap change is supported only by secondary sources. Flat by 3:10 PM CT with auto-flatten at 3:08; holding past 3:10 is a breach. Bans hedging and coordinated trading across accounts; copier lead account with the lowest maximum position size. Since 2012; NFA-registered introducing-broker affiliate. Pro se suit McCrudden v. Topstepfunded LLC (M.D. Fla., Apr 2026) pending. Pass-through commissions about $3.78 round trip per mini and $1.22 per micro (CL $4.02, GC $4.32, MGC $1.92).
- **TradeDay** (futures). Own automation only inside NinjaTrader, Tradovate, TradingView, Jigsaw or Quantower; "we do not make platform APIs available"; third-party bots banned. Five funded sim plus up to five funded live. Copying from a personal account elsewhere into a TradeDay account is allowed. "If multiple users are making the same trades, then all the accounts will be shut down." Flat-by time: verify on its page. Owner and payout record not verified.

## Clauses that kill a bot fleet (verbatim or near-verbatim at the time)

1. One bot = one strategy = one cap (FTMO $400k per trader or strategy; FundedNext $300k per EA; E8 one strategy per user).
2. Same trades across own accounts = copy or group trading (The5ers, FTUK, FundedNext detection on entry price, exit price, lot size, symbol or time; HyroTrader §10.2(b); FTMO "Modus Operandi being repeated on more accounts").
3. Third-party or bought bots banned (Bulenox, TradeDay, Tradeify sole-owner proof, The5ers, Blue Guardian): the bot and any bridge must be Itamar's own code, provably.
4. Cross-firm clauses: Tradeify §6.6 and §6.7(i); Lucid's hedging ban across firms. One firm reversing while another still holds is a breach.
5. Where the bot may run: Topstep own device only; Funding Pips and Blue Guardian VPS bans; FTMO allows VPN/VPS.
6. Success ends the fleet: Topstep and Lucid Live call-ups close the sim accounts; MyFundedFutures merges them.
7. One finding voids everything: Topstep ToU §27; FTMO 7.8; Tradeify §6.7(h).
8. Discretion over payouts and rules: Tradeify §4.4; MyFundedFutures §3.2 (amend at any time); Bulenox "refuse to claim any profit"; Topstep "reject profit claims if abuse is suspected". Fee refunds almost nowhere.
9. Sim-fill exploitation clauses: MyFundedFutures and Topstep tight brackets and auto-breakeven; Lucid 5-second holds; Tradeify 10-second holds.
10. News windows: MyFundedFutures Rapid/Pro ±2 minutes including limit orders; FTMO Standard ±2 minutes; Lucid Daily hard breach; Topstep, Bulenox and FTMO Swing allow news trading.

## Structural facts

Every funded account is simulated; payouts are paid from the firm's cash at its discretion. The CFTC case against My Forex Funds was dismissed with prejudice in May 2025 over the CFTC's own misconduct; nobody ruled on the model. At least 50 firms closed in 2024 and about 80–100 by early 2026. ProjectX ended third-party service around Feb 2026 (platform risk). The trading day at futures firms starts at the 18:00 ET Globex reopen and ends at the firm's flat deadline, so overnight-session holds are inside one day and cross-close holds are not (verify per firm in writing). About 20–25 futures accounts across four firms plus one FTMO strategy at $400k is the realistic ceiling.
