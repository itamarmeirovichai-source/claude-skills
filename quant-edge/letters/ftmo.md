**Subject:** Written confirmation before purchase — automated strategy on 2-Step FTMO Challenge (Swing), several accounts, Israeli resident

Before I buy my first FTMO Challenge I would like written confirmation of a few points, so that my setup complies from day one. I am an individual resident in Israel.

1. **Automation.** I trade with a program I wrote myself (not a third-party EA). It runs fully automatically and unattended, on a VPS, and places identical trades at the same moment on every FTMO account I hold. Is this permitted on the 2-Step FTMO Challenge with the Swing account type, through Verification and on the FTMO Account? Which written rule governs it?

3. **One strategy, several accounts.** Your FAQ caps allocation at $400,000 "per trader or strategy" and warns that identical trading across accounts may lead to suspension. If four $100,000 Swing accounts are all traded by the same program, is that allowed, and is it counted as one strategy? Does the same program running at another firm matter?

4. **Swing.** Please confirm that a Swing FTMO Account may hold positions overnight and over weekends, is exempt from the 2-minute news restriction that applies to Standard FTMO Accounts, and runs at 1:30 leverage.

5. **Terms.** Clause 7.8 of the FTMO Challenge Terms (1 November 2026) allows termination of all Agreements after a Forbidden Trading Practice on one account; 13.2.4 allows termination without cause on five business days' notice. Are these also in the FTMO Account Terms and Conditions, and may I receive that document before purchase?

6. **Eligibility and payouts.** Please confirm that an Israeli resident may join and be paid, and which reward methods (bank wire, Visa Direct/Mastercard Send, Skrill, crypto) are available to Israel, with their limits.
