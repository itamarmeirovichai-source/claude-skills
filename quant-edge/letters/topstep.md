**Subject:** Written confirmation — own automated strategy via TopstepX API on several Express Funded Accounts, own-PC rule, copier, Israeli resident

Hello Topstep Trader Support,

I am an individual trader resident in Israel. Before buying Trading Combines I would like written confirmation of the following.

1. **Automation.** Your "TopstepX API Access" article says "custom automated strategies and bots are allowed via the TopstepX / ProjectX API", "all trading activity must originate from your personal device", and a server "can watch and record, but it cannot trade". My strategy is my own program. It runs fully automatically and unattended on my own PC at home and places identical trades at the same moment on up to five Express Funded Accounts. Is that permitted in the Trading Combine and the Express Funded Account, and under which written rule?

2. **Remote signal source.** May the signal be computed on a server I own — which never holds the API key and never sends orders — and relayed to my PC, where my program places the orders? Or must signal generation itself happen on the personal device?

3. **Copier and several accounts.** Should identical trades on several XFAs go through the TopstepX Trade Copier (Lead account with the lowest Maximum Position Size), or may my program submit each account's orders directly through the API? Does the same program running at other firms raise a "coordinated trading" concern?

4. **Live Funded Account.** The API Gateway "isn't available on Live", a call-up closes all Express Funded Accounts, and the Risk Team decides case by case. What are the criteria, and may a trader ask to remain on Express Funded Accounts? Is Israel treated as "XFA-only", and does the $200,000 total-payout cap apply?

5. **Eligibility and payouts.** Israel is on neither of your eligibility lists; please confirm that an Israeli resident may trade, earn funding and receive payouts, and that Wire/SWIFT (international, $30 fee) is the available rail.

Please reply by email so I can keep the answer on file.

Kind regards,
[Name] — [Topstep account email]
