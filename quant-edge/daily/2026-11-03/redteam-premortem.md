# Red Team pre-mortem, Day 0: how the quant-edge program had failed by 19 December 2026

Written 2026-11-03 as if dated 2026-12-19. Default verdict: refuted. Every probability in the table is an estimate; every other number cites a file or the scratchpad script named at the end.

## Narrative

19 December 2026. The memo says N1, and it is not an honest one. The Day-1 run launched the sweep, the data run, the letters, the Rules Keeper and the IBKR diagnosis together and died at the usage limit the way the previous program's run did on 3 November (`pr26/prompts/WORK-LOG.md`: 13 of 14 workflow agents killed); the second attempt produced 61 candidates, most of them rows whose evidence was a 2015–2024 equity curve on a blog, registered with OOS 2015–2024. The Databento confirmations arrived on Day 9; by then batch 1 had been re-ordered twice, CAL had never run on Databento bars, and three result files read parquet directly because `src/qe/gateway.py` (present on Day 0, untracked, without a planted-leak test) was "not ready yet". The Referee found OOS reads without log lines, counted them, and closed two families. One original lane-1 mechanism passed Gate 3 at t = 3.9 and Gate 4 after the Re-implementer matched; the F2-12 re-run had moved the floor at 20% volatility above 1.5 (estimate), so Gate 5 failed at the decayed Sharpe; on 5 December its hold-out mean fell outside the OOS 90% confidence interval, as it would for about half of all true edges under the criterion as written (mode 4), and the family closed. No firm letter had been answered, so even a pass would have had no firm. The memo could not separate "not tested, data not bought" from "tested and failed", and Itamar read "no edge found" where the truth was "no data bought, one mis-specified test, one unsent letter".

## Three things the Director must change in the plan this week

1. **Step 2.2 becomes a hard precondition of any P&L** (modes 1, 8, 10, 11). Commit `gateway.py` with planted-leak and hold-out-refusal tests, a test that fails on any `read_parquet`/`read_csv` outside the gateway, licence-status refusal for gated windows, and a synthetic end-to-end harness that prints the joint pass rate of all twelve criteria at Sharpe 1.5, 2.0 and 3.0. The Referee rejects a result file whose reads are not in `registry_reads.log`.
2. **Two statistical definitions are fixed in `PARAMETERS.md` before the first registration** (modes 3, 4). The hold-out pass criterion takes the Phase-6 form: one-sided, centred on haircut × OOS Sharpe, tolerance 1.28 standard errors of the difference, with its pass-probability table printed. The "derivative" rule extends to published evidence: the OOS excludes any window the cited evidence displays, or uses markets it did not touch; the candidate format gets an "evidence window" field.
3. **Dates and client items go on paper now** (modes 2, 5, 6, 7, 9). The critical path: Gate 5 by 28 Nov for any S1; P1 only after Gates 4 and 5 and the hold-out read; 6 Nov is the 72-hour Databento trigger, after which batch 1 is at least half free-data rows and CAL has a free daily-data fallback. The firm letters go out this week through one decision request, not "before any purchase". Day 1 runs the sweep and the letters only, inside 2 compute agents. The N1 memo template, with its "not tested: data not bought" and "weaker stated standard" tables, is written before any result exists.
