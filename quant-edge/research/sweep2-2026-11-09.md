## 1. Candidate blocks

### SW2-01 — Famous-firm post-close earnings-cluster eve: ES/NQ long 18:00 ET (T−1) → 15:55 ET (T)
- mechanism: on the few days when several high-attention firms report after the close, investors (retail and attention-driven
  institutions) buy the announcers and the index ahead of the event; dealers/market makers demand a premium to carry index
  inventory into a known, concentrated variance event (announcement-risk premium at the market level). The effect is earned
  before the release and is uncorrelated with the announcement outcome (no leakage), so it is a priced-risk/attention premium,
  not information. Persistence argument: the premium is a compensation for bearing scheduled jump risk; it is small in aggregate
  (12 days/yr) and requires holding beta into a jump, which deters arbitrage.
- evidence: Chen, Cohen, Wang, "Famous Firms, Earnings Clusters, and the Stock Market" (SSRN 3685452, WP 2020/2021). FETCHED the
  CXO Advisory summary https://www.cxoadvisory.com/calendar-effects/three-high-attention-earnings-announcement-clusters-drive-market/
  (2026-11-09): E-mini S&P 1999–2018, three most prominent PM clusters per earnings month (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct) = 12 days/yr;
  close-to-close +0.34% vs +0.02% typical day; split ≈ 0.11% overnight (evening before) + 0.23% day session; only PM clusters, not AM;
  prominence = prior-year Dow Jones news count; weaker but significant with count or market-cap definitions; "annualized Sharpe 4.5
  on these days" (per-day). FETCHED https://wallstreethorizon.com/news/famours-firms-earnings-clusters (abstract only: "17x the norm").
  Related, SNIPPET only: AQR "The Earnings Announcement Premium and Trading Volume" (premium strong in large caps); Ogneva–Xia–Ye
  (index futures move on large-firm releases; PDF 403).
- evidence window: 1999–2018 (in-sample); 2019–2024 is post-publication (6 y) inside our OOS window.
- rule sketch (all fixed ex ante): famous-firm set = the 10 largest S&P 500 members by market cap at the prior 31 Dec plus a frozen
  attention list {AAPL, AMZN, MSFT, GOOGL, META, NFLX, TSLA, NVDA, INTC, IBM} (union, ≤ 20 names). An after-close (AMC) release date is a
  cluster day if ≥ 3 set members report AMC that date. In each Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct take the up-to-3 cluster days with the most set members
  (ties → earlier date). Trade: long 1 ES (variant pre-registered as a second arm: NQ) at the 18:00 ET Globex open of the evening before
  (T−1), exit 15:55 ET on T (before the releases and before every firm deadline). No stop. Size at 1% daily vol target from 20-day ES
  realized vol at T−1.
- data in hand: PARTLY. ES/NQ 1h 2010-06..2026 (Databento) in hand; earnings dates/timing NOT in hand — free from SEC EDGAR 8-K item 2.02
  acceptance timestamps (after 16:00 ET = AMC) for ≤ 20 tickers (reachable, $0, labour ≈ 1 day; acceptance time is point-in-time).
- gross per trade vs cost: in-sample +34 bp close-to-close; haircut 0.5 → ≈ 17 bp vs ES RT 1.2 bp (14×); passes the screen easily.
- trades/yr: 12.
- realistic decayed S: per-trade Sharpe in-sample ≈ 0.34/1.2 ≈ 0.28 → annual 0.28·√12 ≈ 0.98 gross; × 0.5 haircut → ≈ 0.48 net
  (costs negligible). As a standalone sleeve it is mostly flat cash; S is per-year of the sleeve.
- OOS years: 10 (2015–2024); 6 post-publication (2019–2024).
- criterion-4 power: Φ(0.48·√10 − 3.6807) = 0.015 (6 y: 0.006). Even at the unhaircut S ≈ 0.98: Φ(3.10 − 3.68) = 0.28.
- honest prior: 0.15 (clear mechanism, futures-level evidence by the authors, cheap to implement; but 12 events/yr and the 2019–24
  era of mega-cap concentration could amplify or arbitrage it).
- closed-family check: none. Not H0023 (turn-of-month), not E2 (macro releases), not the bare OPEX/day-of-week rows; IG C-12-24
  (buyback dip buying) is stock-flow based and different. Not in candidates.md.
- firm-structure fit: overnight-session futures (18:00 → 15:55 is inside one trading day; flat before Topstep 16:10 ET) — IF/ONF;
  also own capital. FTMO intraday CFD cannot hold from 18:00 (crosses the CE(S)T day? 18:00 ET = 00:00 CET: daily-loss reset — fine, but
  the hold crosses the FTMO day end only if exit after 18:00 ET; exit 15:55 is fine) → also FI on US500/US100.
- power×prior: 0.015 × 0.15 = 0.002. Value is as a stackable sleeve, not as a standalone pass.
