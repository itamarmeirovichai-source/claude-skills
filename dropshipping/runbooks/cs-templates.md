# Customer-service templates (10 most common tickets; manual first, automation after month 2)

Tone: short, specific, one next step, no excuses. Every reply states the order number and the date of the next action. Disputes: reply the same day; PayPal buyers escalate after ~4 days of silence (S-118).

1. **Where is my order?** "Hi [name], order [#] shipped on [date] from our US warehouse; tracking: [link]. Estimated delivery [date]. If it has not arrived by [date + 3 business days], reply here and we will reship or refund."
2. **Tracking not updating.** "Carriers sometimes skip scans for 2–3 days. If no movement by [date], we reship at our cost or refund in full, your choice."
3. **Arrived damaged.** "Sorry about that. Please reply with a photo of the item and the box. We will send a replacement or a full refund within 1 business day of the photo; no need to return it."
4. **Wrong item.** Same as 3, plus: "Keep the item; we will ship the correct one today."
5. **Cancel before shipping.** "Done: order [#] is cancelled and refunded to your original payment method; it shows within 7 working days." (If already shipped: template 6.)
6. **Return request.** "Return address: [address]. Include the order number inside. Refund within 7 working days of receipt. Return shipping: [customer / prepaid label if our error]."
7. **Delay notice (we cannot ship within the stated time).** "Your order [#] will ship on [new date], later than promised. Reply CANCEL for an immediate full refund, or do nothing and we ship on [new date]." (FTC rule: consent or refund.)
8. **Discount request / price match.** "Our price includes shipping and is the same for everyone; we do not run timed sales. If a competitor is cheaper for the same product, we will understand you choosing them."
9. **PayPal / card dispute opened.** Internal: respond in the processor's center within 24 h with tracking showing delivery to the transaction address (S-118). Customer: "We see you opened a dispute. We can refund you directly today if you close it, or let the case run; either way you will not lose money."
10. **Product question before purchase.** Answer from the sample's measured facts only (size, materials, delivery days). "We tested it ourselves: [fact]." Never invent a spec.
