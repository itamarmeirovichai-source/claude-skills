## 0. What the reviews and the price scan changed

- **Price pressure (the main finding).** The mass market sells this lamp at $16–35: NBC's Oct 2026 roundup lists eight lamps at $15.99–29.99 (S-215); Walmart Mainstays lamps sit around 4.6★ at mass prices, DAYBETTER 4.6★/872 ratings, MEHOOM 4.5★/79, Fimilo 4.2★/380 (S-217); Grove's silver hobnail is $34.99 (S-172, 4.9★/9); Candle Warmers Etc. lamps $34.99 (S-216). Our $59.90 is 2–3× that. At $49.90 the calculator fails the screening floor (share 0.314, breakeven ROAS 3.18 > 3.0), so the price stays at $59.90 and the product must **earn** the gap: a design-led lamp (ceramic, marble or solid-wood base, fabric or milk-glass shade) **plus two spare bulbs in the box**, because bulb failure is the category's #1 complaint. With landed $24 (lamp + 2 bulbs) the row still passes: share 0.396, breakeven ROAS 2.53. Demand signal is the weakest of the four (one advertiser, Maria's Finds, 2 ads since Jun 29, 2026; two creator ads, S-184), which is why the prior drops to 7%.
- **34 review voices read (S-213..S-217).** Failures: the halogen bulb dies early ("the first one only lasted for 3 uses"; "bulb went out after 2 hours"; "first bulb blew the second it was plugged in"), units quit ("two out of three stopped working after one week"; "stopped working in a little over 30 days"), weak melt ("never truly melted the candle much"; "bulb doesn't get hot enough to melt the wax"), glass shade "shattered while the warmer was on the lowest setting", "the adjuster can loosen and feel unstable", "replacement lightbulbs aren't standard and may be tricky to source", "controls aren't immediately intuitive". Praise: "makes my candles last much, much longer without the soot"; "fills my home with subtle fragrance while the candle still looks brand new"; "adjust the brightness to control how fast the candle melts"; "all of the wax melted"; timer lets it switch off by itself.
- **Safety framing, from a named expert, not from us.** FDNY battalion commander Jack Oehm (NBC, S-215): warmers are "not as much of a risk as a lit candle that can be knocked down", but avoid "the edge of an unstable nightstand or where there is flammable material", never burn the candle while the warmer is on, and let it cool before moving. The page repeats these as **care instructions**, never as a "safe" claim (an absolute safety claim is unsubstantiable; S-94). Bulb reaches over 100 °C (S-215 guide); the page says "bulb gets hot; adjust height only when off".
- Electrical: a 120 V lamp with a GU10 halogen bulb is a listed product class; the SKU must carry a UL/ETL/cETLus mark on the base or cord label (same kill logic as P-0003). The ad never shows a child or pet near the lamp.
