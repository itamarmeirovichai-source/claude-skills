# Store policies, draft for a US-facing store (M0 step 6)

Written to satisfy: FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (S-34), FTC consumer reviews rule (S-36), FTC dark-patterns examples (S-117), Baymard abandonment reasons (S-139), PayPal dispute evidence (S-118). Placeholders in [brackets] are filled per product from the supplier's per-SKU "ships from" data (S-56). The mother, as account owner, approves the final text.

## Shipping policy
- We ship to the United States only.
- Orders are processed within [1–2] business days and ship from a warehouse in the United States. Delivery takes [3–5] business days after shipping. These are the carrier's estimates; we will email you tracking as soon as the order ships.
- If we cannot ship within 30 days of your order, we will email you with the new date and the option to cancel for a full refund (FTC rule). If we cannot ship at all, we cancel and refund without being asked.
- Shipping is included in the price shown on the product page. There are no additional fees at checkout.
- We do not ship to PO boxes or freight forwarders.

## Returns and refunds
- 30-day returns on unused items in original packaging. Email [support address] with your order number; we send a return address within 2 business days. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or wrong, in which case we send a prepaid label.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 working days of receiving the return or of a cancellation (FTC rule).
- Damaged or wrong item: send a photo within 7 days of delivery and we replace or refund, no return needed.

## Reviews
- Reviews on this site are from verified purchasers. We never pay for reviews or condition any reward on a positive review. We do not remove negative reviews that comply with our content rules (FTC reviews rule).

## Pricing and promotions
- The price shown is the price charged. Any "compare at" price refers to a price this store actually charged for at least [30] days (S-77, S-117). We do not use countdown timers, stock counters or viewer counters.
