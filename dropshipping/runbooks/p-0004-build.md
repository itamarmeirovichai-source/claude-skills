# P-0004 build package: 6-pack rechargeable motion-sensor closet lights at $49.90 (written 2026-11-11, $0 spent)

Second in the test order (after P-0002 resolves). Registry: `registry.md` P-0004. Stop rule: T1 $120 over 6 days at $20/day, continue only if purchases ≥ 6; T2 $120, pass if ≥ 2. Prior 12%. Kill condition: cells > 20 Wh or supplier without UN3481 handling → kill (S-46/S-47 lithium rules). **[M0]** marks what needs an adult account or a real SKU. סיכום בעברית בסוף.

## 0. What the reviews changed

- 50 real reviews read (S-206..S-208): Tanga 54-LED 2-pack (172 ratings, 4.0/5; 2-star, 3-star, 5-star pages), Tanga 6-pack 10-LED bars (352 ratings, 4.3/5; 1-star page and a mixed page), retailer snippets (Home Depot/Lowe's Brilliant Evolution 6-pack 4.7–4.8, Walmart LOFTER-PRO 4.2/859). Pattern: **the category's failures are not about light; they are about the mount, the battery and the sensor.** Fails named in order of frequency: adhesive pad lets go (lights fall; one reviewer: "falling lights can cause damage"), battery holds less and less charge after weeks, sensor triggers only in darkness or not at all, charging port loosens, units arrive switched on. Praise: brightness ("2 of these lights is enough brightness to properly and fully replace a 100W or so light bulb"), two-minute tool-free install, "comes on as soon as I open the door", 20-second auto-off "felt nearly perfect" (Bob Vila test, S-209).
- Market is mature and a long-running advertiser exists (Modolight, 245 days, 14 ads; NightLume 167 days, S-186). The 6-pack at $49.90 wins only if the **mount** and **battery** are visibly better than the $20–35 packs and the page says exactly what the sensor does (dark-only or always). The offer is "six lights, one charge a month, mounts that hold", not "closet light".
- Safety and policy: lithium cells installed in equipment (UN3481) ship by ground/air with the supplier's handling; the page states cell size in Wh (kill condition). No "fire-safe" or "child-safe" claims. Not marketed for children's rooms (CPSC children's-product exclusion, S-47) even though it is lawful; keeps the ad set clean.

## 2. Positioning (Schwartz, S-102)

- Awareness: **solution-aware** (they know stick-on sensor lights exist; many own a bad one). Sophistication: 4 (claims are worn: "bright", "easy"; the mechanism that beats the last one must be shown: the mount and the battery).
- One-line promise (true only if the SKU spec confirms it): *Six rechargeable sensor lights that stay where you put them and charge once a month.*
- Who it is for: homeowners and renters lighting closets, pantries, under-cabinet counters, stairs and hallways without an electrician. Not sold for nurseries or as a safety device.
- Price $49.90 for 6, shipping included ($8.32 per light). No compare-at. No "BOGO ends tonight" (that is NightLume's badge, S-186, and an FTC dark-pattern example, S-117).
