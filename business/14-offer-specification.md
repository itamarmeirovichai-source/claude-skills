## 1. Product evaluation

Eight product shapes were considered. Scored on buyer clarity, margin, delivery load, repeat
potential, and risk.

| Product | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Single property video pack** | ✅ **Launch** | Clearest unit of value; matches the buying trigger (new unit, pre-season); simplest to price and scope |
| **Paid trial (reduced scope)** | ✅ **Launch** | Replaces the free sample (D-11). Low commitment, still proves willingness to pay (A-01) |
| Creative variation bundle (same property, multiple angles) | ✅ **Launch, as the Standard tier's second concept** | Cheap to produce from one asset prep; genuinely useful for ad testing |
| **Multi-property / portfolio package** | ✅ **Launch** | Where the margin is (template reuse). But **priced only after per-unit delivery cost is measured** ([19](19-property-manager-sales.md)) |
| Seasonal packages (summer/winter re-cut) | ⏸️ **90 days** | Attractive and depends on A-05 repeat evidence. Do not build before it |
| Recurring content subscription | ❌ **Not at launch** | D-10. Forcing a subscription onto a possibly-annual need produces churn and refunds |
| Premium custom production | ⏸️ **90 days** | Needs a portfolio (S4/S5 gating in [08](08-segment-selection-matrix.md)) |
| Distribution service (we post for them) | ❌ **Not at launch** | Requires account access, ongoing availability (violates A-15), and creates responsibility for outcomes we cannot promise (D-05). **We provide a distribution guide, not a distribution service** ([35](35-customer-operations.md)) |
| **Arrival / guest walkthrough video** | ✅ **Launch, as an add-on** | E-02: Airbnb permits video in the post-booking message thread. Zero marketing-claim risk, genuinely useful, differentiated |

## 3. What is explicitly excluded, in all packages

- Property visits, photography, or re-shoots
- Posting, scheduling, or managing the customer's accounts
- Any guarantee of bookings, occupancy, enquiries, reach, or platform ranking (D-05)
- Drone or exterior footage not derivable from supplied photos
- Showing features, rooms, views, or amenities the property does not have
  ([26](26-property-accuracy-rules.md))
- Music selection from the customer's own library unless they supply a licence
- Airbnb branding of any kind (E-13)
- Booking.com marketing video — that platform's video upload is location verification only (E-09)

## 4. The revision policy, and the six categories it depends on

Most service businesses lose their margin here. The distinction below is the single most
important operational control in the business ([15](15-financial-model.md) shows why: time is
the dominant cost).

| # | Category | Definition | Who pays | Turnaround impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Our error** | We delivered something other than the agreed brief: wrong file spec, a typo we introduced, a caption that misstates a fact from the Fact Sheet, an accuracy defect | **Us.** Free, unlimited, prioritised | Fixed within 1 business day |
| 2 | **Included revision** | Changes *within* the agreed brief: swap a shot for another supplied photo, adjust caption wording, change music track, re-order two scenes, trim length | Included — 1 round | +1–2 business days |
| 3 | **New creative direction** | The brief was met; they now want a different approach: different narrative, different audience, different tone | **Customer.** Priced as an additional concept (+$89) | New slot in the queue |
| 4 | **Changed source material** | New or replacement photos supplied after production started | **Customer.** Quoted case-by-case; usually +$49–$89 | Restarts asset prep |
| 5 | **Changed brief** | Property facts, target guest, or destination channels changed after sign-off | **Customer.** Re-quote | Re-enters intake |
| 6 | **Additional deliverable** | A format not in the package | **Customer.** Per the add-on table | +1–2 business days |

**"One round" means one consolidated set of feedback**, submitted together, within 7 calendar
days of delivery. Feedback arriving in five separate messages over three days is still one
round, but we consolidate it and act once, and we say so at delivery. After 7 days with no
feedback the order closes and further changes are category 3 or 6.

### Scope change decision tree

Run this on every post-delivery request. It exists so the answer is not decided by how tired the
founder is.

```
Request received
│
├─ Does the deliverable differ from the written brief/spec?
│  └─ YES → Category 1. Fix free, fast, apologise once, don't grovel.
│
├─ Is it a factual/accuracy problem (something shown that isn't real)?
│  └─ YES → Category 1 AND trigger accuracy incident process (26 §Escalation).
│           Fix free. Log it. This is never billed, ever.
│
├─ Has the revision round already been used?
│  ├─ NO  → Is the request achievable using already-supplied assets,
│  │        within the agreed concept and the agreed length?
│  │        ├─ YES → Category 2. Included. Do it.
│  │        └─ NO  → go to the next branch (tell them which category and why)
│  └─ YES → Category 6 pricing (+$59 extra round) or the relevant category below
│
├─ Are they supplying new/different photos?
│  └─ YES → Category 4. Quote.
│
├─ Have the property facts, audience, or destinations changed?
│  └─ YES → Category 5. Re-quote, re-sign the Fact Sheet.
│
├─ Do they want a different creative approach to the same property?
│  └─ YES → Category 3. +$89.
│
└─ Do they want a format not in the package?
   └─ YES → Category 6. Add-on price.
```

**The sentence that makes this work** (and it must be said kindly, not defensively):

> "That's a bit different from what we scoped — it's a [new direction / new format] rather than
> a revision. It's $89 and adds two days. Or, if you'd rather stay in scope, I can [in-scope
> alternative]. Your call."

Offering the in-scope alternative is what stops this reading as a shakedown.

## 5. The paid trial, and why there is no free sample

**Decision D-11.** Free production is an unbounded cost against an unknown production cost
(A-03) with an unproven conversion return.

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| The trial | Starter package at $149. Same quality, same accuracy review, smaller scope |
| Eligibility | Meets Q1–Q7 ([08](08-segment-selection-matrix.md)). One per company |
| Cost ceiling | It is a profitable order, not a loss leader. If measured delivery cost makes $149 unprofitable, **raise the price, don't subsidise it** |
| If they don't like it | Refund position per [35](35-customer-operations.md) |
| Next step | If satisfied, Standard or Portfolio, no discount for having trialled |

**Proof substitute for prospects who need to see work first:** a public portfolio of
**illustrative videos made on rights-clear imagery of properties we do not represent**, labelled
on the page in plain language: *"Illustrative work. These are not client properties."*
Presenting illustrative work as client work is forbidden ([21](21-claim-register.md)).

### Conditional reversal

If, after 40 qualified conversations, order conversion is under 10% **and** lost-reason coding
([41](41-measurement-definitions.md)) shows "wouldn't spend money on unseen quality" as the top
cause, test a strictly bounded free micro-sample: **one 8-second clip, one property, one per
company, maximum 5 per month, hard-stopped.** Measure conversion of sampled vs non-sampled leads
before extending it.

## 7. Recommendation

**Launch with Standard as the single advertised offer.** Starter exists as a de-risking option
mentioned in conversation and on the pricing page, not advertised. Portfolio is quoted, never
listed.

Rationale: one advertised offer removes the choice-architecture problem entirely
([13](13-consumer-psychology-and-sales-method.md) §4 — where the evidence is weakest, prefer the
simpler structure on operational grounds). It also concentrates our measurement: with one price
and one scope, the day-30 conversion number means something. Three advertised prices would split
a small sample into three meaningless ones.

## 8. Acceptance criteria for this specification

The offer spec is working if, after 10 orders:
- [ ] Zero disputes about what was included (scope clarity)
- [ ] ≤40% of orders use their revision round (A-11)
- [ ] ≤10% of orders generate a category 3/4/5 dispute
- [ ] 100% of delivered orders shipped every file listed in the package
- [ ] Median delivery is **on or before** the promised day
- [ ] Zero accuracy incidents (category 1 accuracy fixes)
