# 21 — Claim Register

**Authoritative source for what we may and may not say in public.** Every public claim — website,
ad, social post, proposal, email, DM — must appear on the permitted list, or it does not go out.

## How to read this

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | The exact assertion, as a customer would read it |
| **Status** | ✅ permitted · ⏳ permitted after a named verification · ❌ forbidden |
| **Backing** | The evidence or fact that supports it |
| **Who can verify** | How a skeptical customer could check it themselves |

**Rule: if a customer could not verify it and we could not produce evidence on demand, it is not
a claim — it is an assertion, and it does not ship.**

## ⏳ Permitted only after a named verification

These are true as far as our evidence goes, but the primary source was **blocked by the egress
proxy** during research (see [02](02-evidence-ledger.md) §Research limitations). **They must not
be published until the primary source has been read directly.** Verification tasks are in
[42](42-validation-plan.md) V-0.

| Claim | Backing | Blocked on |
|---|---|---|
| "A music-free cut that meets Vrbo's published video guidelines" | E-03–E-07 (grade B) | Reading help.vrbo.com directly |
| "Vrbo rejects added music and on-screen contact details" | E-04, E-05 (grade B) | Same |
| "Airbnb can require hosts to remove listing media altered to add features the property doesn't have" | E-11 (grade B) | Reading Airbnb Ground Rules / Content Policy directly |
| "Sized to Google Business Profile's requirements — under 30 seconds, under 75MB" | E-08 (grade B) | Reading Google's support page directly |
| "Licensed music, cleared for client use" | E-18 (grade B) | Reading the full Epidemic Sound licence |
| "Booking.com's partner video upload is for location verification, not marketing" | E-09 (grade B) | Reading Booking.com partner docs directly |

**Until verified, these may be used in conversation with "as I understand it" framing, and must
not appear in published marketing copy.** There is a real difference between telling a customer
what you believe and printing it on a website.

## ❌ Forbidden

### Outcome claims

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| "Increases bookings" / "more bookings" / "boost your bookings" | E-20 — no causal evidence exists; D-05 |
| "Increases occupancy" / "raises your ADR" / "more revenue" | Same |
| "Improves your ranking on [any platform]" | No evidence; also unverifiable |
| "Listings with video get X% more views" | **Even if a vendor published this, it is D-grade marketing about their own product.** Citing it is laundering someone else's unsubstantiated claim |
| "Guests are Y times more likely to book" | Same |
| "Pays for itself in one booking" | An ROI claim dressed as arithmetic. Requires the causal premise we do not have |
| **Any implication by juxtaposition** — e.g. a video next to a booking screenshot | The implication is the claim. This is the most likely way we would break D-05 by accident |

### Social proof claims

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| "Trusted by hundreds of hosts" | We have none |
| Any testimonial we did not receive, with permission, from a real customer | Fabrication |
| Any case study we did not run | Fabrication |
| Stock photos presented as clients | Fabrication |
| Follower, view or client counts we have not met | Fabrication |
| Presenting illustrative work as client work | Fabrication. **Illustrative work must be labelled** ([14](14-offer-specification.md) §5) |

### Comparison claims

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| "Better than a videographer" | False in the cases that matter — they capture real footage of the real space ([11](11-competitor-analysis.md)) |
| "Save $1,200 vs a film crew" | Misleading comparison against a product we don't sell and a price they'd never have paid |
| "Was $599, now $279" | Fake reference pricing. We have never charged $599 |
| Naming a competitor and asserting their limitations | We have no verified knowledge of their delivery ([11](11-competitor-analysis.md) V-1) |

### Brand and platform claims

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| Any use of the Airbnb logo, Bélo, Superhost badge, or AirCover mark | E-13 — written permission required |
| "Airbnb-approved" / "Official Airbnb partner" / anything implying endorsement | E-13; false |
| "Airbnb-optimised video" | Implies a placement that does not exist (E-01) |
| Vrbo, Booking.com, Google or Meta logos implying endorsement | Same principle |
| "Compliant with Airbnb's rules" as a guarantee | We can describe our accuracy process; we cannot guarantee another company's enforcement decision |

### Capability claims

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| "Indistinguishable from a real shoot" | Not true, and unprovable |
| "Unlimited revisions" | Contradicts [14](14-offer-specification.md) |
| "Same-day turnaround" | Contradicts A-15 and [37](37-capacity-and-contractors.md) |
| "24/7 support" | One person who sleeps |
| "Our team" / "our studio" / "our editors" | One person (D-13) |

### Urgency and scarcity

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| Countdown timers, "limited spots" that reset, "price rising soon" unless it is | Fake scarcity ([13](13-consumer-psychology-and-sales-method.md)) |
| "Only 3 slots left this month" unless the capacity board says so **and we actually decline work at that number** | A true statement we fail to honour becomes a false one |

## The disclosure statements we always make

These appear in the proposal and on the pricing page. They are not fine print — they are part of
the offer.

1. **"Airbnb does not accept host-uploaded video on listing galleries. None of these files are
   for that placement."**
2. **"We make no claims about bookings, occupancy or revenue. There is no credible evidence that
   property video causes bookings, and we won't pretend otherwise."**
3. **"These videos are made with AI assistance and reviewed by a person before delivery."**
4. **"We don't show rooms, views, amenities or features a property doesn't have. If your photos
   and your fact sheet disagree, we'll stop and ask you."**
5. **"Illustrative examples on this site are not client properties."** *(portfolio page)*

## Change control

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| Adding a claim | Requires an entry in [02](02-evidence-ledger.md) with a grade of B or better, **and** a way for a customer to verify it |
| Moving a claim from ⏳ to ✅ | Requires the named primary source to have been read and the E-entry upgraded to grade A |
| Any claim about our own results | Requires ≥5 delivered orders and the customer's written permission |
| **Any outcome claim** | Requires a controlled test we ran ourselves. Assume this never happens (D-05 reversal note) |
| Review cadence | At every go/no-go gate ([43](43-launch-plan.md)) |

## Enforcement

Any published claim not on this register is a **defect**, handled like a production defect:
removed immediately, logged, and the process that let it through is reviewed. An agent drafting
copy has no authority to add a claim ([39](39-data-and-agent-architecture.md)); an agent that
generates an unlisted claim is a test failure in [40](40-testing-plan.md), whether or not it was
published.
