# 20 — Brand Voice & Copy

Every promise here must match [14](14-offer-specification.md) and [15](15-financial-model.md),
and every claim must be permitted by [21](21-claim-register.md).

## 1. Voice

**We sound like a competent person explaining their work, who would rather lose a sale than
overstate one.**

### Rules

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use specific numbers: "seven files", "three to five business days" | "Fast turnaround", "a full suite of assets" |
| Name the limitation before they ask | Bury it in terms |
| Short sentences. Ordinary words | "Leverage", "unlock", "elevate", "transform", "seamless", "game-changing" |
| Say "I" — there is one person here | "Our team", "we're excited to", "the [Brand] family" |
| Concede the honest point | Rebut everything |
| One idea per sentence | Stacked clauses joined by dashes |
| Plain punctuation | Em-dash pileups, exclamation marks, ellipses for effect |
| Contractions where natural | Formal-register stiffness |

### Banned phrases

"Revolutionise your listing" · "In today's competitive market" · "Unlock the power of" ·
"Take your rentals to the next level" · "Studies show" (without a study) · "Most hosts don't
realise" · "Here's the secret" · "Let that sink in" · "Delve" · "Elevate" · "Seamlessly" ·
"Game-changer" · "It's a no-brainer" · any sentence that starts "Imagine if..."

### Two examples of the voice working

| ❌ Generic AI marketing copy | ✅ Our voice |
|---|---|
| "Transform your property listings with stunning AI-powered video that captivates travelers and drives bookings!" | "Seven short videos made from the photos you already have. Three to five days. I can't tell you it gets you bookings — nobody can." |
| "We leverage cutting-edge technology to deliver industry-leading results for discerning hospitality professionals." | "I work from your photos, so there's no shoot to schedule around guests. The trade-off is I can't capture anything your photos didn't." |

**The test:** would a skeptical property manager with fourteen units and no patience read this
and think "this person knows what they're doing" or "this person is selling me something"?

## 5. Calls to action

| Context | CTA | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Site hero | **See what you'd get** | Low commitment; the objection at this stage is "what is this" |
| Pricing page | **Start an order** | Commitment language matches intent |
| Ad | **See examples** | Cold traffic; asking for an order is premature |
| Organic post | **Link in bio if you want one** | Deliberately flat. Enthusiasm reads as an ad |
| Proposal | **Pay and send your photos** | Two actions, both required, stated together |
| Declined lead | **Come back when you've got somewhere to post it** | Genuine, not a hook |

Never: "Book your free strategy call!", "Limited spots!", "Don't miss out!", countdown timers.

## 6. Message library

### Inquiry response (qualified)

> Thanks — [specific detail from their form] is a good setup for this.
>
> One question before I suggest anything: [the single most useful diagnostic for their
> situation]?
>
> [Name]

**Never:** open with a pitch. **Never:** ask four questions.

### Inquiry response (needs qualification)

> Thanks for this. Before anything else — where were you thinking of putting the video?
>
> Asking because Airbnb doesn't let hosts upload video to the listing gallery, so if that's the
> plan I'd rather tell you now than sell you something with nowhere to go.
>
> [Name]

### Decline (no publishing channel)

> Thanks for getting in touch — I'm going to say no, and here's why.
>
> Airbnb doesn't accept host-uploaded video on listing galleries. Photos only. So a video
> wouldn't have anywhere to live on your listing, and I'm not going to sell you a file with
> nowhere to put it.
>
> Video does work for rentals — on Instagram, your own booking site, a Vrbo listing, or a Google
> Business Profile. If you want to build one of those, start with Instagram and post photos for
> a month. If it feels worth continuing, come back and I'll make you something to post.
>
> Here's a short guide on setting that up. No charge and I won't follow up.
>
> [Name]

### Proposal covering message

> Here's the scope on one page — [property name], seven files, $279, delivered by [date] once
> your photos land.
>
> Two things I've put in writing because they matter: what's excluded, and the fact that I make
> no claims about bookings. If either is a problem, better to know now.
>
> Payment link is at the bottom. Send photos whenever — the clock starts when they arrive, not
> when you pay.

### Checkout explanation

> **What happens after you pay**
> 1. You'll get an upload link and an eight-question form about the property. About twenty
>    minutes.
> 2. Your delivery date is counted from when both arrive, not from today.
> 3. I'll confirm the date once they're in.
> 4. You get seven files plus a one-page guide on where each one goes.
> 5. One revision round, within seven days.
>
> Nothing else is charged. If something you want turns out to be outside this scope, I'll tell
> you the price before doing it, not after.

### Onboarding message

> Payment's through, thanks. Two links:
>
> **Upload your photos:** [link] — 20 or more, 1500px or bigger.
> **Property Fact Sheet:** [link] — eight questions, about five minutes. This is what I check the
> video against, so it matters more than it looks.
>
> As soon as both are in I'll confirm your delivery date.

### Progress update (only when there is something to say)

> Quick update — your photos are in and I've got the first cut of both concepts. Accuracy review
> tomorrow, then exports. Still on track for [date].
>
> One thing: [photo #] shows [detail] and your fact sheet doesn't mention it. Can you confirm
> whether it's currently there? I'd rather ask than guess.

**Rule: no update that says nothing.** "Just checking in to say we're working on it" is noise.

### Delivery message (the most important message in the business)

Per [13](13-consumer-psychology-and-sales-method.md) §10, this is the end of the experience and
weighs disproportionately in memory. It gets more care than anything else we write.

> Your videos are ready — [link], all seven files.
>
> **Where each one goes**
> - `01-reels-hook-a.mp4` and `02-reels-hook-b.mp4` — Instagram Reels and TikTok. Two different
>   openings; post one, and if it does nothing, post the other next week.
> - `03-feed-square.mp4` — Instagram feed, Facebook.
> - `04-web-hero.mp4` — your booking site, above the fold.
> - `05-web-loop-silent.mp4` — the header loop. No sound, made to autoplay.
> - `06-vrbo-cut.mp4` — your Vrbo listing. No music and no text overlay on purpose: Vrbo rejects
>   added music and on-screen contact details.
> - `07-google-profile.mp4` — Google Business Profile. Under 30 seconds and under 75MB, which is
>   what they want.
> - `captions.srt` — if you'd rather use platform captions than the burned-in ones.
>
> **If I'd post one thing first:** `01-reels-hook-a.mp4`. It opens on [specific detail], which is
> the thing that makes this property different from the other six in your area.
>
> Revisions: one round, within seven days, all in one message if you can.
>
> Files stay up for 30 days, then I archive them. Download them now if you can.

### Revision acknowledgement

> Got it. Working through these now — [restate what they asked, in one line, so they know it
> landed correctly]. Back to you by [date].
>
> [If any item is out of scope:] One of these — [item] — is a bit different from what we scoped.
> It's [category] rather than a revision, which is $[X]. The other three are in scope and I'm
> doing them now regardless. Say the word on the fourth.

### Follow-up (once, then stop)

> Following up once on [property] — no pressure and I won't chase again.
>
> If the timing's wrong, tell me roughly when and I'll make a note rather than emailing you.
>
> [Name]

### Referral request (after value delivered, never incentivised)

> Glad they worked.
>
> Two things, both optional and neither changes anything about your order:
>
> If you'd write one sentence I could put on the site, I'd appreciate it — with your name or
> without, whichever you prefer.
>
> And if you know another operator who'd find this useful, I'd rather hear from them than run
> ads. No referral fee, because I don't want you recommending me for a discount.

**Why no incentive:** an incentivised referral or review changes what the reviewer says and what
the recipient hears. [36](36-retention-plan.md) — we do not incentivise reviews.

## 7. Copy review checklist

Before anything is published, check every item:

- [ ] Does every promise appear in [14](14-offer-specification.md)?
- [ ] Is every price the price in [15](15-financial-model.md)?
- [ ] Is every claim on the permitted list in [21](21-claim-register.md)?
- [ ] Any implication that video causes bookings, even by juxtaposition? **Remove it.**
- [ ] Any Airbnb logo, Bélo, Superhost badge, or implied endorsement? **Remove it** (E-13)
- [ ] Any illustrative work presented as client work? **Label it**
- [ ] Any number without a source?
- [ ] Any banned phrase from §1?
- [ ] Any urgency or scarcity that is not literally true?
- [ ] Does it say "our team" when there is one person?
- [ ] Is the limitation stated before the benefit, or at least on the same page?
- [ ] Read aloud: does it sound like a person, or like a landing page?
