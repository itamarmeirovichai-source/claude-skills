# 34 — Website & Conversion Path

## 1. Conversion route — the choice

| Route | Friction | Founder time | Fits our situation? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct inquiry (email link) | Low | High — every lead is unstructured | ❌ Produces a round of basic questions before anything useful |
| **Short qualification form** | Low–medium | **Low** | ✅ **Chosen** |
| Conversation / chat | Very low | **Very high** — and A-15 means nobody is there for 8–10h | ❌ |
| Paid trial as the entry | Medium | Low | ⏸️ Offered, not the primary route |
| Proposal request | High | Medium | ⏸️ For PM/portfolio only ([19](19-property-manager-sales.md)) |
| Instant checkout | **Lowest** | Low | ❌ **Rejected — see below** |

### Why not instant checkout

It is the obvious choice for a productised service and it is wrong here, because **we must
disqualify people before they pay.** Q1–Q7 ([08](08-segment-selection-matrix.md)) exist because
an S1 host with 8 low-resolution photos and no publishing channel generates a 5-hour job and a
refund ([15](15-financial-model.md) §10). A checkout button sells to them, takes their money, and
then we have to give it back having done the work.

The form costs conversion. It buys a qualified order book. At our margins that is the correct
trade — and it will be revisited if qualified-lead-to-order conversion turns out to be high
enough that the friction is unnecessary.

**Chosen route: short qualification form**, with the paid trial available as a low-commitment
option in conversation.

### Pricing page

The table from [14](14-offer-specification.md) §6, plus:
- Revision policy, stated **above** the CTA, not below it.
- Add-on prices, all of them. **Every charge visible before payment.**
- Market context, factually: "A local videographer is typically $150–$500 for a basic walkthrough,
  more with travel" (E-31). Honest anchoring ([13](13-consumer-psychology-and-sales-method.md)
  §5) — never a fake "was/now."
- The non-claim: "We make no claims about bookings, occupancy or revenue."

### Examples page

- 6–8 illustrative videos across property types, shown in their actual delivery formats.
- **Every video labelled as illustrative**, not as client work ([21](21-claim-register.md)).
- One example shown as the full seven-file set, so the deliverable is concrete.
- **Include one "why this shot was rejected" example.** Showing the rejects is more persuasive
  than showing only the wins, and nobody else does it.

### Accuracy checklist page

The Gate 1 checklist from [26](26-property-accuracy-rules.md), published in full. This is a
reason to believe (RTB-4) and the landing page for AD-3 ([30](30-advertising-packages.md)).

### Property managers page

For AD-4 traffic. Pilot structure, portfolio deliverables, the "no volume pricing before a
measured pilot" position, proposal request rather than the standard form
([19](19-property-manager-sales.md)).

## 3. FAQ — the real questions

Answers are short and match [17](17-objection-library.md).

1. **Can I put this on my Airbnb listing?** No. Airbnb doesn't accept host-uploaded video on
   listing galleries — it's photos only. These files are for Instagram, your booking site, Vrbo,
   Google Business Profile and email.
2. **Will this get me more bookings?** We don't know and we won't claim it. There's no credible
   evidence that property video causes bookings. What we commit to is the files, the formats, the
   accuracy review and the date.
3. **What do I actually send you?** 20+ photos at 1500px or more, an eight-question fact sheet,
   and confirmation the photos are yours to use. About 20 minutes.
4. **How is this different from doing it myself with an AI tool?** If you've got two hours and
   enjoy editing, do it yourself. You're paying for the time, the choice of which photos and in
   what order, the accuracy check, and seven finished files instead of one you re-crop.
5. **What if I don't like it?** One revision round is included. If we missed the spec or made a
   factual error, we fix it free and fast, and refund if we can't.
6. **How long does it take?** Three to five business days from when your photos and fact sheet
   arrive — not from when you pay.
7. **Can you show something my property doesn't have?** No. Platforms can require hosts to remove
   listing media altered to add features that aren't there, and it's not worth it to you.
8. **Do you use AI?** Yes, with human review. Motion is generated from your photographs; nothing
   about the property is invented.
9. **Who owns the videos?** You do, for marketing the property in the order, in perpetuity,
   worldwide.
10. **Do you post them for me?** No. You get files and a one-page guide on where each goes.

## 4. The form

Fields per [16](16-sales-playbook.md) §4. Design rules:

- **One screen on a phone.** No multi-step wizard.
- **9 fields maximum.** Every additional field costs completions.
- The publishing-channel question is **required** — it is the one that matters.
- Email is the only contact field required. **No phone number** — it lowers completion and we do
  not cold-call.
- Confirmation page states a real response time: "I'll reply within 4 business hours. One
  person, so that's a real number, not a chatbot."
- **No countdown timers, no fake scarcity, no "limited spots."**

### Confirmation page

> **Got it.**
>
> I'll read this properly and reply within 4 business hours — usually sooner.
>
> If it turns out I'm not the right fit, I'll say so and tell you what I'd do instead. That
> happens fairly often and it saves us both time.
>
> **While you wait:** [the accuracy checklist] is what every video gets checked against before
> delivery. It's the most useful thing on this site.

## 5. Message consistency

The same words must survive from ad to conversation. A mismatch at any hop is a conversion leak
that looks like a creative problem ([31](31-experiment-design.md) §Diagnosing failure).

| Ad | Landing page | First reply |
|---|---|---|
| AD-1 "Your photos already contain a video" | Home — same H1 | "You've got photos sitting in a folder" |
| AD-2 "Airbnb won't let you post video" | **A page that leads with that fact**, not the generic home page | "Where were you thinking of putting it?" |
| AD-3 "A property video that shows the property that exists" | **Accuracy checklist page** | "The checklist is what every video gets checked against" |
| AD-4 "Show owners the marketing you'd do" | **Property managers page** | "Who'd be approving this?" |
| AD-5 "Nothing to schedule around guests" | Home | "Are your units booked solid?" |
| AD-6 "I won't promise you bookings" | Pricing page, where the non-claim is stated | "I won't, and here's what I will promise" |

**AD-2 and AD-3 need their own landing pages.** Sending them to the generic home page is the
single most likely cause of a false negative in AD-T1 — we would conclude the message failed when
the page failed.

## 6. Technical requirements

| Requirement | Target |
|---|---|
| Mobile-first | ICP checks email on a phone between turnovers |
| Load time | **Under 2s on 4G.** Hero video lazy-loaded, poster frame first |
| Hero video | Muted, autoplay, `playsinline`, under 3MB, with a static fallback |
| Accessibility | Captions on every video; contrast ≥4.5:1; keyboard-navigable form; labelled inputs; alt text |
| Form | Works without JavaScript, or degrades to mailto |
| Analytics | Minimal and privacy-respecting. UTM capture into a hidden form field |
| Friction to remove | No popups, no chat widget, no cookie wall beyond what is required, no "download our guide" gate before the price |
| Price visibility | **Prices on the page.** "Contact for pricing" is friction disguised as positioning |

## 7. What is never on this site

- Illustrative work presented as client work
- Testimonials, logos, client counts, or case studies we do not have
- Any booking, occupancy, revenue or ranking claim (D-05)
- Airbnb, Vrbo, Google or Meta branding (E-13)
- Countdown timers, fake scarcity, fake reference pricing
- "Our team", "our studio", "our editors" (D-13 — one person)
- Stock photos of a fictional office or a fictional team

## 8. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every claim checked against [21](21-claim-register.md)
- [ ] Load under 2s on a throttled mobile connection, measured
- [ ] Form completes on a phone in under 90 seconds, timed by someone who is not the founder
- [ ] Illustrative work labelled on every instance
- [ ] Prices visible without contact
- [ ] Airbnb-video fact appears above the fold
- [ ] AD-2 and AD-3 have dedicated landing pages before AD-T1 runs
