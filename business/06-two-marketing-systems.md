# 06 — The Two Marketing Systems

The single most common way this business fails intellectually is collapsing two different
marketing problems into one. They share a medium and nothing else.

| | **System A — selling our service** | **System B — the video selling a property** |
|---|---|---|
| **Buyer** | Operator of vacation rental units (3–25) | Nobody buys. The traveler books a *stay*, not the video |
| **Viewer** | Owner, property manager, marketing lead | Traveler planning a trip |
| **Decision maker** | Owner/principal; in a PM, often a marketing or ops lead with sign-off under ~$500 | Traveler, usually with a partner or group influencing |
| **Problem being solved** | "I have a direct booking site and social accounts and nothing good to put on them, and no time" | "I need to decide whether this specific place fits my trip, and I can't tell from photos" |
| **Offer** | A pack of finished, publishable video files | A clearer view of the property than a photo grid gives |
| **Distribution** | Instagram/TikTok organic, Meta paid test, partnerships, search content | Their site, their social, their Vrbo listing, their email, their GBP |
| **Desired action** | Submit qualification form → pay → send assets | Stay watching → click to the listing/site → check dates |
| **Primary measure** | Cost per qualified lead; paid orders; contribution margin | Watch-through and click-through **on the customer's own channel** |
| **Hard constraint** | We may claim nothing about bookings (E-20, [21](21-claim-register.md)) | The property shown must be the property that exists (E-11, [26](26-property-accuracy-rules.md)) |

## The two confusions to refuse

**Confusion 1: traveler engagement is not demand for our service.**
A property video getting 40,000 views on TikTok tells us travelers enjoy property content. It
tells us nothing about whether an operator will pay $279 for one. These are different markets
with different buyers. Our own content performing well is System A evidence; a *client's* video
performing well is System B evidence. They must never be reported in the same number.

**Confusion 2: a watched video is not a caused booking.**
A booking that follows a video is not a booking caused by a video. Seasonality, pricing changes,
review accumulation, OTA ranking changes, and the operator's own other marketing all move at
once. E-20: no credible causal evidence exists in public. We will not manufacture it by
correlation. See [22 — Customer Success](35-customer-operations.md) and
[41](41-measurement-definitions.md) for how we report outcomes honestly.

## Where video can *actually* be used — the channel reality map

This table is the operational heart of System B. It is why the offer is a *pack* of files rather
than a file.

| Surface | Accepts marketing video? | Spec that binds us | Evidence | Our deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Airbnb listing gallery** | ❌ **No.** No MP4, no embed. Native video exists only for Luxe and some API-connected professional accounts | — | E-01 | **None.** We say so plainly on the website |
| **Airbnb guest message thread** | ✅ After booking confirmed | Practical/arrival content, not marketing | E-02 | Optional arrival-walkthrough add-on |
| **Vrbo listing** | ✅ **Yes** | 15s min / 60s preferred / 2min max; mp4 or mov; **no added music**; **no on-screen contact details**; ≤~20% area footage; rights to all audio | E-03–E-07 | **"Vrbo cut"** — 45–60s, music-free, caption-led, no CTA overlay |
| **Booking.com** | ❌ Not for marketing. Their video upload is **location verification** — must be <8 weeks old, unedited, showing street sign → exterior → path → interior | — | E-09 | **None.** Do not sell it. If a customer asks, tell them their verification video must be real footage, not ours |
| **Instagram Reels / TikTok** | ✅ | 9:16, sound-off-legible, captions | E-33 | 2× 9:16 social cuts, 15–30s, burned-in captions |
| **Instagram feed / Facebook** | ✅ | 1:1 or 4:5 | — | 1× 1:1 cut |
| **Direct booking website hero** | ✅ | 16:9, muted autoplay, short loop | — | 1× 16:9 cut + 1× silent 8–12s loop |
| **Google Business Profile** | ✅ Where the property qualifies for a profile | ≤30s recommended, 720p/1080p, <75MB | E-08 | 1× 30s, ≤75MB export |
| **Email to past guests** | ✅ | Most clients block video playback — needs a still + link | — | Poster frame + hosted link |
| **Meta/Google ads for direct bookings** | ✅ | Platform ad policies apply to the *customer* | — | 9:16 and 1:1 cuts are reusable |

### What this map decided

1. **The deliverable is a pack, not a file.** Seven files in the Standard package exists because
   there are seven genuinely different destinations with incompatible specs — not to inflate
   perceived value.
2. **The music-free cut is a real differentiator** (D-06). A competitor shipping one cinematic
   file with a music bed has produced something that is ineligible for Vrbo (E-04). We ship a
   compliant version by default and say why.
3. **We must never imply Booking.com or Airbnb gallery placement.** This is the most likely
   accidental false claim in the whole business, because every competitor SEO page implies it.

## System A definition (selling our service)

**Buyer:** see [08](08-segment-selection-matrix.md).
**Problem:** stated in the buyer's words, from [07](07-customer-research.md) — "I know I should
be posting, I don't have anything to post, and shooting it myself takes a whole day I don't
have."
**Offer:** [14](14-offer-specification.md).
**Channels:** [28](28-channel-strategy.md) — organic short-form primary, bounded paid test,
partnerships, search content.
**Desired action:** a single qualification form submission. Not a call booking — that adds
friction at an unproven top of funnel and consumes founder hours we need for production.
**Measurement:** [41](41-measurement-definitions.md). Primary: cost per **qualified** lead and
qualified-lead-to-paid-order rate. Not impressions, not followers.
**Constraints:**
- No booking-lift claims (D-05).
- No Airbnb branding (E-13, D-13).
- Founder may be unavailable 8–10h (A-15) — so no channel whose economics depend on sub-hour
  response, and no live-chat promise.
- Total pre-revenue spend ceiling: see [15](15-financial-model.md).

## System B definition (the property video)

**Buyer:** none — this is a communication artifact, not a product sold to travelers.
**Viewer:** traveler, mid-funnel, comparing 3–8 shortlisted properties.
**Problem:** photos answer "is it attractive"; they answer poorly "does it fit my trip" — layout,
scale, light, flow between rooms, distance to things, suitability for a group of six.
**Offer to the viewer:** 20–45 seconds that resolves one specific doubt.
**Distribution:** whichever surfaces above the customer actually operates — established at
intake, not assumed.
**Desired action:** continue watching, then click through to dates.
**Measurement:** watch-through and click-through on the *customer's* channel, reported as
observation with explicit attribution caveats ([41](41-measurement-definitions.md)).
**Constraints:** [26](26-property-accuracy-rules.md) governs absolutely. Vrbo cut carries E-03–
E-07. Nothing implied that the property cannot deliver.
