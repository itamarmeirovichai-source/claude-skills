# 08 — Segment Selection Matrix

**Authoritative source for the ICP definition and disqualification rules.**

## Scoring method

Nine criteria, each scored 1–5, weighted by how much it moves first-year survival for a solo
operator with limited cash. Weights are judgment, stated openly so they can be argued with.

| Criterion | Weight | Why this weight |
|---|---|---|
| Has somewhere to publish the deliverable | **3.0** | E-01 makes this existential. A buyer with no channel cannot use what we sell |
| Contribution margin per order | 2.5 | Solo operator; a low-margin order consumes the scarcest resource |
| Delivery difficulty (inverted: 5 = easy) | 2.5 | Time is the binding constraint (A-10), not cash |
| Decision speed / approval simplicity | 2.0 | Cash cycle length determines survival |
| Budget ownership clarity | 2.0 | Distinguishes a business expense from personal discretionary spend |
| Repeat potential | 1.5 | Real but unproven (A-05), so weighted below present-tense factors |
| Asset availability | 1.5 | Directly drives production cost and refusal rate (A-08) |
| Acquisition difficulty (inverted) | 1.5 | Matters, but a reachable non-buyer is worthless |
| Urgency / trigger strength | 1.0 | Nice, not decisive |

### Reading the matrix

S1 scores worst despite being the largest and cheapest-to-reach population (E-27: 5M+ hosts).
That is the whole point of weighting the channel criterion at 3.0. **A million reachable people
with nowhere to put your product is not a market.** Any future session tempted by S1's size
should re-read E-01 first.

S3 and S2 are close enough that they are treated as **one combined ICP with two sub-motions**
rather than a choice: same product, same accuracy standard, different sales process
([16](16-sales-playbook.md) for S2, [19](19-property-manager-sales.md) for S3).

S4 and S5 score respectably on value and poorly on feasibility-now. They are **90-day
candidates**, gated on having 15 delivered jobs and a real portfolio (D-03 reversal conditions).

## Recommended initial ICP

> **A US-based vacation rental operator running 3 to 25 units, who already has a direct booking
> website and at least one active social account, holds professional or semi-professional
> photography for most units, and is within ten weeks of a high season or is onboarding new
> units.**

### Qualification criteria (all must be true)

| # | Criterion | How it is verified | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Operates 3–25 units | Intake form; quick site check | Below 3, margin and repeat collapse; above 25, sales cycle exceeds our cash runway |
| Q2 | Has a direct booking site **or** an active social account with ≥1 post in 30 days | URL provided on the form | E-01 — they must have somewhere to publish |
| Q3 | Has ≥15 photos per property at ≥1500px on the long edge | Sample requested at intake | A-08; below this, production cost and refusal risk spike |
| Q4 | Confirms they hold or control the rights to those photos | Explicit checkbox + free-text on the intake form | A-08 legal exposure ([38](38-rights-and-security.md)) |
| Q5 | Decision-maker is in the conversation, or is one approval step away | Asked directly, early | A-06 |
| Q6 | Accepts a 3–5 business day turnaround, or pays the rush fee | Stated in the offer before payment | A-15 |
| Q7 | Accepts that we make no booking-performance claims | Stated plainly in the proposal | D-05 |

### Who we decline, and why we say so out loud

Declining well is a sales asset. Each of these is a real refusal with a named reason, not a
soft-close.

| We decline | Reason | What we say instead |
|---|---|---|
| Single-unit Airbnb-only host with no site and no social | Nowhere to publish (E-01) | "Airbnb won't let you put video on your listing. Until you've got a direct site or a social account running, this won't earn its money. Here's a free guide to setting one up — come back after." |
| Anyone asking us to show a feature the property does not have | [26](26-property-accuracy-rules.md), E-11 | "I can't do that one. Airbnb can pull listing media that adds amenities that aren't there, and it can reach payouts. I'd rather lose the job." |
| Fewer than 15 usable photos, or under 1500px | A-08, A-03 | "With what you've got I'd be inventing rooms rather than showing them. Get a photographer for a half-day first — I'd rather you spend the money there." |
| Rights to photos unclear or held by a photographer | A-08, [38](38-rights-and-security.md) | "Ask your photographer for written permission to make derivative video. Many licences stop at listing use. Send that and we're good to go." |
| Requesting a booking or occupancy guarantee | D-05 | "Nobody can honestly promise that, and I'd be lying if I did. What I can promise is the files, the formats, and the deadline." |
| First-order deadline under 48 hours | A-15, [37](37-capacity-and-contractors.md) | "Not on a first order. I don't know your property yet and rushing is how accuracy mistakes happen. Earliest is [date]." |
| Free sample requests | D-11 | "No free production. The trial package is $149 and it's the same quality — if it's not right, [refund policy, 35](35-customer-operations.md)." |
| Boutique hotels and luxury operators (for now) | Feasibility, portfolio gap | "You'd want to see work in your tier, and I don't have it yet. Ask me in three months — I'll tell you honestly if I do." |

## Sizing — and the honest caveat

E-26 puts US active STR listings at ~1.6M (2025) rising to ~1.77M (2026). E-35 gives VRMA member
company counts by state (CA: 432 companies; NC: 192) with wildly different average portfolio
sizes (NC ~141 units/company among disclosers; CA ~49).

**This does not size our market.** It sizes the population. Our addressable market is
"operators in that population who will pay $279 for a video pack," and that number is **unknown
and possibly near zero** (A-01). Nothing in this document should be used to argue that the
opportunity is large. The matrix answers *which* customer to approach first; it does not answer
*whether* any of them will buy. Only [42](42-validation-plan.md) answers that.

## Review trigger

Re-score this matrix at the day-30 gate ([43](43-launch-plan.md)) using actual conversion data
per segment, replacing judgment scores with observed ones. If any segment we declined generated
unsolicited inbound that converted, re-open it.
