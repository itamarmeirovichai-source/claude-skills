## 2. Recommendation

### Primary: Instagram Reels, cross-posted to TikTok
**Why:** our product *is* short video. Showing the work is the most credible possible proof for a
business with no track record, and the marginal cost of producing our own content is near zero
because we are already in the editor. It also builds an asset that compounds
([13](13-consumer-psychology-and-sales-method.md) §6 — process transparency substituting for
social proof we do not have).

**Cost:** ~4 hours/week ([29](29-content-calendar.md)).
**Risk:** low buying intent (score 2) and poor tracking (score 2). We may generate views and no
customers — **that is the explicit A-04 kill condition**, and it is a real possibility.

### Supporting: Partnerships
**Why:** the highest buying intent available to us (score 5) and the lowest creative load.
Property photographers already hold the relationship, the assets, and the rights conversation
([33](33-partnership-plan.md)). Slow to start, but each relationship compounds.

### Bounded experiment: Meta paid
**Why:** the only channel that produces evidence in days rather than months (score 5). Its job at
launch is **not customer acquisition — it is learning which message works**
([31](31-experiment-design.md)).
**Hard control:** an **account spending limit** must be set before any campaign runs. Meta daily
budgets are not caps — reported single-day allowance is daily × 1.75 with a rolling 7-day cap at
7× daily (E-34, D-09). At a $300 test budget, an unmonitored 75% overrun is material, and A-15
says nobody is watching for 8–10 hours at a time.

### Slow compounding: search content
Two or three genuinely useful pages, written once ([32](32-search-email-remarketing.md)). Time to
evidence is the worst of any channel (score 1), so it gets effort but no expectations.

## 3. What should wait, and why

| Channel | Wait until |
|---|---|
| **Google Ads** | We have a measured conversion rate. High intent, but search ads against an unvalidated landing page buy expensive clicks to learn something a $300 Meta test teaches for less. **Revisit at 90 days** ([32](32-search-email-remarketing.md)) |
| **Industry events** | Revenue exists. VRMA-type events reach exactly our ICP (E-35) at travel-and-ticket cost, against a $400 pre-revenue ceiling ([15](15-financial-model.md) §9) |
| **Creator collaborations** | We can afford to lose the fee. Paying a creator before knowing our own message works is buying distribution for an unvalidated offer |
| **Long-form YouTube** | Probably never. Worst time-to-evidence and highest production load of anything on the list |
| **Cold outreach** | Channel rules verified (V-2) **and** a portfolio exists. See [16](16-sales-playbook.md) §2 |
| **Referrals** | We have customers. This is not a choice — it is a dependency |

## 4. The marketplace question

Fiverr/Upwork scores well on intent (5) and time-to-evidence (4) and would produce orders
quickly. It is rejected at launch for two reasons worth stating explicitly, because the
temptation will recur:

1. **It selects for the buyer we cannot serve profitably.** Marketplace buyers arrive
   price-anchored to the $40 tier (OBJ-8). At our $149 floor, we would be the expensive option
   competing on a page that sorts by price.
2. **It is a platform whose rules we do not control**, ranking our livelihood, with a
   very-high third-party risk score.

**However** — it is the fastest available test of A-01 (will anyone pay at all). If 30 days of
organic and $300 of ads produce nothing, a bounded marketplace listing at full price is a
legitimate diagnostic before concluding that nobody buys. Registered as a conditional experiment
in [31](31-experiment-design.md).

## 5. Community participation rules

For Facebook groups, forums, and subreddits where our ICP gathers:

- **Read the rules first. Follow them.** Most STR groups ban promotion outright.
- Participate by answering questions **without linking**. The Airbnb-video fact (E-01) is
  genuinely useful and we can give it away freely.
- Never post a pitch, never DM from a group thread, never use multiple accounts.
- **No automated posting, no fabricated engagement, no bought followers or reviews.**
- If a group permits promotion on a specific day or thread, use exactly that.

**The measure of success here is not leads. It is whether anyone recognises the name later.**

## 6. Audience and message matrix

| Segment | Buying trigger | Awareness level | Main objection | Offer | Channel | Message angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2 owner 2–5 units | New unit going live | Problem-aware | "Can't I do this myself?" | Standard | IG Reels | "Your photos already contain a video" |
| S2 owner | Pre-season, 6–10 weeks out (A-14) | Solution-aware | Price | Standard | IG + Meta paid | "Seven files, three to five days" |
| S2 owner | Built a direct site, it's not converting (E-28) | Solution-aware | "Does it work?" | Standard | Meta paid | "You built the site. Here's something to put on it" |
| S3 PM 6–25 units | Onboarding a batch | Solution-aware | Consistency across a portfolio | Portfolio pilot | Partnerships, LinkedIn | "Fourteen units, one look, one schedule" |
| S3 PM | **Pitching a new owner** | Problem-aware | Cost per unit | Portfolio pilot | Meta paid (AD-4), LinkedIn | "Show owners the marketing you'd do for their property" |
| S3 PM | Direct-booking push | Solution-aware | Vendor reliability | Pilot | Partnerships | "A pilot of three, at full price, before anyone talks volume" |
| Any | Tried an AI tool, disliked it | **Most aware** | "AI looks fake" | Standard | IG Reels | "Here's what gets rejected before you see it" |
| Any | Confused about Airbnb video | Unaware | — | None yet | IG Reels, search | **"Airbnb won't let you post video"** — pure education |
| S1 host (declined) | Any | Unaware | — | **Decline** | — | Give them the fact, send them away kindly (C-3) |

**The bottom two rows are the interesting ones.** The Airbnb-video fact is our best organic hook
precisely because it contradicts what the rest of the category implies, and it costs us nothing
to give away. It also naturally filters: people who learn it and *still* want video are
self-qualifying into Q2.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

After 30 days:
- [ ] ≥20 pieces of organic content published
- [ ] Account spending limit set **before** the first ad impression (D-09)
- [ ] ≥1 partner conversation opened
- [ ] Every lead tagged with its source ([41](41-measurement-definitions.md))
- [ ] Cost per qualified lead calculated for the paid test
- [ ] **A-04 kill condition checked:** zero qualified leads from 30 days organic + $300 paid →
      pivot to partnership-led acquisition **before** changing the offer
