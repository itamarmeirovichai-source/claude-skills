# Vacation Rental Video Business — Operating System

An evidence-based operating system for a solo-founder business that produces AI-assisted
marketing video for vacation rental owners, property managers, and boutique hospitality
operators.

**Status:** Desk research and system design complete. **Zero customer contact has occurred.
Zero money has been spent. Zero claims have been validated by purchase.** Every commercial
assumption in here is labelled and testable.

## The three findings that shaped everything

1. **Airbnb does not accept host-uploaded video in the listing gallery.** Any offer built on
   "video for your Airbnb listing" is broken at the premise. See [06](06-two-marketing-systems.md)
   and [E-01 in the Evidence Ledger](02-evidence-ledger.md).
2. **Airbnb's content policy explicitly targets AI-altered listing media** that edits flaws,
   hides damage, or adds amenities. Generative video applied carelessly to a real property is a
   platform-risk product, not just a creative one. This forces the product to be *accurate by
   construction*. See [26](26-property-accuracy-rules.md).
3. **Nobody in public has credible causal evidence that property video increases bookings.**
   Correlational vendor claims are abundant; causal evidence is absent. We therefore cannot sell
   on booking lift, and the claim register forbids it. See [21](21-claim-register.md).

## Rules for maintaining this system

1. **One authoritative source per policy.** If a document needs a price, it links to
   [15](15-financial-model.md). If it needs a claim, it links to [21](21-claim-register.md). Do
   not restate policy — reference it. Where a document restates something for readability, it
   says "(authoritative: NN)".
2. **Every external fact gets an evidence ID** (`E-NN`) registered in
   [02](02-evidence-ledger.md), with its quality grade.
3. **Every gap gets an assumption ID** (`A-NN`) registered in [03](03-assumptions-register.md),
   with the decisions that depend on it.
4. **Nothing moves from "assumed" to "known" without a purchase, a measurement, or a primary
   source.** Documenting a weakness does not resolve it.
