## Platform guidance — what is official and what is folklore

**Separating these matters, because acting on folklore as though it were policy is how a content
plan wastes a month.**

| Official (E-33, grade B) | Practitioner folklore (E-33, grade E) |
|---|---|
| Meta runs a "Best Practices" hub in the Instagram professional dashboard | "The first 3 seconds decide everything" |
| Meta advises designing Reels to be followable **without sound** | "Post 3–5 times a week" |
| Meta states captions and on-screen text **increase view-through** | "The algorithm favours X" |
| | "Reels under 7 seconds get more reach" |

We act on the left column as guidance. We treat the right column as **untested heuristics** —
reasonable starting points, not facts, and never repeated to customers as though they were.

**We do not claim to know how any algorithm works.** Nobody outside those companies does, and
content marketing that asserts otherwise is the same failure mode as claiming booking lift.

**Two things we will not assume:** that virality is achievable by design, and that more posting
always produces better results. Both are common, both are unevidenced, and the second one
directly threatens the founder hours production needs.

## Notes on the plan

**Day 2 is the flagship post.** The Airbnb-video fact (E-01) is true, useful, contradicts what
the category implies, costs nothing to give away, and pre-qualifies anyone who still wants video
afterwards. If one post works in 30 days, this is the most likely candidate.

**Six of twenty posts have no CTA at all.** Content that only ever sells stops being worth
following. The ones giving away genuinely valuable information — the rights trap (day 16), the
selection guide (day 11), what gets listing media pulled (day 12) — are deliberately
pitch-free.

**Day 10 and day 20 are unusual and deliberate.** Publishing our real attempt ratio and our
decline list is the process-transparency substitute for social proof we do not have
([13](13-consumer-psychology-and-sales-method.md) §6). Businesses that hide the unglamorous parts
look identical to every competitor; showing them is cheap differentiation.

**Every demonstration uses illustrative properties, labelled.** No client property appears
without separate written portfolio permission ([26](26-property-accuracy-rules.md) §5).

## Production workflow for content

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Batching** | Two sessions per week, ~2 hours each. Never make a post the day it goes out |
| **Asset reuse** | Every client order produces (with permission) at least one process clip. **Series B and G cost almost nothing because we are already in the editor** |
| **Scheduling** | Native schedulers. Never an automated poster in a community space |
| **Adaptation** | Vertical master → TikTok and Shorts unchanged; 1:1 version for the feed. **One edit, three placements** |
| **Response** | Reply to every comment in one daily block. A question about the product gets an answer, not a pitch; the link goes in one reply, once |
| **Time budget** | **4 hours/week, hard cap.** If content is eating production hours, content loses ([15](15-financial-model.md) — time is the binding constraint) |

## What we will not do

- Buy followers, views, or engagement
- Post the same comment across many accounts
- Use engagement-bait ("comment YES for the link") as a growth tactic
- Claim knowledge of any algorithm
- Post client property without written portfolio permission
- Claim or imply booking outcomes (D-05)
- Use trending audio without checking the commercial-use position (E-24 — the same music trap)
- Present illustrative work as client work

## Acceptance criteria

After 30 days:
- [ ] ≥20 posts published
- [ ] Content time ≤4h/week, tracked
- [ ] Every post checked against [21](21-claim-register.md) before publishing
- [ ] Saves and profile visits recorded per post (proxies for intent; **views are not a metric**)
- [ ] ≥3 posts reused from client production work
- [ ] **Qualified leads attributable to organic, counted honestly** — including zero
- [ ] Top 3 and bottom 3 posts identified by profile visits, and the difference described
