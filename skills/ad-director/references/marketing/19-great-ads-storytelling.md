# Great short product films: storylines with a twist (פואנטה) that sell

Research brief (2026-10-07). Use it **after** `00-master-playbook`, `../creative-brain.md`, `01-fundamentals` and `02-niches`. It does not repeat them. It adds three things:
1. An annotated library of 49 proven short films.
2. 14 repeatable twist patterns, each with an AI shooting recipe.
3. A 3-storylines-per-product template with a scoring rubric.

**Method and evidence levels**
- **Watched:** 7 films were pulled from Ads of the World's video host (`video.adsoftheworld.com`) as timestamped contact sheets; their beat timings are measured.
- **Sourced:** all other premises and results come from award databases and trade press, cited inline.
- **[inf]** marks my own inference, including beat timings for films I could not watch.
- **Results are self-reported** unless marked otherwise.
- **No YouTube transcripts:** `fetch_yt.py` and the transcript API were bot-blocked (`IpBlocked`) here.

**What the winners share [inf]**
- **One idea, one image.** You can describe the twist in ≤12 words.
- **The product is the cause of the twist.** In Leon, Force, Moldy Whopper and Quiet the Noise, the twist does not work without the product.
- **The film still reads with the sound off,** except the talking-head examples (CRED, Claude, Old Spice).
- **The scale is small.** Over half carry the idea with 0–1 human faces. The film runs on the object, a world or the brand code.

## 2. Twist patterns for 15–30 s, and how to shoot them with AI
Every pattern is listed with its examples and an AI recipe. The recipes assume our stack (Flare/Nano-Banana stills, then Kling start/end frames or Seedance, then reel-studio edit). They need no lip-sync and at most hands or bodies without faces.

| # | Pattern | Mechanic | Examples | AI recipe (no lip-sync) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Reversal / rewind** | Time runs backwards; the rewind *is* the claim (undo, renewal, origin). | noitulovE, Dove Reverse Selfie, R.I.P. Leon (unsend) | Generate the **forward** event (shatter, melt, scatter) and play it **reversed** in the edit (`ffmpeg -vf reverse`). Reverse physics hides AI flaws [inf]. End on the product re-forming. |
| P2 | **Scale shift (FOOH)** | The product is giant in the real world, or the world is miniature. | Maybelline train, Jacquemus bags, Haut straightener, Revolut miniatures | Plate (Soul/Flare) plus product composited in Flare at scale. Kling with a slow drone or handheld push: "only the [product] moves". Add real-city cues (signage blank, people tiny). Label it as CGI. |
| P3 | **Literalised claim** | Make one product adjective physically true. | MacBook Air envelope (thin), Smirnoff Sea (pure), Mercedes chicken (stable), Škoda cake (lovely stuff) | Write the claim as one adjective, then find the everyday object that proves it. One big-idea still, then a 5 s i2v. No functional fake proof; it is a metaphor (Playbook rule). |
| P4 | **Invisible made visible** | Show the thing nobody can see: noise, scent, flavour, data. | Quiet the Noise, FACES, Maggi, Data Auction | Particles and objects as the "substance": notes floating, sound objects levitating. Seedance multi-reference handles the particles, Kling does the product lock. Use one colour world for the invisible stuff. |
| P5 | **It was the product all along** | The magic or mystery has a product cause, revealed at the end. | VW Force, Coca-Cola Happiness Factory, San Marco statues | Shoot the effect first (the lights obey, the statues are gone). The reveal is a **pull-back** using Kling start frame = close effect, end frame = wide with the product. |
| P6 | **Emotional misdirection / genre flip** | Set up genre A (tragedy, thriller, luxury), then pay off as genre B. | IKEA Lamp, R.I.P. Leon, BK Ice Burger, Moldy Whopper | Genre comes from **grade and music**, so it is cheap with AI. Score A drops to silence, then the product hit and score B. Use objects as the "characters" (a lamp, a glass) to avoid faces. |
| P7 | **Mirror / parallel** | Two worlds in the same composition; the second is the product's. | Heineken Walk-in Fridge, La MIE painter/chef | Generate both shots from **one locked composition prompt** with a single variable swap. Cut on the matching action or use a split screen. Hands only. |
| P8 | **Morph / transformation** | A form flows into the product, or the product into a form. | Guinness Surfer (waves→horses), Citroën robot, Masterpiece (bottle restyles) | Kling **start/end frames** do this natively. Start = the metaphor (wave), end = the product (pint). Keep the shapes compatible (silhouette and colour). |
| P9 | **Brand-code only** | The distinctive asset carries the film with little or no product. | McDonald's Arches, Heinz Draw Ketchup, Corona Sun Shadows, Coinbase QR | Find the brand **shape or colour** in nature or in the city (shadow, steam, ripple). Time-lapse via Kling slow drift. The logo is set in post. Cheapest pattern [inf]. |
| P10 | **Format hijack** | Borrow a familiar format and put the wrong content in it. | IKEA BookBook (keynote), Coinbase (screensaver), The Ordinary (exam hall) | Formats forgive AI: keynote stage, museum audio guide, nature-doc macro, product-recall notice. VO only. |
| P11 | **Escalation → release** | Pressure builds in 3 beats; the product releases it. | Revolut miniatures, Lacoste collapse, Honda Grrr | 3 escalating stills (tighter, darker, more objects), then one **release** shot (space, light, slow motion). The beat-3 cut lands on a music drop. |
| P12 | **Payoff loop** | The last frame returns to the first with new meaning; it rewards rewatching. | noitulovE (pint → pint), Surfer (waiting), Corona sunset | Kling start frame = the end frame of the last shot. Design a seamless reel loop and put the brand in the loop point. |
| P13 | **Personified object** | The product, or the old product, "acts" through motion only. | IKEA Lamp, Citroën robot, Telstra claymation | No faces needed. The acting is camera and physics (a tilt, a flicker). AI is strong at objects and weak at faces, so this suits it [inf]. |
| P14 | **Desire → effort / sacrifice** | Someone, or something, goes to absurd lengths for the product. | Peugeot Sculptor, Axe Angels, Talabat bites | Show the effort through objects: dents, tools, a trail of discarded alternatives. People appear as silhouettes or hands. |

**Pattern selection rules [inf]**
- **Pick from 3 different families** so the 3 storylines feel different (Andromeda "really different concepts", see 03/01):
  - Transform (P1, P2, P3, P4, P8)
  - Story (P5, P6, P7, P11, P13, P14)
  - Code/format (P9, P10, P12)
- **Map the claim to a pattern:**

| Claim type | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Purity or quality | P3, P1 |
| Cold or sensation | P4, P3 |
| Size or thinness | P3, P2 |
| Craft or heritage | P7, P12 |
| Convenience or speed | P5, P11 |
| Status or desire | P14, P2 |
| Scent or flavour | P4, P8 |

- **Avoid destruction-as-metaphor for creative tools** (the Crush backlash), and avoid spectacle with no claim (Roller Babies).

## 3. Writing 3 storylines per product
### 3a. Inputs (from the strategy card in 00-playbook)
Fill these in first:
- Product
- Single-minded claim (≤7 words)
- Proof object or mechanism
- Brand codes: colour, shape, sound, signature device
- Top objection
- Awareness level

### 3b. Generator steps
1. **Write the claim as an adjective plus a contrast object.** Example: "thin → envelope", "cold → 42 °F bath".
2. **Draft 5 premises across ≥3 pattern families.** Each premise is one sentence, in this form: "We think X; it turns out Y, because [product]."
3. **Run the twist tests on each premise:**
   - **Swap test:** put a competitor's logo in. Does the twist die? (It must.)
   - **Mute test:** is it readable without sound?
   - **One-line test:** can you tell it in ≤12 words?
4. **Keep 3 premises, one per family,** and write each in the card below.

### 3c. Storyline card (copy one per storyline)
```
TITLE — LOGLINE (≤25 words, ends on the twist)
Pattern: P# · Claim proven: … · Tone: aesthetic brand film
FRAME 0 (0–1 s): the strange, beautiful image; brand colour visible
BEATS (20 s master; adapt for 15/30):
 0–3   HOOK     – product or brand code in shot; question raised
 3–8   SETUP    – the rule of the world; product present (shot 2)
 8–14  BUILD    – 2 escalations/variations; crave shot ≥1
 14–17 TWIST    – silence 0.5 s → reveal; product CAUSES it
 17–20 RESOLVE  – hero packshot in colour world + logo + CTA; sonic logo
Brand in every shot via: colour / shape / pack / light / prop (name which per shot)
People: none | hands | body no face (state which)
AI plan: stills (Flare) → Kling start/end (P#) → reverse/loop in edit?
Risk: what could read wrong (Crush test) · Legal: claim is metaphor, not fake proof
Cutdowns: 6 s = frame 0 + twist + logo; 15 s = drop one BUILD beat
```

### 3d. Worked mini-example (fictional brand "NORDA" glacier water)
