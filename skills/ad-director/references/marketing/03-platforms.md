## Rules
1. **The whole idea goes in the first 3 s.** Hook visually and with audio, show the product or problem in frame 1, never open on a logo sting, and land the key message by 5 s.
2. **A hook does three jobs:** stop the scroll, qualify the viewer, set up the payoff. Proven patterns: bold claim or number, the pain in the viewer's own words, a surprising visual, a question, "POV:".
3. **Read hook rate and hold rate separately.** A low hook rate means the opening fails. A good hook rate with a low hold rate means the body breaks the hook's promise.
4. **9:16, made for sound on, with captions** that still carry the message muted. Speech + music is the strongest combination.
5. **Safe zones.**
   - Meta (unified, Mar 2026): top 14%, bottom 35%, 6% each side.
   - TikTok: top ~13%, bottom 37%, right 22%, left 11%.
   - Put text, logo and offer in the centre band.
6. **Supers** at 5–10 words/s within the first 7 s. Native-UI styles (caption boxes, sticky notes) over-index.
7. **Pacing.** Change the visual every 1.5–3 s in DR; static shots lose ~38%. Brand films can run slower but still need change.
8. **Length.** Awareness 6–15 s; conversion 15–34 s. Cut 6/15/30 s versions of every winner.
9. **Branding.**
   - Show the product several times (2.7×).
   - On DR, keep the brand under ~25% of screen time.
   - Product visibly on screen on TikTok.
   - "See & say" the brand (YouTube).
10. **One CTA, in both voice and text (1.9×).** Repeat it on Shorts; use CTA cards on TikTok; show the price in action ads.
11. **TikTok Creative Codes:** TikTok-first (3.3× more actions), trends, structure (hook → body → CTA), stimulation, sound (88% say sound is vital), creators.
12. **Shorts ABCDs by objective.**
    - Awareness: real talent, announce with audio.
    - Consideration: a see-and-say product moment.
    - Action: benefits first, show the price, repeat the CTA.
13. **Format choice.**
    - Conversion workhorses: offer-first, demo, testimonial, unboxing, before/after, listicle, us-vs-them.
    - Brand: aesthetic film, founder story, ASMR. Add a VO when recall matters.
14. **Premium vs budget.**
    - Premium: polished craft and detail demos, sensory close-ups, origin story, credible proof. Low-fi UGC can erode luxury.
    - Budget: offer-first, listicle, raw UGC, price shown.
    - Rule: the pricier the product, the more proof, detail and status cues it needs.
15. **Creative is the targeting (Andromeda).** Near-duplicate creatives cluster and compete. Run 8–15 distinct concepts (angle × persona × format × visual world × audio) with 2–3 iterations each.
16. **Concept vs iteration.**
    - A concept finds new audiences; an iteration (new hook, length or opener) extends a winner.
    - Split roughly 50/50, or 70/30 once a winner exists.
    - Test hooks by swapping only the first 3 s.
17. **AI disclosure.**
    - Meta labels significant AI edits, and photorealistic AI humans always get an "AI info" label.
    - TikTok requires the AIGC label for realistic content.
    - YouTube has an "altered or synthetic" label.
    - Never present fake testimonials, fake UGC creators, or renders that misrepresent the product.
18. **Trust.**
    - A large minority distrust AI ads, and ~90% want disclosure.
    - Accepted: stylised or obviously crafted visuals, variants, localisation.
    - Distrusted: uncanny hands, faces or lip-sync; fake people; generic voice and copy.
