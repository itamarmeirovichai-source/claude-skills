# Niche playbook: what makes people buy from short video ads

Research brief (2026-10-07).

**Key:** T = buying triggers, P = proof needed, F = formats, H = hero shots, O = objections and how to answer them, C = compliance, AI = fit for AI video (1–5).

## Cross-niche facts
- The first 3 s drive >60% of engagement. Open on the best sensory moment, not the logo ([tlinky](https://tlinky.com/tiktok-ads-for-beauty-brands/)).
- Native and UGC styles often beat polished work on CPA: about +50% CTR in beauty, and overproduced fitness ads had 40–60% worse CPA. Build a polished hero film, then cut a native-style variant.
- 64% of TikTok users love product sounds (fizz, pour, crunch) ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/pl-PL/blog/elevate-your-food-and-drink-ads)).
- The FTC banned fake and AI testimonials from 2024-10-21. Meta and TikTok AI labels apply.
- AI weak spots in 2026: hands, readable text and logos, fine jewelry detail, liquid and cloth physics over long shots, and the product changing mid-shot. Use Kling to lock the product; use Seedance for multi-reference story clips.

## Niches
- **Skincare.**
  - T: confidence, visible results, ritual.
  - P: texture swatch, before/after, ingredient %.
  - H: dropper drip, serum spread on the hand, dewy glow, cream swirl.
  - O: breakouts (show ingredients), worth it (cost per use).
  - C: say "improves the *appearance* of", never "treats"; Meta bans anti-wrinkle before/after.
  - AI: 4 for product and texture, 2 for faces.
- **Makeup.**
  - T: transformation, trend FOMO, an occasion.
  - P: swatches across skin tones, wear test.
  - H: lipstick twist-up, pigment smear, powder cloud, compact click, shimmer.
  - AI: 3.
- **Fragrance.**
  - T: identity and seduction, mood or memory, status.
  - P: notes visualised (fig, smoke, rain) through sensory storytelling; a sample or discovery set de-risks the purchase. $162M in TikTok Shop fragrance sales in 2025.
  - H: light refracting through glass, backlit spray mist, the notes visualised, a heavy cap closing.
  - O: blind buy (sample), longevity.
  - C: no pheromone or "attraction" claims.
  - **AI: 5.**
- **Haircare.**
  - P: before/after, swish, shine.
  - C: growth, loss and dandruff claims count as drug claims.
  - AI: 3.
- **Fashion.**
  - P: fit on bodies, fabric macro, several angles.
  - F: try-on haul, GRWM, home runway.
  - H: drape and swing, transitions.
  - AI: 3 (cloth physics is weak).
- **Sneakers.**
  - T: status and scarcity drops, identity, performance.
  - H: box-lid lift, laces snapping tight, sole compressing in slow motion, colourway reveal, floating exploded view.
  - AI: **4**.
- **Jewelry and watches.**
  - T: gifting and love, milestones, status.
  - H: light beam over facets, slow turn on velvet, second hand ticking in macro, clasp, box opening.
  - O: real gold or tarnish (state specs).
  - C: FTC Jewelry Guides.
  - AI: 4; dials and prongs can morph.
- **Supplements.**
  - The hardest niche: DSHEA "supports" language only, no disease claims; Meta bans before/after.
  - AI: 2.
- **Fitness gear.**
  - P: demo in a small apartment.
  - AI: 2–3 (human motion is weak).
- **Food and snacks.**
  - T: craving, novelty.
  - H: crunch, cheese pull, sauce pour, steam.
  - AI: 3 (bites look uncanny).
- **Beverages.**
  - H: can crack with spray, ice clink, bubbles, condensation, splash crown.
  - AI: 4; keep pours under 3 s.
- **Coffee.**
  - T: ritual, energy, craft identity.
  - H: bean cascade, grinder burst, espresso pull, crema, steam, latte art, milk swirling into iced coffee.
  - O: better than a café? (cost per cup).
  - AI: **4–5**.
- **Tech and audio.**
  - T: novelty, problem-solving, status.
  - H: exploded view, light sweep along the chassis, case snapping shut, an ANC sound-design cut.
  - O: battery, price versus Apple or Sony.
  - AI: 4 (screens and UI are weak).
- **Candles and home.**
  - T: cosiness, aesthetic identity, gifting.
  - H: match strike, wick catching, wax pool, flame flicker, lid lift.
  - O: scent throw, burn hours.
  - **AI: 5.**
- **Kitchen.**
  - The functional demo is the proof; faking it is deceptive.
  - AI: 3.
- **Pets.**
  - P: the pet's real reaction.
  - AI: 3.
- **Baby.**
  - AI: **1**; avoid.
- **Outdoor and travel.**
  - P: stress tests.
  - AI: 4 for mood, 2 for proof.
- **Luxury goods.**
  - T: status, scarcity, craft and heritage.
  - P: maker story, material macros. Craft videos that include a consumer scene raise purchase intent ([Emerald](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/APJML-09-2019-0551/full/html)).
  - H: leather grain, stitching, gold hardware glint, slow orbit on marble.
  - **AI: 5.**

## Best niches for a fictional premium brand made with AI (portfolio)
1. **Fragrance**: surreal by convention, no proof needed, and glass, mist and notes are what AI does best.
2. **Luxury leather goods and accessories**: slow orbits and material macros.
3. **Coffee / premium beverage**: beans, steam, crema; keep pours under 3 s.
4. **Candles and home fragrance**: flame light hides artefacts.
5. **Watches/jewelry or sneakers**: hard objects with dramatic light; add text in post.

**Avoid:** baby, supplements, skincare or haircare *results*, fitness transformations.

**Execution:** start from one product reference, shoot 3–5 s shots, add the logo and text in post, open on the sensory hook, cut a native variant, and label the piece as a fictional AI concept.
