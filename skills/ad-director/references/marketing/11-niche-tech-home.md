## Shared rules for these five niches
- **Proof drives purchase here, unlike fragrance.** AI footage must never *be* the demonstration. Show proof as one of:
  - a **metaphor/BIG IDEA**;
  - a **spec super with a footnote** ("up to 40 h, ANC off, 50% vol.");
  - a "dramatization" label.

  Real demo clips are for client work only. FTC: no fake or AI reviews (since 2024-10-21).
- **No real marks:** no Bluetooth, Hi-Res, UL/ETL, IGBC or Apple/Spotify UI. Generate logos, knob numerals, displays and wordmarks **blank**; comp them in post.
- **BAN LIST** (append to every prompt): no HDR, no oversaturation, no CG render look, no chrome spheres or bubbles, no reflective void floor, no centred symmetric composition, no generated text, numbers or logos, no glowing UI, no flare unless the source is in frame, no slow-motion easing, no locked-off camera, no extra fingers or paws, the product never changes shape.
- **ElevenLabs default:**
  - Music: 18 s, timestamped: 0–2 s diegetic only, a hit on the BIG-IDEA cut, a 0.4 s breath at 13.5 s, then a 2-note sonic logo.
  - SFX: layered close, mid and far (≤22 s each).
  - VO: one eleven_v3 line in the last 3 s (`[calm]`/`[warm]`), with burned-in captions.
- **Edit default:**
  - 24 fps film chain (06), type inside the safe zones.
  - A 6 s cutdown = BIG IDEA + packshot + CTA.
  - A native variant: no letterbox, handheld punch-ins, captions.
- **Budget (≤ $10):**

## 1. TECH & AUDIO (headphones, speakers, gadgets): AI 4

**Grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - a 3/4 hero with a light sweep along the chassis;
  - grille, mesh and knit macros;
  - the hand-to-earcup press;
  - the wearer from behind or in profile, low angle;
  - an exploded view with real shadows;
  - the case snap; LED dots.
- **Light:** gradient strip-box wraps on gloss ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/how-to-create-gradients-of-light-product-photography/)); overcast street light for lifestyle.
- **Colour:** monochrome plus one accent.
- **Texture:** anodised micro-grain, fingerprints.
- **Pacing:** 0.8–1.5 s, one feature per beat.
- **Type:** small, wide-tracked Heebo Light.
- **Sound signature:** the **ANC cut**, city noise hard-cut to silence. Apple's "Quiet the Noise" levitates a street until she toggles ANC ([shots.net](https://shots.net/news/view/megaforce-quiet-the-noise-for-apple)).

**Buy triggers and traps**
- Buyers want novelty, problem-solving (commute, focus), status, and battery/fit versus Apple or Sony.
- Superlatives need substantiation plus a footnote.
- Battery and water claims carry test conditions (IPX4 ≠ waterproof).
- Screens need "screen images simulated".

**Formats (18 s)**
1. **Noise→silence.**
   - 0–3 s: chaotic city macros, handheld.
   - 3–4 s: earcup press, hard mute.
   - 4–12 s: the same street, slowed and desaturated.
   - 12–15 s: chassis sweep, "Up to 40 h".
   - 15–18 s: packshot.
2. **Anatomy.**
   - 0–2 s: perforation macro with a raking light.
   - 2–8 s: exploded parts settle.
   - 8–12 s: case snap, LED.
   - 12–18 s: hero sweep, logo.
3. **Sound made visible.**
   - 0–3 s: the speaker on a sideboard, water trembling in a glass.
   - 3–10 s: ripples in a liquid tray, curtains breathing.
   - 10–15 s: a dancer's back.
   - 15–18 s: end card.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional HALDE / ORRE)**
- **"Turn the city off."** She cups the earcup. The street behind her is a **printed paper backdrop** that tears and slumps to the floor, revealing a quiet grey studio (Nothing's practical trick hides AI).
- **"Bass you can see."** Each kick draws rings across a black water tray over the speaker. The final ring resolves into the logo (comp).

**Recipe**
- **STYLE HEADER:** "ARRI Alexa 35, 40mm Cooke S4, overcast 6000K daylight plus one gridded strip light, concrete-grey world with one burnt-orange accent, matte anodised aluminium with micro-grain and real fingerprints, Kodak Vision3 250D grain, muted contrast, off-centre thirds. [BAN LIST]"
- **Shot plan:**

  | Shot | Still | Motion |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1 | Flare + ref. Headphones on a raw concrete plinth, left third, 3/4 view, the strip light skimming the earcup, the rest falling to grey, blank earcup, dust on the plinth. | Kling: "strip light slides left to right drawing a moving highlight; only the light moves; handheld micro-drift; already at full speed." |
  | S2 | Flare. Grille-perforation macro at f/11, a 3200K raking slash, one smudge. | Kling: "10 cm push-in, focus lands a beat late, perforations stay rigid." |
  | S3 | Soul Cinema for the woman, then a Flare composite. Back of a woman, hand cupping the earcup, a rainy street defocused, 85mm. | Kling with start and end frames: "hand presses earcup once; crowd behind slows; she never turns her face." |
  | S4 BIG IDEA | Soul Cinema. Studio with a giant printed city photo hung on a pipe, a figure in the foreground. | Seedance i2v, 6 s, 480p: "paper backdrop tears from the top and slumps in one heavy fold revealing a grey cyc; dust lifts; she keeps walking; handheld 35mm." |
  | S5 | Flare. Components floating in a row over a pale grey sweep, each casting a soft shadow, a window-shadow slash. | Kling: "parts drift together and click into the headphone; shadows follow." |

- **Screens:** black glass reflecting the room, or angled away. Comp UI and supers in ffmpeg. LEDs as "three small white dots", never digits.
- **Sound:** horns and a distant jackhammer, then a 50 ms hard mute to room tone and one Rhodes note. SFX: "magnetic case snap, close-mic". Music: "minimal electronic, 96 bpm, silence at 3.0 s".

**AI weak spots → fixes**
- Logos warp → blank shells, comp the logo.
- Glossy CG look → matte finish, fingerprints, overcast light.
- Earbud-in-ear anatomy → profile or back shots, hair over the ear.
- Centred drift → a layout map.

## 2. CANDLES & HOME FRAGRANCE / DECOR: AI 5

**Grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - match strike, then the wick catching;
  - wax-pool macro;
  - lid lift with a smoke curl;
  - a hand with a snuffer;
  - a blue-hour room wide with the candle as the only practical;
  - linen, ceramics, travertine, dried stems in the foreground.
- **Light:** the **flame (2000 K) as the motivated key** against cool 5600 K window spill.
- **Colour:** ivory, tobacco and smoke, or one bold hue (Boy Smells pink; [adlibrary](https://adlibrary.com/brands/boy-smells)).
- **Pacing:** 2–3 s holds. Diptyque proves that stillness is the luxury.
- **Type:** lowercase Cormorant italic.
- **Sound:** match rasp, wooden-wick crackle, rain on glass, felt piano.

**Buy triggers and traps**
- Buyers want cosiness, identity, gifting, scent throw, and burn hours ("approx.").
- No aromatherapy or health claims. "Clean-burning" or "non-toxic" need substantiation.
- **Never show unsafe use** (a flame unattended, near curtains, kids or pets): it contradicts the NCA label rules "burn within sight / keep away from things that catch fire / from children" ([candles.org](https://candles.org/understanding-safety-standards/)).

**Formats (18 s)**
1. **Ritual.**
   - 0–2 s: strike.
   - 2–5 s: wick catches.
   - 5–10 s: the room warms (a grade shift from cool to warm).
   - 10–14 s: lid lift, smoke.
   - 14–18 s: packshot and notes.
2. **Notes as a world.** Three 3 s literal-note cuts (fig leaf in rain, cedar shavings, smoke on stone), each match-cut to the wax pool, then hero and CTA.
3. **Time.** One 15 s held composition while sunlight crosses the room and the pool widens. "50 hours, approx." Logo.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional MAISON VEILLE)**
- **"Light it, and the week ends."** As the wick catches, the rain-streaked window behind it turns to warm late-summer dusk.
- **"A room in a jar."** The melted pool reflects a sunlit Mediterranean courtyard instead of the ceiling, and the camera pushes into the reflection.

**Recipe**
- **STYLE HEADER:** "35mm Kodak Portra 400 look, single candle flame 2000K as key, cool 5600K window spill from left, deep but detailed shadows, ivory linen, tobacco ceramics, travertine, dust motes in the window beam, soot ring on the glass rim, wax drips, off-centre thirds, 50mm f/2. [BAN LIST]"
- **Shot plan:**

  | Shot | Still | Motion |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1 | Flare + ref. Unlit amber-glass candle, right third, on a scarred oak sideboard, blue-hour window, blank label. | Kling: "a match enters left, strikes, sulphur flare, lights the wick; flame steadies with small flicker; handheld sway." |
  | S2 | Flare. Top-down macro of a liquid wax pool, black wick, soot specks. | Kling: "wax trembles, flame reflection dances, slow push-in." |
  | S3 BIG IDEA | Flare start frame (rain window) and end frame (the same framing, golden dusk). | Kling `last_image_url`: "window light shifts from cold rain to warm dusk; candle untouched; only breathing camera." |
  | S4 | Soul Cinema. Empty blue-hour living room, the candle a pinpoint, 16mm wide. | Kling: "very slow lateral slide; curtain stirs in a draught." |
  | S5 | Flare. Linen-sleeved hand lowering a brass snuffer, a smoke ribbon. | Kling: "smoke ribbon rises and curls, 3 s, full speed." |

- **Sound:** rasp and crackle (close), rain (mid), street (far).

**AI weak spots → fixes**
- Flame loops or strobes → small, slightly defocused flame, 3–4 s clips.
- Smoke goes CG → short ribbons only.
- Labels → comp them.
- Too clean → name soot, drips, dust.
- Decor → empty rooms from Soul Cinema at $0.004 each, product composited with Flare.

## 3. KITCHEN & APPLIANCES: AI 3 (cookware stills 4)

**Grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - top-down stovetop and tablescape;
  - pan at 3/4 on a grate;
  - sizzle macro;
  - steam backlit;
  - knife through vegetables;
  - espresso pull;
  - pastel appliances as colour blocks in a lived-in kitchen (crumbs, a towel, tile grout).
- **Light:** north window plus a backlight for steam.
- **Pacing:** a fast function montage (Our Place: 1–1.5 s).
- **Type:** friendly serif.
- **Sound:** sizzle, spatula scrape, lid clink, grinder.

**Buy triggers and traps**
- Buyers want versatility ("replaces 8 pieces"), ease (slides off, wipes clean), design-as-decor, and cost per use.
- **The demo is the proof, so a faked performance is deceptive.**
- "Non-toxic/PFAS-free": HexClad paid $2.5 M over PTFE ([TINA](https://truthinadvertising.org/class-action/hexclad-cookware/?pg=7)).
- Wattage and capacity must be exact. No fake safety marks.

**Formats (18 s)**
1. **One pan, many jobs.** Seven 1.2 s top-down uses, match-cut on the pan rim, then a 3 s hero and a 3 s end card.
2. **Satisfying proof.**
   - 0–3 s: caramel burnt on.
   - 3–7 s: it slides off and wipes clean ("dramatization").
   - 7–12 s: plated dinner.
   - 12–18 s: colourway line-up, CTA.
3. **Morning ritual (appliance).**
   - 0–2 s: dark kitchen, power light.
   - 2–6 s: grind burst.
   - 6–10 s: pull (under 3 s).
   - 10–14 s: steam wand.
   - 14–18 s: window, cup, logo.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional OKKA / BRIO)**
- **"Clear the cupboard."** A cabinet crammed with eight mismatched pots, then a hard cut to the same cabinet empty except for the OKKA pan, lit like a museum piece.
- **"Your café is home."** Push into the crema. Its reflection is a sunlit Roman terrace; pull out to a small real kitchen.

**Recipe**
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Sony Venice 2, 35mm Zeiss Supreme T2.8, soft north window key from left, warm 3200K pendant practical, lived-in kitchen with worn tile grout, crumbs, linen towel, cast-iron grate, steam backlit, sage-and-cream palette, Kodak 250D grain. [BAN LIST]"
- **Shot plan:**

  | Shot | Still | Motion |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1 | Flare + ref. Sage ceramic pan on a gas grate, left third, high 3/4, blue flame ring, oil splatter on the hob, blank handle. | Kling: "butter drops in and foams, bubbles spread; handheld drift; no slow motion." |
  | S2 | Flare. Top-down of seared vegetables in the pan on a worn butcher block, a hand with a wooden spatula from the right. | Kling: "spatula stirs once; steam rises into the backlight." |
  | S3 BIG IDEA | Soul Cinema, the same cupboard full, then empty, plus a Flare still of the pan on the shelf. | Hard cut in the edit, then a Kling slow push. |
  | S4 | Flare + ref. Pastel espresso machine on terrazzo, morning sun slash, blank dial. | Kling: "twin streams fall into a glass cup for 3 s, crema forming; breathing camera." |
  | S5 | Flare. Top-down tablescape, off-centre. | Seedance 6 s: "four hands serve; plates move; no faces." |

- **Sound:** sizzle (close), extractor hum (mid), radio (far). Music: "bossa guitar, 110 bpm".

**AI weak spots → fixes**
- Chewing and biting → show plated food only.
- Pours over 3 s distort.
- Utensils clip through food → one stir.
- Food morphs → macro, one action.
- Displays and knob numerals → blank, then comp.
- Hands → one hand at the frame edge.

## 4. FITNESS & OUTDOOR GEAR: AI 2–3 for bodies, 4 for gear and mood

**Grammar**
- **Fitness:**
  - chalked hands on a bar;
  - feet on a treadmill;
  - a small-apartment corner;
  - sweat macro;
  - window silhouettes.
- **Outdoor:**
  - a tiny human in a vast landscape;
  - gear macros (zip, ripstop, rain beading);
  - stress tests;
  - weathered faces, 5 am alarms;
  - YETI-style mixed formats (fisheye, IR trail-cam, 4:3 snapshots).
- **Light:** hard dawn sidelight, overcast, headlamp.
- **Colour:** desaturated olive-teal-earth.
- **Pacing:** 1–2 s, ramping.
- **Type:** Heebo Bold caps.
- **Sound:** breath, wind, gravel, ice rattle.

**Buy triggers and traps**
- Durability proof sells: the Stanley tumbler that survived a car fire with ice still inside got 60 M+ views ([Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/viral-video-lands-user-new-car-stanley-tumbler-withstands-fire)). So do identity, space-saving and community.
- No weight-loss or results claims, no transformations.
- Avoid body-pressure and gender tropes: Peloton's "Gift That Gives Back" knocked up to 10% off the stock ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2019/12/04/peloton-stock-falls-after-backlash-gift-that-gives-ad)).
- "Waterproof" and "bear-resistant" need ratings or certification. Green Guides: no vague "sustainable".

**Formats (18 s)**
1. **Abuse test.**
   - 0–3 s: thrown, dropped or frozen.
   - 3–9 s: three escalating ordeals.
   - 9–13 s: the reveal (ice still rattles).
   - 13–18 s: hero, CTA, "dramatization".
2. **Dawn mission.**
   - 0–2 s: alarm (digits comped).
   - 2–8 s: laces, door, trail.
   - 8–13 s: summit wide.
   - 13–18 s: gear macro, logo.
3. **Apartment gym.**
   - 0–3 s: cramped living room.
   - 3–10 s: the gear unfolds and stows (gear-only shots).
   - 10–15 s: silhouette workout.
   - 15–18 s: end card.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional KETTLE PEAK / FOLDA)**

- **"The gym is folded."** Top-down of a living room. The rug unrolls into a lifting platform with the dumbbells, then rolls back to a tidy room.

**Recipe**

[BAN LIST]"

## 5. PET PRODUCTS: AI 3 (single-behaviour close-ups 4)

**Grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - shot at a 50 cm pet's-eye height;
  - head tilt at a squeak;
  - nose pushing open the box;
  - POV from inside the box (BarkBox);
  - paws on wood;
  - a cat's slow blink in a sun patch;
  - the empty bowl after;
  - the sofa lifestyle.
- **Light:** soft window light, golden backyard.
- **Colour:** warm, with one brand colour on toys and pack.
- **Pacing:** 1.5–2 s with reaction holds.
- **Type:** rounded sans.
- **Sound:** tag jingle, squeaker, crinkle, paws, a happy grunt.

**Buy triggers and traps**
- **The pet's reaction is the proof**, along with health and safety, chewer durability, and owner joy or guilt.
- Treat and food claims follow AAFCO/FDA. "Vet recommended" needs data.
- AI animals are dramatization: never present them as real reactions or reviews.
- No unsafe use: unsupervised chews, hot cars, toxic foods on the table.

**Formats (18 s)**
1. **Reaction first.**
   - 0–2 s: squeak, head tilt.
   - 2–6 s: run to the box.
   - 6–11 s: play.
   - 11–15 s: asleep on the bed.
   - 15–18 s: packshot.
2. **Pet-POV world.** The whole film at 50 cm: human legs, the door, the treat jar as a monument. End card at dog height.
3. **Made for them.**
   - 0–3 s: squeaks aimed at the viewer's dog.
   - 3–12 s: dogs in many homes turn their heads.
   - 12–18 s: brand.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional NOOK)**
- **"The sound they wait for."** The NOOK jar lid pops. Across the city, at windows and in parks, four dogs lift their ears in unison.
- **"Built at their height."** The house from 50 cm: everything is out of reach except the NOOK bed, the only thing lit and in focus.

**Recipe**
- **Pet sheet first:** Soul Cinema, the same breed, a grey seamless, side, front and 3/4 views. It feeds Seedance reference-to-video, because Kling i2v drifts coat patterns.
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Canon C70 at 50cm height, 35mm f/2, soft window key from left, warm 4300K, real fur with individual backlit guard hairs, slightly wet nose, shed hairs on the fabric, lived-in home with a scuffed oak floor, Portra grain, handheld at pet height. [BAN LIST + no extra legs, no human-like expressions, no cartoon eyes]"
- **Shot plan:**

  | Shot | Still | Motion |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1 | Flare + bed ref. Golden retriever on the NOOK bed, left third, oak floor. | Kling: "dog lifts its head and tilts it once to the right; ears move; one blink; mouth closed." |
  | S2 | Flare. Low POV from inside a cardboard box up at a dog's nose. | Kling: "nose pushes in, sniffs, whiskers twitch." |
  | S3 BIG IDEA | Seedance reference-to-video, 8 s: "Exactly 4 shots, hard cuts: beagle on a windowsill, husky in a park, pug on a sofa, terrier at a door; each lifts its ears and turns its head toward camera left; natural canine timing." | |
  | S4 | Flare. A cat in a sun patch beside the product. | Kling: "slow blink once; tail tip flicks." |
  | S5 | Flare. Bowl licked clean, the product beside it. | Kling: "light shifts; breathing camera." (Proof without eating.) |

- **Animal rules:**
  - one behaviour per 3–4 s clip;
  - no human smiles;
  - paws occluded by a rug or the frame edge;
  - **no eating, drinking or licking on camera**;
  - no petting hands (fingers intersect the fur), or a hand only at the frame edge;
  - no two animals interacting.
- **Sound:** jingle, squeaker, crinkle (close); paws on wood (mid); kids outside (far). VO `[warm]`. Music: "ukulele and whistle, 100 bpm".

**AI weak spots → fixes**
- Coat drift → pet sheet plus Seedance refs.
- Leg count and gait → crop the legs.
- Mouth–food contact → before/after only.
- Uncanny "acting" → name canine behaviours.
- Fur smears at speed → keep motion slow.
- Cats morph more than dogs → stills plus a slow push.
