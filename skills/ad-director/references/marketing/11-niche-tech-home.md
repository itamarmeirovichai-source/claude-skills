## Shared rules for these five niches
- **Proof is the purchase driver here, unlike fragrance.** AI footage must never *be* the demonstration. Show proof as (a) a **metaphor/BIG IDEA**, (b) a **spec super with footnote** ("up to 40 h, ANC off, 50% volume"), or (c) a "dramatization" label. Real demo clips are for client work only. FTC: no fake or AI testimonials or reviews (since 2024-10-21).
- **No real marks:** no Bluetooth, Hi-Res, UL/ETL, IGBC or Apple/Spotify UI. Render every logo, knob number, display and wordmark as **blank**, then comp in post.
- **BAN LIST** (append to every still and motion prompt): no HDR, no oversaturation, no CG render look, no chrome spheres or bubbles, no reflective void floor, no centred symmetric composition, no generated text, numbers or logos, no glowing UI, no lens flare unless the source is in frame, no slow-motion easing, no locked-off camera, no extra fingers or paws, the product never changes shape.
- **ElevenLabs default:**
  - Music: 18 s, timestamped. 0–2 s diegetic only. A hit on the BIG-IDEA cut. Drop out at 13.5 s for a 0.4 s breath, then a 2-note sonic logo.
  - SFX layered close, mid and far (≤22 s each).
  - One eleven_v3 VO line in the last 3 s, `[calm]` or `[warm]`.
  - Captions burned in.
- **Edit default:** 24 fps film chain (06). Type in the safe zone (top 14%, bottom 35%). A 6 s cutdown = BIG IDEA + packshot + CTA. A native variant: no letterbox, handheld punch-ins, captions.
- **Budget template (≤ $10):**

  | Item | Cost |

  | 5 Flare shot stills | $1.50 |

## 1. TECH & AUDIO (headphones, speakers, gadgets): AI score 4

**Visual grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - a 3/4 hero with a light sweep along the chassis;
  - grille, mesh and fabric macros;
  - the hand-to-earcup or press gesture;
  - the wearer in the world (back of head, profile, low angle);
  - an exploded view with real shadows (10: divewatch);
  - the case snapping shut;
  - LED status dots.
- **Light:** gradient strip-box wraps on glossy shells ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/how-to-create-gradients-of-light-product-photography/)), or overcast flat street light for lifestyle.
- **Colour worlds:** monochrome plus one accent (Nothing grey-teal, B&O red, Sonos pink).
- **Textures:** anodised micro-grain, knit fabric, perforations, fingerprints on gloss.
- **Pacing:** 0.8–1.5 s, one feature per beat.
- **Type:** small, wide-tracked sans (Heebo Light) or dot-matrix.
- **Sound:** the **ANC cut**, where city noise hard-cuts to near silence plus one music note. It is the genre's signature (Apple "Quiet the Noise", Megaforce: the street levitates until she toggles ANC; [shots.net](https://shots.net/news/view/megaforce-quiet-the-noise-for-apple)).

**What sells and the traps.** Buyers want novelty, problem-solving (noise, commute, focus), status, and battery, fit and price against Apple or Sony.
- Superlatives ("world's best ANC") need substantiation plus a footnote.
- Battery and water claims carry test conditions (IPX4 ≠ waterproof).
- Any screen needs "screen images simulated".
- Never fake third-party logos.

**3 formats (18 s)**
1. **Noise → silence.** 0–3 s: chaotic city macro montage (horns, drill), handheld. 3–4 s: hand presses the earcup, hard cut to silence. 4–12 s: the same street, slow and desaturated, she moves freely. 12–15 s: chassis macro sweep, "Up to 40 h" super. 15–18 s: packshot and CTA.
2. **Material anatomy.** 0–2 s: perforation macro with light raking across. 2–8 s: exploded parts settle with shadows. 8–12 s: case snap and LED. 12–15 s: the hero sweep. 15–18 s: logo.
3. **Sound made visible.** 0–3 s: the speaker on a sideboard, water in a glass beside it trembling. 3–10 s: bass ripples spreading through a liquid tray or sand, curtains breathing. 10–15 s: room wide, a person's back, dancing. 15–18 s: end card.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional brand HALDE)**
- **"Turn the city off."** She cups the earcup. The street behind her is a **printed paper backdrop** that peels and drops to the floor, revealing a quiet grey studio (Nothing's practical trick, which hides AI).
- **"Bass you can see."** An ORRE speaker sits under a shallow black water tray. Each kick draws perfect rings that **carry the logo shape** (comp the final ring in post).

**Recipe**
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with 40mm Cooke S4, overcast 6000K daylight plus one gridded strip light, monochrome concrete-grey world with one burnt-orange accent, matte anodised aluminium with fine micro-grain, real fingerprints, Kodak Vision3 250D grain, muted contrast, off-centre thirds. [BAN LIST]"
- **Shot plan:**

  | Shot | Still | Motion |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1 hero | Flare + product ref. Headphones on a raw concrete plinth at the left third, 3/4 view, one strip light skimming the earcup leaving a soft gradient highlight, the rest falling to grey, blank smooth earcup without logo, fine dust on the plinth. | Kling: "Strip light slides slowly left to right along the earcup drawing a moving highlight; only the light moves; camera breathing handheld micro-drift; already at full speed." |
  | S2 macro | Flare. Extreme macro of the speaker grille perforations at f/11, raking 3200K slash, one smudge. | Kling: "Slow push-in 10 cm, focus lands a beat late, perforations stay rigid." |
  | S3 gesture | Soul Cinema for the woman, Flare for the composite. A woman from behind, a gloved hand cupping the earcup, a rainy street out of focus, 85mm. | Kling start+end frames: "Hand presses earcup once; background crowd slows; no face turn." |

A studio with a huge printed city photo backdrop hung on a pipe, a figure in the foreground. | Seedance 2.5 i2v 6 s, 480p draft: "Paper backdrop tears from the top and slumps to the floor in one heavy fold, revealing a grey cyc; dust lifts; the figure keeps walking; handheld 35mm." |
  | S5 exploded | Flare. Components floating in a line over a pale grey sweep, each casting a soft shadow, a window-shadow slash. | Kling: "Components drift together and click into the assembled headphone; shadows follow." |

- **Screens and text:** keep phone screens black, reflecting the room, or angled away. Comp UI and supers in ffmpeg. LEDs are simple dots: "three small white LED dots", never digits.
- **Sound:** city bed (horns, a jackhammer at a distance); a 50 ms hard mute to room tone; a single Rhodes note. SFX: "plastic case magnet snap, close-mic". Music brief: "minimal electronic, 96 bpm, sub kick, silence at 3.0 s".

**AI weak spots and workarounds**
- Screens and UI: comp them in post.
- Logos warp in motion: blank shells, comp the logo on the packshot only.
- Symmetric product drift: give it an off-centre layout map.
- Glossy CG look: matte finishes, fingerprints, overcast light.
- Earbuds in ears (anatomy): back or profile shots, hair over the ear.

## 2. CANDLES & HOME FRAGRANCE / HOME DECOR: AI score 5

**Visual grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - the match strike, then the wick catching;
  - wax pool macro and flame flicker in a dark room;
  - lid lift with a smoke curl;
  - a hand lowering a cloche;
  - room wides at blue hour with the candle as the only practical;
  - linen, ceramics, stone; dried stems in the foreground.
- **Light:** **flame as the motivated key** (2000 K) against cool window daylight (5600 K).
- **Colour:** ivory, tobacco and smoke, or one bold brand hue (Boy Smells pink; [adlibrary](https://adlibrary.com/brands/boy-smells)).
- **Pacing:** slow, 2–3 s holds; Diptyque proves stillness *is* the luxury.
- **Type:** lowercase Cormorant italic, small.
- **Sound:** match rasp, wooden-wick crackle, rain on glass, a soft piano.

**What sells and the traps.** Cosiness, identity, gifting, scent throw and burn hours ("approx. 50 h").
- No health or aromatherapy claims ("relieves anxiety").
- "Clean-burning" or "non-toxic" claims need substantiation.
- **Never show unsafe use**: the flame unattended, near curtains, kids or pets. These contradict the NCA label rules "burn within sight / keep away from things that catch fire / keep away from children" ([candles.org](https://candles.org/understanding-safety-standards/)).

**3 formats (18 s)**
1. **Ritual.** 0–2 s: match strike macro. 2–5 s: wick catches. 5–10 s: the room warms (cut from cool to warm grade). 10–14 s: hand lifts the lid and smoke curls. 14–18 s: packshot with the note list.
2. **Notes as a world.** Three 3 s cuts of literal notes (fig leaf in rain, cedar shavings, smoke over stone), each match-cut to the wax pool, then hero and CTA.
3. **Time.** A 15 s single locked composition with drifting window light: sun crosses the room while the wax pool widens; "50 hours, approx." super; logo.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional brand MAISON VEILLE)**
- **"Light it, and the week ends."** As the wick catches, the rain-streaked window behind it slowly **turns to late-summer dusk**: warm haze, cicadas.
- **"A room in a jar."** The macro of the melted wax pool **reflects a sunlit Mediterranean courtyard** instead of the ceiling. The camera pushes into the reflection.

[BAN LIST]"
- **Shot plan:**

  | Shot | Still | Motion |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1 | Flare + product ref. Amber glass candle at the right third on a scarred oak sideboard, unlit, blue-hour window, a blank label area. | Kling: "A match enters frame left, strikes, flare of sulphur flame, lights the wick; flame steadies with small flicker; handheld micro-sway." |
  | S2 | Flare. Macro top-down of a liquid wax pool with a black wick, small soot specks, flame out of focus. | Kling: "Wax surface trembles, flame reflection dances; slow push-in." |
  | S3 BIG IDEA | Flare, start frame and end frame: the same candle, rain window, then the same framing with a golden dusk courtyard. | Kling `last_image_url`: "Window light shifts from cold rain to warm dusk; candle untouched; no camera move except breathing." |

- **Sound:** match rasp (close); wooden-wick crackle loop; rain on glass (mid); street (far). Music: "felt piano and cello, 60 bpm, sparse".

**AI weak spots and workarounds**
- Flame physics can loop or strobe: keep the flame small and slightly defocused, 3–4 s clips.
- Smoke turns to CG: short ribbons only.
- Labels: comp them.
- Too clean: name soot, drips, dust.
- Home decor: generate **empty rooms** in Soul Cinema (cheap), then composite the product with Flare.

## 3. KITCHEN & APPLIANCES: AI score 3 (4 for cookware stills)

**Visual grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - top-down stovetop and tablescape;
  - pan hero at 3/4 on a stove grate;
  - sizzle macro, steam rising into a backlight;
  - knife through vegetables;
  - espresso pull or crema;
  - an appliance in a real, lived-in kitchen (crumbs, a towel, a tiled splashback);
  - SMEG-style pastel appliances as colour blocks.
- **Light:** a big north window plus a backlight for the steam.
- **Pacing:** fast functional montage (Our Place: about 15 uses in 31 s).
- **Type:** friendly serif or rounded sans.
- **Sound:** sizzle, a spatula scrape, a lid clink, the grinder.

**What sells and the traps.** Versatility ("replaces 8 pieces"), ease (food slides, wipe-clean), design-as-decor, and cost per use.
- **The functional demo is the proof, so faking it is deceptive.** Food styling cannot misstate performance.
- "Non-toxic" or "PFAS-free" claims cost HexClad a $2.5 M settlement over PTFE ([TINA](https://truthinadvertising.org/class-action/hexclad-cookware/?pg=7); [Chowhound](https://chowhound.com/1884025/gordon-ramsay-hexclad-cookware-brand-toxin-claims)).
- Wattage and capacity claims must be exact.
- No fake safety certification marks.

**3 formats (18 s)**
1. **One pan, many jobs.** Seven 1.2 s top-down cuts (sear, steam, fry, strain, braise, bake, serve), with a match-cut on the pan's rim each time. Then a 3 s hero and a 3 s end card.
2. **Satisfying proof.** 0–3 s: sticky caramel burns on. 3–7 s: it slides off and wipes clean ("dramatization" label for our concepts). 7–12 s: plated dinner. 12–18 s: the colour line-up and CTA.
3. **Morning ritual (appliance).** 0–2 s: dark kitchen, the machine's power light. 2–6 s: grind burst. 6–10 s: espresso pull (under 3 s). 10–14 s: steam wand. 14–18 s: window, cup, logo.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional brand OKKA cookware / BRIO espresso)**
- **"Clear the cupboard."** Wide on an open cabinet stacked with eight mismatched pots. Hard cut: the same cabinet empty, with **only the OKKA pan** on the shelf, lit like a museum piece.
- **"Your café is home."** The camera pushes into the crema, and its reflection becomes a **sunlit Roman café terrace**, then pulls out to the user's small kitchen.

**Recipe**
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Shot on Sony Venice 2, 35mm Zeiss Supreme at T2.8, soft north window key from camera left, warm 3200K practical pendant, real lived-in kitchen: worn tile grout, crumbs, linen towel, cast-iron grate, steam backlit, muted sage-and-cream palette, Kodak 250D grain. [BAN LIST]"
- **Shot plan:**

  | Shot | Still | Motion |
  |---|---|---|
  | S1 | Flare + ref. Sage ceramic pan on a gas grate at the left third, 3/4 high angle, blue flame ring under it, a splatter of oil on the hob, blank handle. | Kling: "Butter drops in and foams, small bubbles spread; handheld drift; no slow motion." |
  | S2 | Flare. Top-down of the pan with seared vegetables on a worn butcher block, a hand with a wooden spatula entering frame right. | Kling: "Spatula stirs once, steam rises into backlight." |
  | S3 BIG IDEA | Soul Cinema, two frames of the same cupboard: full of eight mixed pots, then empty. Flare for the final shelf with the pan. | Hard cut in the edit, plus a Kling slow push on the final frame. |
  | S4 | Flare + ref. Pastel espresso machine on a terrazzo counter, morning sun slash, a blank dial face. | Kling: "Twin espresso streams fall into a glass cup for 3 seconds, crema forming; camera static-breathing." |

- **Text and knobs:** generate blank dials and comp the numerals. Never show a readable display.
- **Sound:** sizzle (close), extractor hum (mid), radio murmur (far). SFX: "butter hitting hot ceramic pan, sizzle". Music: "upbeat bossa guitar, 110 bpm".

**AI weak spots and workarounds**
- Eating and biting look uncanny: show plated food, never chewing.
- Pours over 3 s distort; keep liquids short.
- Utensil–food contact clips through: one stir only.
- Food morphs: keep it in macro, one action.
- Appliance displays: blank them.
- Hands: one hand at the frame edge.

## 4. FITNESS & OUTDOOR GEAR: AI score 2–3 (human motion), 4 for gear and mood

**Visual grammar**
- **Fitness shots:**
  - chalked hands gripping a bar;
  - feet hitting a treadmill;
  - a small-apartment gym corner;
  - sweat macro;
  - silhouettes against a window;
  - a product snorricam (a bottle on the hip while running).
- **Outdoor shots:**
  - tiny human in a vast landscape;
  - gear macros (zip, ripstop, rain beading on DWR);
  - stress tests (cooler tumbling, tumbler in snow);
  - weathered faces, 5 am alarms.
  - YETI mixes formats: fisheye, trail-cam IR, 4:3 snapshots.
- **Light:** hard dawn sidelight, overcast, headlamp.
- **Colour:** earth tones, desaturated teal-olive.
- **Pacing:** 1–2 s, intensity ramps.
- **Type:** bold condensed caps (Heebo Bold).
- **Sound:** breath, wind, boots on gravel, ice rattle.

**What sells and the traps.** Durability proof (the Stanley tumbler that survived a car fire with ice still inside earned 60 M+ views; [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/viral-video-lands-user-new-car-stanley-tumbler-withstands-fire)), identity, space-saving, and community.
- Weight-loss or results claims need substantiation; no transformations.
- Avoid gender and body-pressure tropes (Peloton's "Gift That Gives Back" backlash cut 10% off the stock; [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2019/12/04/peloton-stock-falls-after-backlash-gift-that-gives-ad)).
- "Waterproof" or "bear-resistant" need ratings or certification.
- Green Guides: no vague "sustainable".

**3 formats (18 s)**
1. **Abuse test.** 0–3 s: the product thrown, dropped or frozen. 3–9 s: three escalating ordeals. 9–13 s: the reveal (ice still rattles). 13–18 s: hero and CTA, plus a "dramatization" label on our concepts.
2. **Dawn mission.** 0–2 s: 4:58 alarm (comp the digits). 2–8 s: lacing up, the door, the trail. 8–13 s: summit wide. 13–18 s: gear macro and logo.
3. **Apartment gym.** 0–3 s: a cramped living room. 3–10 s: the product unfolds and tucks away (gear-only shots). 10–15 s: silhouette workout. 15–18 s: end card.

## 5. PET PRODUCTS: AI score 3 (4 for single-behaviour close-ups)

**Visual grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - dog-eye height (50 cm) through the whole film;
  - the head tilt at a squeak;
  - a nose pushing the box open;
  - the POV from inside the box (BarkBox);
  - paws on a wood floor;
  - a cat's slow blink in a sun patch;
  - the bowl, empty after;
  - the lifestyle sofa.
- **Light:** soft window light, golden backyard.
- **Colour:** warm, playful, one brand colour on toys or packaging.
- **Pacing:** 1.5–2 s, with holds on reactions.
- **Type:** rounded sans, playful.
- **Sound:** tag jingle, squeaker, wrapper crinkle, paws, a happy grunt.

**What sells and the traps.** **The pet's reaction is the proof**, plus health and safety, durability for chewers, and owner guilt or joy.
- Food and treat claims follow AAFCO/FDA rules. "Vet recommended" needs data.
- AI animals are **dramatization**; never present them as real reactions or reviews.
- Never depict unsafe use: unsupervised chews, a dog in a hot car, toxic foods on the table.

**3 formats (18 s)**
1. **Reaction first.** 0–2 s: squeak SFX, head tilt. 2–6 s: zoomies to the box. 6–11 s: the product in play. 11–15 s: tired dog on the bed. 15–18 s: packshot.
2. **Pet-POV world.** The entire film at 50 cm: legs of humans, the door, the treat jar as a monument; the end card is at dog height.
3. **Made for them.** An ad "for dogs" (Bark's squeaker carol): 0–3 s squeaks to the camera; 3–12 s dogs in many homes turn their heads; 12–18 s brand.

**BIG IDEAs (fictional brand NOOK)**
- **"The sound they wait for."** The NOOK jar lid pops. Across the city, at windows and parks, **dogs' ears lift in unison**: a montage of 4 head-turns.
- **"Built at their height."** The house shot from 50 cm: everything is out of reach except the NOOK bed, the only thing lit and in focus.

**Recipe**
- **Assets:** build one **pet sheet** first: Soul Cinema, the same breed, a grey seamless, side/front/3-4 views. It feeds Seedance reference-to-video, because Kling i2v drifts coat patterns.
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Shot on Canon C70 at 50cm height, 35mm f/2, soft window key camera left, warm 4300K, real fur with individual backlit guard hairs, slightly wet nose, a few shed hairs on the fabric, lived-in home with scuffed oak floor, Kodak Portra grain, handheld at pet height. [BAN LIST + no extra legs, no human-like expressions, no cartoon eyes]"
- **Shot plan:**

  | Shot | Still | Motion |
  |---|---|---|

- **Animal realism rules:**
  - one behaviour per clip, 3–4 s;
  - real gaze, no human smiles;
  - paws occluded by a rug or the frame edge;
  - **no eating, drinking or licking on camera**;
  - no hands petting (finger–fur intersections), or a hand only at the frame edge;
  - no two animals interacting.
- **Sound:** tag jingle, squeaker, crinkle (close); paws on wood (mid); kids playing outside (far). VO `[warm]`. Music: "light ukulele and whistle, 100 bpm".

**AI weak spots and workarounds**
- Coat pattern drift: pet sheet plus Seedance refs.
- Leg count and gait: low wides with the legs cropped.
- Mouth–food contact: show before and after only.
- Uncanny "acting" faces: name canine behaviours (ear lift, head tilt, sniff).
- Fur at speed smears: keep motion slow.
- Cats morph more than dogs: use stills plus a slow push.
