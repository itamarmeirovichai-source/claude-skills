# 11 — Beauty niche playbook: fragrance, skincare, makeup, haircare

Adds only what is beauty-specific to 00/02/05/06/09/10.

## Frame study (real ads)
Keyframes of 20 official spots: `research/ai-video-reels/youtube/frames/niches/beauty/mosaic_1-5.jpg`.
- **Fragrance = a world, not a product.** Dior Sauvage Elixir: 2.39, red desert, puma, low sun. YSL Libre: the word burning at dusk plus B&W inserts. YSL MYSLF: grey seamless, dusk sky, head tipped back. Chanel N°5: Malibu daylight, rig in frame. Byredo Blanche: B&W sheets, stencil logo, frame-within-frame. Byredo Bal d'Afrique: tungsten on dark skin, pearls, one red rose. Le Labo: overcast NYC docu. **The bottle often appears for <3 s, at the end.**
- **Skincare = texture plus one number.** The Ordinary: extreme macro of real pores against a yellow wall, one sans line. La Mer: thick cream on a cheek, raking sun, 50+ model, footnoted "91% said skin looks more energized". Rhode: white bathroom, product to lips.
- **Makeup = colour as hero.** Fenty Gloss Bomb: one-colour pink world, lip-macro triptych, shade on several skin tones, red set for HEAT. Charlotte Tilbury: high-key white, "NEW!" sans supers, "SHOP NOW" end card. Glossier You: tight handheld faces, pastel, small italic super.
- **Haircare = movement and proof devices.** Dyson: grey seamless monochrome profiles of tool and result. Olaplex No.3Plus (Mother): red/white "lab", giant "3" tiles. Olaplex "Unretouched": the proof is a promise about the footage itself.
- Higgsfield lip-serum prompt: 29° tele at 4 m for faces, 18° macro-tele for product, match cut "serum mark becomes the round pink cap" ([community](https://higgsfield.ai/seedance-2-5-community)).

## Rules shared across beauty
- **Winning hooks:** Instant Reveal (macro texture payoff in second 1), voiceless use (close-up → use → outcome), ASMR, concern call-out, bold claim; tactility beats polish ([Billo](https://billo.app/blog/tiktok-ad-trends/); [TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/2025-beauty-blog-tiktok-smb)). UGC-style cuts reach ~4× CTR ([Syncly](https://syncly.app/blog/tiktok-influencer-marketing-beauty-brands)); always export a native variant (handheld, captions).
- **Compliance core:**
  - FDA: treating acne, eczema or dandruff, wrinkle removal, hair restoration or lash growth are **drug claims** ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/laws-regulations/cosmetics-us-law)).
  - Meta: cosmetics may show before/after, but not with negative self-perception ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness)).
  - ASA: banned Rimmel for lash inserts and post work that exaggerated the effect, and treats filters that exaggerate a result as misleading ([Cosmetics Business](https://www.cosmeticsbusiness.com/rimmel-mascara-ad-banned-for-misleading-consumers-128287); [Garrigues](https://blogip.garrigues.com/en/advertising/beauty-filters-on-instagram-are-in-the-spotlight-are-they-misleading)).
  - **For us:** AI footage is NEVER a result or demo claim. Show results as **metaphor**; faces/skin are mood only. Label portfolio pieces "fictional AI concept".
- **BAN LIST (every prompt):** no HDR, oversaturation, waxy/poreless skin, beauty retouch, CG spheres/bubbles, reflective-floor float, centred symmetry, generated text/logos, lens flare, slow-mo easing, locked-off camera, extra fingers; product never changes shape.

## 1. FRAGRANCE (AI score 5: our best niche)

**(1) Visual grammar**
- **Shots:** note-world wide · body fragment in emotion · backlit glass macro · mist in backlight · cap detail · hero bottle on a real surface
- **Lenses:** anamorphic 2.39 or full-frame primes; macro at f/11+.
- **Light:** one hard source (low sun, a tungsten slash, fire) and huge blacks.
- **Colour:** ONE world per scent (amber/rust for woods, bone/white for musks, ink/blue for aquatics).
- **Textures:** skin, linen, stone, smoke, fabric.
- **Pacing:** 1.5–3 s, long held breaths before the logo.
- **Type:** wide-tracked serif or a Byredo-style stencil sans, tiny, set in a corner.
- **Sound:** a breath, cloth, the cap's "thock", a score swell, one whispered word.

**(2) What sells**
- **Proof:** **notes made visible** plus a de-risk offer (discovery set/sample). TikTok Shop: $162M fragrance in 2025; #PerfumeTok drives 45% of social-inspired purchases ([Beauty Independent](https://www.beautyindependent.com/top-10-brands-driving-fragrance-sales-tiktok-shop/); [Scento](https://www.scento.com/pl/blog/perfume-tok-trends-driving-niche-fragrance-sales)).
- **Hooks:** a "smells like…" line, an impossible sensory image, the mist in backlight.
- **CTA:** "Discover the set", "Find your scent", "Try it on your skin first".
- **Traps:** no pheromone, attraction or "they'll want you" claims; no "lasts 24 h" without a test; no real-house bottle silhouettes or "dupe" framing.

**(3) Formats (15–20 s)**
- **A. Note Journey (Dior/YSL).** 0–2 s impossible note image + logo bug · 2–6 note 1 macro · 6–9 note 2 · 9–12 body fragment in the world · 12–15 backlit mist · 15–18 hero bottle + name · 18–20 "Discover the set"
- **B. Ritual Close-up (Byredo).** 0–2 s cap lifts in a tungsten slash · 2–5 wrist turns into light · 5–8 spray, mist drifts · 8–12 cloth/skin fragments · 12–15 bottle on linen, fingerprint on glass · 15–18 end card
- **C. Smells-Like List (native).** 0–2 s super "Smells like 6 p.m. in August" · 3 note shots × 3 s with captions · bottle in hand 11 s · sample-set CTA 15 s

**(4) BIG IDEAS (fictional "HALDEN — Figue Brûlée")**
- **"Wear the orchard."** She sprays at a fogged bathroom mirror. Where the mist lands, the reflection becomes a sunlit fig orchard, not the bathroom. Payoff: bottle on the sink, fig leaves in its reflection.
- **"Bottled at 6 p.m."** The amber juice holds a low sun. As the cap closes, the room dims as if the sun set inside (Kling start/end pair).

**(5) Recipe**
- **Routing:**
  - product and glass macros on Flare plus Kling
  - world and body fragments on Soul Cinema, then Seedance 480p→720p
  - the mirror transformation on Kling start+end frames
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Fragrance campaign still, 35mm anamorphic, single hard warm key from low camera-left, deep negative fill, large areas falling to black, one colour world of [amber/rust/bone], Kodak Vision3 250D grain, gentle halation, slightly off-centre on the left third, real dust and a faint fingerprint on the glass. [BAN LIST]"
- **Stills (Flare with the product ref unless noted):**
  1. "@image_1 exact bottle, three-quarter angle, on raw travertine at left third, single snooted tungsten backlight making the juice glow from within, one vertical specular, a fig leaf out of focus in foreground."
  2. "Extreme macro of the bottle shoulder and cap, f/16, micro-scratches, thin sliver of light across the glass, rest black."
  3. (Soul) "Woman's neck and collarbone, head tipped back, eyes closed, late sun raking from right, real skin texture, linen shirt falling off the shoulder, no face fully visible."
  4. "Bottle held in a hand at frame edge, fingers partly occluding the label, backlit spray mist leaving the atomiser into a dark room."
- **Motion (Kling 5 s):**
  1. "Slow slider truck right 10 cm, only the light sliver moves across the glass, bottle locked, no easing."
  2. "Barely-there push-in, the light slash travels from cap to base."
  3. (Seedance) "Handheld, breathing, she exhales and the shirt slips 2 cm; already at full speed, no slow motion."
  4. "Atomiser pressed once at start; mist plume billows into backlight and dissipates; camera static-handheld with micro drift."
- **Sound (ElevenLabs):**
  - Music: "0–2 s low cello drone and breath; 2–12 s sparse piano, warm tape, 68 bpm; 12 s swell; 15 s single sub hit on the bottle; 17–20 s room tone."
  - SFX: glass cap "thock", atomiser hiss, linen rustle, a distant cicada bed.
  - VO (eleven_v3): "[whispers] Figue Brûlée. [pause] Wear the orchard."
- **Edit:**
  - 2.39 letterbox in 9:16 (hero), full-bleed (native).
  - Cut on exhale; dissolve only into the end card.
  - Cormorant Garamond, tracking +200, lower-third left.
  - Film chain with halation up, grain 0.35.
  - 6 s cut: mist, then mirror orchard, then bottle and name.
- **Budget:** 2 ref $0.60 + 4 Flare $1.20 + 4 Soul $0.02 + 4 Kling $1.90 + Seedance 720p 5 s $2.30 + retakes $3 ≈ **$9**.

**(6) AI limits**
- Finger on atomiser morphs: hand half off-frame, start mid-spray. Embossed name: blank it, comp in post. Juice level/colour drift: lock "liquid fills 80%, amber". Over-dense mist: "thin, uneven plume".

## 2. SKINCARE (AI score 4 for product/texture, 2 for faces)

**(1) Visual grammar**
- **Shots:** texture swatch · hanging dropper drop · fingertip spread on hand · pore-level skin macro · daylight shelf · ingredient still life · packshot with a number
- **Light:** soft window daylight 5600 K or raking sun; avoid sterile white.
- **Colour:** clean neutrals plus one accent wall (yellow, sage, blue sky).
- **Textures:** wet, glossy, translucent; condensation; terrycloth.
- **Pacing:** slower (2–3 s); ASMR.
- **Type:** clean sans (Heebo) for claims, with the footnote visible.
- **Sound:** squish, pump, dropper "plip", tap water, skin pat.

**(2) What sells**
- **Proof:** texture, ingredient % on pack, footnoted perception stat (La Mer), "unretouched" honesty, dermatologist tested.
- **Hooks:**
  - a concern call-out ("Tight skin by noon?")
  - texture ASMR in the first second
  - "Every last drop" (The Ordinary)
- **CTA:** "Shop the routine", "Find your regimen", "Free sample with first order".
- **Traps:**
  - say "improves the appearance of"; never "treats acne", "repairs", "heals", "anti-ageing results";
  - stats must be real and footnoted (fictional brands: none).

**(3) Formats**
- **A. Instant Reveal.** 0–1.5 s serum drop lands · 1.5–5 spread on hand, gloss catches window · 5–8 ingredient still life · 8–12 BIG IDEA · 12–15 packshot + ingredient % · 15–18 CTA
- **B. Morning Ritual POV.** 0–2 s tap, cupped hands · 2–5 pump · 5–8 pat on jaw (below eyes) · 8–11 bottle on sunlit sink · 11–15 mirror steam clears on logo · 15–17 CTA
- **C. Ingredient Explainer.** 0–2 s super "3 ingredients. Nothing else." · 3 ingredient macros × 3 s · formula drop 11 s · packshot with % 14 s · CTA

**(4) BIG IDEAS (fictional "SOLENNE Barrier Serum")**
- **"Holds water like a leaf holds dew."** A serum bead on the back of a hand is match-cut to an identical bead of dew on a fig leaf at dawn, and back. Hydration made visible, no face, no result claim.
- **"The 6 a.m. drought."** A macro of cracked pale clay. A single drop falls, and the cracks darken and close outward in a ring (Kling start: dry, end: moist). Cut to the same drop on skin.

**(5) Recipe**
- **Routing:**
  - textures and bottle on Flare plus Kling (best macro)
  - hands and bathroom on Soul plus Seedance
  - the clay metaphor on Kling start/end frames
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Skincare campaign still, 100mm macro, soft north-window daylight 5600 K from camera-left with visible falloff, white foam-core bounce, palette of bone, sage and [accent], clean but lived-in bathroom, water spots on the ceramic, real skin texture with pores and fine vellus hair, fine film grain, off-centre composition. [BAN LIST]"
- **Stills:**
  1. (Flare) "@image_1 exact dropper bottle on a wet ceramic sink edge at right third, drop hanging from the pipette, window light behind making the serum translucent amber."
  2. (Soul) "Back of a hand, real knuckle creases, a glossy bead of clear serum sitting on the skin, fingertip entering from frame edge."
  3. (Soul) "Fig leaf at dawn, a single round dew drop, backlit, shallow focus, cold blue morning."
  4. (Soul) "Macro of cracked pale clay riverbed, raking low sun, dust."
- **Motion:**
  1. "Drop releases from the pipette and lands out of frame; camera micro-drift; no slow-motion easing."
  2. (Seedance) "Fingertip spreads the bead in two small circles; skin moves naturally; gloss catches the window."
  3. "Dew drop trembles and rolls 1 cm along the vein; leaf sways."
  4. (Kling start/end) "Drop falls into the centre; moisture darkens the clay outward in a ring; cracks narrow; camera static-handheld."
- **Sound:**
  - Music: "airy, 72 bpm, felt piano and soft pads; 0–2 s silence plus the plip; 8 s a gentle rise; 14 s resolve."
  - SFX: dropper plip, pump squish, water tap, cloth pat.
  - VO: "[softly] Water, held. [pause] SOLENNE Barrier Serum."
- **Edit:**
  - Match cuts on the circular bead.
  - Heebo light captions, with any stat footnote at 8 pt.
  - Low grain (0.2) so texture reads; no halation on skin.
  - 6 s cut: drop, leaf, packshot.
- **Budget:** ≈ $8.50 (2 ref + 2 Flare $1.20, 6 Soul $0.03, 4 Kling $1.90, 1 Seedance 720p $2.30, retakes $3).

**(6) AI limits**
- Waxy skin: macro fragments + "visible pores, vellus hair, uneven tone". CG-frosting cream: "air bubbles, uneven peaks, matte edges". Faces read as AI and fake results: avoid. Pumps morph: press off-frame.

## 3. MAKEUP (AI score 3)

**(1) Visual grammar**
- **Shots:** extreme lip macro · twist-up · swatch smear · compact click + powder cloud · product array · shade on many skin tones · night-out moment
- **Light:** hard beauty dish or ring for gloss specular, or a party practical (flash, neon) for the wear story.
- **Colour:** a **one-colour world matched to the shade** (Fenty pink, HEAT deep red). Background, wardrobe and props follow the swatch.
- **Textures:** gloss, glitter, crushed powder, velvet.
- **Pacing:** fast, 1–1.5 s cuts, beat-synced.
- **Type:** bold sans for supers (Charlotte Tilbury "NEW!") or a tiny luxe serif.
- **Sound:** the click, twist, swipe, crushed powder, a party bass bed.

**(2) What sells**
- **Proof:** swatches across skin tones (Fenty 50 shades, [Hypebae](https://hypebae.com/2019/8/rihanna-fenty-beauty-pro-filtr-hydrating-foundation-50-shades-primer-kabuki-brush-price-release-date)), a wear test ("still there at 2 a.m."), a kiss-print test on glass, finish close-ups.
- **Hooks:**
  - the satisfying swatch or crush in the first second
  - transformation
  - a "this shade on every skin" grid
- **CTA:** "Find your shade", "Shop the shade", "New: [name]".
- **Traps:**
  - no lash inserts or post-enhanced effects on the claimed area (ASA Rimmel);
  - AI "wear tests" are fabricated demos: stylised metaphor only;
  - shade colour must match the real product.

**(3) Formats**
- **A. Swatch ASMR.** 0–1 s crush/smear · 1–4 three swatches · 4–8 twist-up + click · 8–12 lips macro · 12–15 shade-world packshot · 15–17 CTA
- **B. Night-Out Wear Test.** 0–2 s "8 p.m." lips · clock-super hard cuts: glass print, dance flash, taxi neon, "2 a.m." mirror · BIG IDEA 12 s · packshot 15 s
- **C. One Shade, Every Skin.** 6 × 1.5 s wrist macros across skin tones, bullet passed hand to hand · array 10 s · "Find your shade" 14 s

**(4) BIG IDEAS (fictional "MAUVE ROOM — Stay Lip")**
- **"Survives the night."** At 4 a.m. a party room is wrecked (fallen balloons, spilled ice, a bent candle). The only thing still perfect is a crisp lip print on a champagne coupe, the bullet standing beside it.
- **"Colour you can stand in."** A pure-colour world: the swatch smear on glass widens until the camera pushes INTO it and the frame becomes the shade's room, where the bullet stands on a velvet plinth.

**(5) Recipe**
- **Routing:**
  - bullets, compacts and arrays on Flare plus Kling
  - lips, hands and party on Soul plus Seedance (multi-shot hard cuts in one prompt, 480p draft)
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Makeup campaign still, 90mm macro, hard gridded key from above-left with crisp specular on gloss, deep [shade-name hex] colour world in walls, props and fabric, flash-on-camera party realism where noted, real lip lines and texture, fine glitter fallout, 35mm grain, off-centre. [BAN LIST]"
- **Stills:**
  1. (Flare) "@image_1 exact lipstick bullet twisted fully up, lying on crushed velvet in [hex], one hard specular down the bullet, flecks of broken pigment around."
  2. (Flare) "Smear of @image_1 shade across clear glass, thick at the start feathering to dry streaks, backlit, colour accurate to swatch [hex]."
  3. (Soul) "Champagne coupe on a wrecked party table at 4 a.m., crisp mauve lip print on the rim, spilled ice, bent candle, on-camera flash, harsh shadow."
  4. (Soul) "Extreme macro of lips wearing a satin mauve colour, real lip lines, tiny imperfection at the cupid's bow, no face above the nose."
- **Motion:**
  1. "Bullet slowly rotates 20° on its side; only the specular travels."
  2. "Camera pushes into the smear until the colour fills frame; no easing."
  3. (Seedance) "Handheld flash photographer energy, two flash pops, slight exposure pumping, camera settles on the coupe."
  4. (Seedance) "Lips press together once and part; natural, at full speed."
- **Sound:**
  - Music: "dark disco 118 bpm; 0–1 s dry swatch sound; 4 s kick in; 12 s filter-down to room tone, ears ringing; 14 s sub hit plus bell."
  - SFX: compact click, bullet twist, glass clink, flash pop, ice melt.
  - VO: "[confident, low] Still here. [pause] MAUVE ROOM."
- **Edit:**
  - Cut every 1–1.25 s on the kick.
  - Clock supers in Heebo bold.
  - Push the colour world but **protect the swatch hex** (check ΔE on the swatch frame).
  - Film chain light.
  - 6 s cut: smear, coupe, bullet.
- **Budget:** ≈ $9 (2 ref + 3 Flare $1.50, 6 Soul $0.03, 3 Kling $1.43, Seedance 480p 15 s multi-shot $3.15, retakes $2.90).

**(6) AI limits**
- Applicator-on-lip strokes morph: show the press, not the stroke. Shade drifts: lock hex, correct in post. Glitter flickers: keep it defocused. Lashes mangle: avoid lash products. Skin tones homogenise: generate each hand separately.

## 4. HAIRCARE (AI score 3; hair motion is now usable in Seedance)

**(1) Visual grammar**
- **Shots:** swish (back/profile) · strand macro with shine band · pump into palm · fingers combing · wet hair in shower · tool-in-hand profile · bottle with condensation
- **Light:** a hard backlight or edge light for **the shine band** (the money shot), or grey-seamless monochrome (Dyson).
- **Colour:** neutral greys or warm amber oil; or a brand lab colour (Olaplex red/white).
- **Textures:** oil viscosity, wet strands, steam, towel.
- **Pacing:** medium, with slow-mo only on the swish.
- **Type:** clinical sans with numbered claims ("3 bonds, 3 minutes").
- **Sound:** a hair whoosh, pump, water, the hiss of brush strokes.

**(2) What sells**
- **Proof:** shine band, strand stretch test, "unretouched" honesty, real before/after, stylist authority.
- **Hooks:**
  - the swish in the first second
  - the frizz silhouette against a backlight
  - a "3 minutes" number hook
- **CTA:** "Shop the treatment", "Build your routine".
- **Traps:**
  - growth, hair-loss, thinning and dandruff claims are drug claims ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/laws-regulations/cosmetics-us-law));
  - keep to "smoother-looking, shinier, less breakage from styling";
  - disclose extensions if implied as a result.

**(3) Formats**
- **A. Shine Band.** 0–1.5 s backlit swish · 1.5–4 pump into palm · 4–7 fingers through wet lengths · 7–10 strand macro, light band travels · 10–13 BIG IDEA · 13–16 bottle in steam · 16–18 CTA
- **B. Lab Numbers (Olaplex).** 0–2 s super "3 min" · apply/wait/rinse, 2 s each with number cards · strand metaphor 9 s · packshot 13 s
- **C. Monochrome Demo (Dyson).** grey seamless profile · hard cuts straight/wave/sleek, 2–3 s each · product hero · end card

**(4) BIG IDEAS (fictional "ORRIS BOND Oil")**
- **"Hangs by a thread."** The full glass bottle is suspended from a single glossy strand of hair, swinging gently in a backlight like a pendulum, with no break. Strength made literal (metaphor, not a test claim).
- **"Liquid silk."** A pour of amber oil falls in slow motion and match-cuts into a ponytail swinging in the same arc and colour, then back to the oil settling in the bottle.

**(5) Recipe**
- **Routing:**
  - bottle, oil and the suspended strand on Flare plus Kling (start/end for the pendulum)
  - swish, wet hair and fingers on Soul plus Seedance 720p (best hair physics)
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Haircare campaign still, 50mm and 100mm macro, hard backlight from behind subject creating a bright shine band on hair, dark warm-grey background with falloff, amber and graphite palette, real flyaways and baby hairs, damp-ends realism, steam haze, 35mm grain, off-centre. [BAN LIST + no plastic hair, no helmet hair, no fused strands]"
- **Stills:**
  1. (Flare) "@image_1 exact amber bottle hanging mid-air from one glossy dark hair strand tied at the neck, strand catching a backlight, real cast shadow on the grey sweep."
  2. (Soul) "Back of a woman's head, long dark hair mid-swing in profile, hard backlight, flyaways glowing, face turned away."
  3. (Soul) "Hands working oil through wet lengths in a steamy tiled shower, water droplets, window light."
  4. (Flare) "Macro of amber oil pouring from @image_1's dropper in a thick ribbon, backlit, viscous, a few air bubbles."
- **Motion:**
  1. (Kling start/end) "Bottle swings slowly like a pendulum, small arc; strand stays taut and intact; shadow follows."
  2. (Seedance) "Hair swings once left to right and settles with natural bounce, flyaways trail; already at full speed then a single 50% ramp in post."
  3. (Seedance) "Fingers comb downward twice, strands separate and re-group, water drips."
  4. "Oil ribbon flows continuously, slight coil at the landing; camera static-handheld; under 3 s used."
- **Sound:**
  - Music: "warm minimal R&B 84 bpm; 0–1.5 s whoosh plus swell; 7 s bass in; 13 s breath; 16 s sonic logo (two-note harp)."
  - SFX: hair whoosh layered close and far, pump, shower patter, oil drip.
  - VO: "[softly, assured] Strength you can see. [pause] ORRIS BOND."
- **Edit:**
  - The speed ramp on the swish (100→40→100%) is the signature.
  - Match cut oil arc to hair arc.
  - Heebo numbers for any routine steps.
  - Halation is fine on the shine band.
  - 6 s cut: pendulum, swish, bottle.
- **Budget:** ≈ $9.60 (2 ref + 3 Flare $1.50, 3 Soul $0.02, 3 Kling $1.43, 2 Seedance 720p 5 s $4.60, retakes $2).

**(6) AI limits**
- Strands fuse or clip through hands: hands at edge, backlit silhouettes. Combing goes rubbery: 1–2 strokes. Thin strand vanishes: thicken it in the still, check every frame. Faces drift: back/profile only. Before/after: never fake it.
