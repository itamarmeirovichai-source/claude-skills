# What makes a short vertical ad SELL (fundamentals brief)

Research brief (2026-10-07). Sources are inline. Apply it together with `../creative-brain.md`.

## 1. Strategy: decide before writing anything
- **Awareness level (Schwartz).** Open on what the viewer already knows ([OptimizeSmart](https://optimizesmart.com/blog/schwartz-five-stages-of-awareness-in-marketing/)):
  - Unaware: a situation they recognise.
  - Problem-aware: a fix exists.
  - Solution-aware: why this one.
  - Product-aware: proof or comparison.
  - Most-aware: offer and deadline.

  Cold Reels/TikTok traffic is mostly unaware or problem-aware, so put the offer only in the last 3 s.
- **Market sophistication.** Crowded categories need a **unique mechanism**: a visible "how" that the camera can show ("cold-pressed at 4 °C", "11 stitches/inch") ([Inceptly](https://inceptly-agency.beehiiv.com/p/secrets-advertising-greats-cheat-sheet-copywriting-mastery-part-1)). Premium categories sit at level 3–5.
- **Jobs-to-be-done / category entry points (Sharp).** Write: "When [situation], I want [progress], so I can [feeling]". Open on that situation ([Think Insights](https://thinkinsights.net/strategy/byron-sharps-mental-availability)). Desire (identity, status, pleasure) sells premium; need (pain) sells DR.
- **Value equation (Hormozi):** (Dream outcome × Likelihood) / (Time × Effort). Every beat should make the outcome bigger, more believable, faster or easier. Luxury replaces the offer with access ("numbered edition").
- **Brand vs activation (Binet & Field, 996 IPA cases):** emotional campaigns are about 2× likelier to deliver top-box profit; split roughly 60/40 brand/activation ([summary](https://www.alexmurrell.co.uk/summaries/les-binet-and-peter-field-the-long-and-the-short-of-it)). Make both an emotional brand cut and an offer cut with the same assets.

## 2. Persuasion, used honestly
- **Specificity (Hopkins):** numbers and processes instead of adjectives ("48-hour cold infusion", not "luxurious").
- **Ogilvy:** respect the consumer, give facts, and own one odd signature device (the Hathaway eyepatch).
- **Cialdini's seven principles:** reciprocity, liking, authority, social proof, consistency, scarcity, unity. **Unity** ("people like us") is the premium lever: show the tribe, don't claim it ([CXL](https://conversionxl.com/cialdini-unity/)).
- **Behavioural economics:**
  - Anchor before the reveal (show the café price first).
  - One emotional **peak**, and the brand on the **end** frame (peak-end rule).
  - Loss framing.
- **Right-brain creative (Orlando Wood, *Lemon*):** scenes, characters, place and implicit humour beat VO walls, text slabs and stock music.

## 3. Structures for 15–30 s
| Framework | 20 s beat map | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Hook–Body–CTA (TikTok) | 0–3 hook + brand / 3–16 USP + demo / 16–20 CTA | default |
| PAS | problem / agitate / product + proof / CTA | problem-aware DR |
| Before/After/Bridge | before → after → product bridge | transformations |
| AIDA | attention / interest / desire / action | brand cuts |
| Google ABCD (+30% short-term sales likelihood) | QA lens: Attract, Brand, Connect, Direct | all |

- **Show, don't tell:** one claim per ad, proven on camera by a demo or mechanism close-up. Never fake a result.
- **Objection:** name the top one (price, efficacy, effort, "not for me") and answer it in one visual beat.
- **Urgency and scarcity** only when they are real.
- **Ethical proof:** the FTC 2024 rule bans AI and fictional testimonials, ratings and customer counts ([Hunton](https://hunton.com/hunton-retail-law-resource/ftc-issues-final-rule-targeting-fake-consumer-reviews-and-testimonials-including-those-generated-by-artificial-intelligence-ai-and-online-bots)). Use these instead:
  - Proof as a **scene** (a queue, a gift being received).
  - **Craft authority** (the maker's hands).
  - **Unity**.
  - Label the piece as AI.

## 4. Premium and luxury sell differently
Kapferer's anti-laws: luxury isn't comparative, advertising isn't there to sell, and luxury sets the price. Show material, craft, time and sensory contact ([The Fashion Law](https://www.thefashionlaw.com/the-luxury-strategy-the-24-anti-laws-of-marketing/)).

| Lever | Mass DR | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | problem or pattern-interrupt | sensory moment, mystery, beauty |
| Proof | demo, numbers | craft, process, materials |
| Pace | fast, dense text | slower, negative space, one line |
| Social proof | reviews, UGC | tribe, setting |
| Offer | discount, bundle | access, edition, ritual, "discover" |
| Ritual | "easy, 1 step" | the ritual *is* the product |

## 5. Brand recognition
- **Brand early.** Meta's study of 759 ads found brand-in-the-first-seconds, ~20 s, sound-off-ready ads scored higher on recall ([SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebooks-published-a-new-study-which-highlights-effective-mobile-video-te/512753)). ABCD says brand within 5 s and throughout.
- **Distinctive assets beat logos.** In Ipsos' 2,000+ ads, owned characters and **sonic cues** were strongest; sonic cues were 8.53× more frequent in top ads ([Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-sg/power-you-why-distinctive-brand-assets-are-driving-force-creative-effectiveness)).
- **Asset kit for every brand:**
  - signature colour + secondary
  - one typeface
  - 1–2 s sonic logo + recurring sound motif
  - signature gesture or prop
  - fixed end-frame layout
- **Timing:** product or colour by 0–2 s, sonic cue in the first 3 s and at the end, logo legible by 3 s and on the last frame.

## 6. Platform mechanics
- 9:16, ≥720p, sound on, 5–10 words/s on screen, proposition within 3 s ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)).
- Captions, because it must work muted.
- Safe zones: bottom 35%, top 14%, sides 6%. Breaking them cost 28% CTR.
- Meta Andromeda: 8–15 *really different* concepts (persona × angle × offer). Test hooks on a locked body.

## 7. The 15 pre-production gates
1. Awareness level named; the opening matches it.
2. A job-to-be-done or entry point, written as a situation, is the opening scene.
3. Single-minded claim, ≤ 7 words.
4. A filmable mechanism or reason-why (number, process, material).
5. Hook in 0–2 s that works muted and with sound.
6. Brand by 3 s; branded end frame.
7. Asset kit applied (colour, type, sonic logo, signature device).
8. Show, don't tell: the story works without VO.
9. Top objection answered in one beat.
10. Value levers visible (bigger, likelier, faster or easier).
11. One emotional peak, and the brand on the final frame.
12. CTA fits the tier; urgency is real.
13. Ethical proof: no fake reviews or testimonials; AI label.
14. Platform spec: 9:16, 15–30 s, safe zones, captions, ≤10 words/s.
15. Variant set differs by persona, angle or offer, plus 3 hooks on a locked body.
