# Niche playbook: watches & jewelry, sneakers & streetwear, luxury leather, eyewear

Builds on 00 (BIG IDEA rule, 10-step pipeline), 05 (DP vocabulary), 06 (anti-AI), 09/10 (Higgsfield). Researched 2026-10-07. Every brand below is FICTIONAL. Clear each name on USPTO before publishing, and never put a real mark, monogram or signature shape in a prompt.

**Cross-niche law.**
- One product per shot, sitting in **one material world**.
- Hands or bodies appear but faces are averted, and the product is often partly hidden.
- Near-static camera; the motion comes from light: a slash, a glint, a flicker.
- Type is tiny: a serif or a wide-tracked sans.
- The logo arrives only at the end.
- The macro is the proof.

## 1. WATCHES & JEWELRY

**(1) Visual grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - a crown or clasp macro
  - a dial ECU with a grazing key
  - a cuff-pull wrist insert (Cartier)
  - neck or collarbone macro (Tiffany)
  - a ring on a textured plinth (Dior)
  - an exploded view whose parts cast shadows
  - a box hinge opening
  - a 10:10 hero on black
- **Framing.** Off-centre, with the product filling about 30–40% of the frame. Fingers at the edge give scale.
- **Light.**
  - Metal needs **big soft sources reflected** plus one hard specular. Facets need a small hard point source to scintillate.
  - Watches are ALWAYS shown at 10:10. The hands frame the logo, don't cover subdials and read as a smile ([Gear Patrol](https://gearpatrol.com/watches/a42447598/why-are-watch-hands-set-to-1010-in-photos/)).
- **Colour worlds:**
  - moss + gold
  - night teal
  - steel-blue desaturated (for a dive watch)
  - oxblood velvet
- **Texture.** Brushed steel, sapphire dust, skin pores, velvet nap.
- **Pacing.** 1.5–3 s per shot, with the glint landing on a beat.
- **Type.** Cormorant Garamond small caps, tracked +150, 2–3% of frame height.
- **Sound.** Movement tick (28,800 vph = 8 beats/s), a crown ratchet, a clasp click, a hinge creak, a felt thump.

**(2) Why they buy, and the traps**
- **Why they buy:**
  - milestones and gifting (Tiffany "Love is a gift")
  - heritage and permanence (Patek "Generations": you never own it, you look after it)
  - self-purchase as a reward
- **Main objection:** "real?" Answer it with a material macro and a stated spec.
- **FTC Jewelry Guides, 16 CFR 23** ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-23), [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/all-that-glitters-is-not-gold-ftc-updates-jewelry-guides)):
  - Don't call anything under 10K "gold".
  - The karat must be accurate.
  - "Gold-filled", "plated" and "vermeil" must be disclosed.
  - Lab-grown stones need "laboratory-grown/-created" *with equal conspicuousness* when "cultured" is used.
  - Disclose treated pearls and gems.
  - Use correct varietal names.
- **Watches:**
  - Don't say "diver's" unless it meets ISO 6425 (≥100 m plus its other criteria) ([ISO 6425](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_Resistant_mark)).
  - Never say "waterproof". Use "water-resistant to X m".
  - "Chronometer" implies certification; "Swiss Made" has legal criteria.
- **Trade dress.** Do not imitate a crown logo, a Tank rectangle with Roman numerals, a Panthère, the Tiffany robin's-egg blue box, the Bvlgari Serpenti or Cartier Love screws. The divewatch community clip showed an IWC logo, so screen every output.

**(3) Formats (15–20 s)**
- **A. "Light finds it" macro reveal**
  - 0–2: black frame; a hard slash sweeps on and the facet glints (SFX ting).
  - 2–5: dial ECU at 10:10, slow push.
  - 5–8: crown or clasp macro, fingers at the edge.
  - 8–11: wrist or neck insert, face cropped.
  - 11–14: BIG IDEA shot.
  - 14–18: hero off-centre, then the logo and CTA.
- **B. "The gift" (box POV)**
  - 0–2: hands cup a box; the lid hinge creaks.
  - 2–4: inside, a velvet glow.
  - 4–7: the piece lifts out on a fingertip.
  - 7–10: clasped on the neck or wrist.
  - 10–13: reaction shown only through body (a hand to the collarbone).
  - 13–17: piece plus line ("For the first one").
  - 17–20: end card.
- **C. "Exploded movement" (tech-as-luxury)**
  - 0–2: hero.
  - 2–6: the case lifts apart into floating parts with shadows on the backdrop.
  - 6–9: bezel and lume macro.
  - 9–12: parts reassemble.
  - 12–15: on-wrist, underwater or in the field.
  - 15–18: spec super ("200 m. Sapphire.") and logo.

**(4) BIG IDEAS**
- **AUREN (dive watch), "Silence at 200 m."**
  - A half-over/half-under split shot: the surface chop is loud above while the dial below sits in total stillness, the seconds hand the only motion. The sound drops to nothing at the waterline.
  - Pay it off with the same split at the end card.
- **SÉLAH (gold chain), "Every link is a hand."**
  - Hands of three generations interlock, then match-cut to a macro of three gold links in the same pose.
  - The claim of heirloom craft becomes literally visible.

**(5) Recipe**

| Shot | Still | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| Dial ECU, crown macro, ring on plinth, hero | Flare + product ref | Kling 3.0 Pro (start = end frame) |
| Hands, neck crop, box POV | Soul Cinema (no ref), product comped or Flare edit | Kling |
| Exploded view, underwater split | Flare | Seedance 2.5 image-to-video 720p, 5 s |

**STYLE HEADER (watch/jewelry):** "Real tabletop insert shot on ARRI Alexa 35, 100 mm macro at f/11, deep focus on metal, everything else falls to black. Single hard 3200 K key raking from frame-left at 15°, one large diffusion card reflected in the polished surfaces, negative fill right. Off-centre composition, product on the right third. Fine dust on sapphire, micro-scratches on the bezel, a faint fingerprint on the caseback. Kodak Vision3 250D grain, gentle halation on speculars, muted grade. No HDR, no oversaturation, no CGI gloss, no symmetry, no floating render, no added text, no logos, no numerals beyond what is in @image_1."

Stills, each followed by its matching motion prompt:
1. **Still (Flare, ref = watch sheet):** "[HEADER] Watch from @image_1 lying on brushed grey slate, hands at exactly 10:10, three-quarter angle from 30° above, dial occupying the right 40% of frame."
   - **Kling motion:** "Barely perceptible slider push-in toward the dial, 3% over 5 s, already moving at the first frame. The dial, hands and indices are a fixed printed surface and never change. Only the light moves: a thin highlight crawls across the bezel. No slow motion, no camera shake, the watch does not rotate."
2. **Still:** "[HEADER] Extreme macro of the crown and its knurling, two fingertips with visible ridges pinching it at frame-left, out of focus."
   - **Kling motion:** "The fingers turn the crown a quarter-turn in two small ratchet steps at real speed. The camera is locked to the case with micro handheld breathing. Nothing else moves."
3. **Still (Soul Cinema):** "Woman's neck and collarbone in three-quarter profile, face cropped above the jaw, unretouched skin with fine down hair, 85 mm at f/2.8, warm window light from the left, dark teal wall."
   - Comp the chain with a Flare edit (ref = chain) before the motion pass.
   - **Kling motion:** "She breathes in slowly; the collarbone rises and the gold links catch one glint. The camera drifts 2 cm left, handheld. No face is revealed."
4. **Still (Flare):** "[HEADER] Ring from @image_1 standing on a moss-covered stone ledge, the lower third, candle bokeh far behind."
   - **Kling motion:** "A slow 5° arc around the ring and a rack focus from the moss to the stone. The ring geometry, stone count and prongs stay fixed."
5. **BIG IDEA (Seedance, 2 refs):** "@image_1 is the exact watch, @image_2 the split-water composition. A half-underwater split frame: storm chop and white noise above the waterline, still blue silence below. The seconds hand moves in steady ticks. Shot through a dome port, waterline meniscus across the frame, light caustics on the case. No fish, no bubbles near the dial, the dial text never changes."

**Dial and text morphing workarounds:**
- (a) Design the fictional dial **without words**: baton indices plus a simple glyph. Add the wordmark in post as an overlay tracked to the push (ffmpeg `zoompan` with the same scale curve).
- (b) Kling `last_image_url` = the same still, which pins the geometry.
- (c) Use only 3 s of each take; morphs appear late.
- (d) Lock the hands at 10:10 and let the second hand move *only* in the BIG IDEA shot, and cut if it drifts.
- (e) Jewelry: pick bezel-set or solitaire stones and chunky links. Avoid pavé, which melts.
- (f) Add sparkle in post as a 4-point star PNG on 1–3 highlights, fading over 8 frames (the Dior convention) rather than prompting "sparkle".

**ElevenLabs:**
- Music, "Solo felt piano and low cello, 72 BPM":
  - 0–3 s near-silence with a room tone
  - 3 s single low note on the first glint
  - 11 s a swell
  - 15 s cut to silence for 0.5 s before the logo
- SFX: "mechanical watch ticking 8 beats per second, close-mic"; "crown winding ratchet"; "jewelry box hinge creak and felt thump"; "underwater muffled pressure hum".
- VO (eleven_v3): `[whispers] Silence… at two hundred metres.` Low, dry, close.

**Edit:**
- 6–7 shots, punch-ins from 100% to 108%.
- The star glint lands on the music hit.
- Type: Cormorant small caps.
- Film chain at half grain strength, because metal shows noise.
- 6 s cutdown = glint → dial → BIG IDEA → logo.

**Budget:**
- 2 ref stills $0.60
- 6 Flare $1.80
- 5 Kling $2.38
- 1 Seedance 720p 5 s $2.30
- retakes $2.50
- Total ≈ **$9.60**

**(6) AI weak spots.** Numerals, logos and hand positions morph. Stone counts and prongs drift. Bracelets fuse with skin. "Sparkle" renders as glitter noise. Chains defy gravity. Fixes: the workarounds above. Keep chains static on skin, and add gravity in the prompt ("chain hangs straight, heavy").

## 2. SNEAKERS & STREETWEAR

**(1) Visual grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - a silhouette opener (Superfly)
  - a box-lid lift with tissue
  - lace cinch macro
  - sole compressing at ground level
  - a snorricam locked to the shoe with the city whipping past
  - an on-feet wide in a real neighbourhood
  - a colourway turntable
  - a mirrored-pair end card
- **Framing.** Ground-level 24 mm, worm's-eye, the shoe filling the lower half.
- **Light.** Hard sun with long shadows, sodium night, or duotone gels (amber/blue) for the product poem.
- **Colour.** One colourway drives the grade: a grey world for a grey shoe (New Balance "Grey Days", DP Norm Li; [AOTW](https://adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/grey-days)).
- **Texture.** Suede nap, mesh weave, gum-sole bubbles, scuffs, creases at the toe box.
- **Pacing.** 0.8–1.7 s per shot (On SS24 is about 1.7 s), cut on the kick drum.
- **Type.** Heebo Black uppercase, tight, or a tiny wide-tracked sans for the premium route.
- **Sound.** Rubber squeak, lace zip, box cardboard, a footstep on concrete, crowd ambience.

**(2) Why they buy, and the traps**
- **Why they buy:**
  - identity and subculture (NB "We Got Now", [Campaign](https://campaignlive.com/article/ad-week-new-balances-we-now/1715921))
  - scarcity and the drop
  - comfort shown by compression
  - the colourway
- **Objections:** sizing and comfort. Answer with a compression macro and an on-feet walk.
- **Traps:**
  - Fake scarcity or countdowns, and "sold out" when it isn't, are FTC deception and dark patterns.
  - Performance numbers ("+15% energy return") need tests.
  - "Made in USA" must be all or virtually all made there ([FTC rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/made-usa-labeling-rule)).
- **Trade dress (never generate):** three parallel stripes, a swoosh-like tick, a jumping silhouette, an "N", Vans-style side wave, Converse-style toe cap and midsole stripe, red soles. Give the fictional brand a **non-stripe signature**, such as a stitched heel tab in an odd colour or a sole notch.

**(3) Formats**
- **A. Drop reveal**
  - 0–1.5: a box falls into frame and slams (SFX).
  - 1.5–3: the lid lifts and the tissue rustles.
  - 3–6: macro chain: suede, lace, heel tab.
  - 6–9: lace cinch and step-in.
  - 9–13: snorricam on-feet through the city.
  - 13–16: BIG IDEA.
  - 16–18: date, "Fri 10AM" and logo.
- **B. Product poem (Superfly)**
  - 0–2: silhouette against a glow.
  - 2–8: 4 macros sliding past a light streak.
  - 8–11: an abstract colour wipe.
  - 11–14: sole compress.
  - 14–17: mirrored pair, logo.
- **C. Neighbourhood portrait (Grey Days / NB)**
  - 0–2: wide grey street, the shoe walking in.
  - 2–10: four 2 s vignettes of crew members, feet and hands only, no faces to camera.
  - 10–14: the shoe on a curb in rain.
  - 14–17: line plus logo.

**(4) BIG IDEAS**
- **GRAYFIELD (runner), "Concrete, softened."** At the footstrike the pavement itself ripples outward like foam, with cracked asphalt rippling in rings. The comfort claim is made visible.
- **GRAYFIELD (court shoe), "Laced into the city."** One lace pull, and the night street's light trails cinch into parallel lines (long-exposure streaks, Superfly-style) that resolve into the stitch line of the shoe.

**(5) Recipe**
- Product macros: Flare (ref = shoe sheet, side + three-quarter) → Kling.
- Snorricam and on-feet: Seedance 2.5 reference-to-video, refs = shoe + location from Soul Cinema.
- Ripple BIG IDEA: Seedance 720p.

**STYLE HEADER:** "Streetwear campaign shot on 35 mm film, Kodak Portra 400 pushed one stop, 24 mm lens at ground level. Hard late-afternoon sun 4800 K raking low from the left, long shadows, slight lens flare. A real worn neighbourhood: cracked asphalt, chalk marks, a chain-link fence, wet gutter. Shoe from @image_1 exact: panel count, stitching, lace count, sole pattern. Creases on the toe box, a scuff on the heel. No logos, no stripes, no text, no glossy CGI, no symmetric framing."

Stills, each followed by its matching motion prompt:
1. **Still:** "[HEADER] The shoe on a cracked curb, three-quarter rear view, heel tab on the left third, puddle reflection."
   - **Kling motion:** "Low slider arc of 8° left to right at real speed. A pigeon shadow crosses. The shoe is perfectly still."
2. **Still:** "[HEADER] Macro of a hand pulling the laces tight, knuckles at frame-right, eyelets sharp."
   - **Kling motion:** "One fast pull, the laces snap taut and the tongue flexes slightly. No slow motion. Camera locked on the eyelets with micro shake."
3. **Snorricam (Seedance):** "@image_1 shoe, @image_2 street. The camera is rigidly bolted to the side of the right shoe at ankle height; the shoe stays fixed in frame while the pavement and kerbs whip past with motion blur, real walking cadence, slight vertical bob. No feet sliding, the sole plants flat."
4. **BIG IDEA (Seedance):** "Ground-level 24 mm. The shoe lands on asphalt at full speed and the asphalt ripples outward in three concentric rings like compressed foam, then settles flat. Dust lifts. The shoe geometry never changes. Real daylight, documentary handheld."
5. **End (Flare):** "[HEADER minus street] The pair, mirrored, toes in, on black seamless, single amber backlight rimming the silhouettes."
   - **Kling motion:** "The backlight swells slowly, the rims brighten. Nothing else moves."

**ElevenLabs:**
- Music, "dusty boom-bap 92 BPM with a sub drop at 13 s"; or a UK-garage shuffle for the drop format.
- SFX: "sneaker squeak on gym floor", "cardboard shoe box lid", "tissue paper rustle", "lace zip pull".

**Edit:**

- One 2-frame flash-frame of the colourway.
- Heebo Black supers.
- Grain at full; a slight gate weave suits the street.
- 6 s cutdown = box slam → lace → ripple → logo.

**Budget:**
- refs $0.60
- 4 Flare $1.20
- 3 Kling $1.43
- 2 Seedance 720p 5 s $4.60
- retakes $1.80
- Total ≈ **$9.63**

**(6) AI weak spots.** Gait: feet slide, merge or swap left and right. Lace and eyelet counts change. Sole text and logos morph. Two shoes in frame may not match. Fixes:
- Prefer shoe-locked rigs over walking wides.
- Single-shoe shots.
- A blank sole sidewall, with the logo set in post.
- Pick the best 2 s of each take.

## 3. LUXURY LEATHER GOODS & BAGS

**(1) Visual grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - grain macro under a raking light
  - edge-paint and saddle-stitch ECU
  - an artisan's hands (a waxed thread pull, an awl)
  - hardware glint
  - the bag carried by a body with the face cropped
  - the bag as an object in a surreal still life (Loewe)
  - silhouette framing (Hermès)
- **Light.** Raking hard light at 10–20° to reveal pores. One gradient wrap on the body of the bag.
- **Colour.** One leather colour sets the world, e.g. a tan bag in a bleached-oak room.
- **Pacing.** Slow, 2–3 s per shot. The film is allowed to breathe.
- **Type.** Cormorant italic for the line, or a tiny sans (Hermès).
- **Sound.** Leather creak, a clasp, a stitch pull, a room on a quiet afternoon.

**(2) Why they buy, and the traps**
- **Why they buy:**
  - craft as status (Bottega: "craft is the language", recognisable without a logo)
  - longevity and patina
  - quiet luxury
  - heritage (Loewe 180 years, [AOTW](https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/180-years-of-loewe))
- Craft videos *with* a consumer scene raise purchase intent (see 02).
- **Traps:**
  - FTC Leather Guides, 16 CFR 24: "genuine", "bonded" and imitation leather must not be misrepresented. "Vegan leather" must make clear it is not leather.
  - "Italian leather" and "handmade" claims need truth.
- **Trade dress:**
  - The Birkin shape. Hermès won infringement and dilution in the MetaBirkins case ([Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2023/02/jury-finds-that-metabirkin-nfts-infringed-hermes-trademark-rights)).
  - The intrecciato-style woven-strip pattern, monogram canvases, red soles.
  - Give the fictional brand its own signature: a stitch colour, an edge detail or a hardware shape.

**(3) Formats**
- **A. "Made by hand"**
  - 0–2: awl pierces leather (ASMR).
  - 2–5: waxed thread pulled taut.
  - 5–8: edge burnishing macro.
  - 8–11: finished bag on a bench.
  - 11–14: carried down a sunlit street, face cropped.
  - 14–17: BIG IDEA, then logo.
- **B. Object in a world (Loewe tableau)**
  - 0–3: the bag hidden in an absurd still life.
  - 3–9: three tableaux, each with the bag.
  - 9–13: hero on wood panelling.
  - 13–16: logo.
- **C. Silhouette walk (Hermès)**
  - 0–3: overcast sky through a foreground shape.
  - 3–9: the body walking, the bag swinging as a silhouette.
  - 9–12: a hardware macro.
  - 12–15: wide horizon line, line of type.

**(4) BIG IDEAS**
- **CASSARO, "Better every year."** One locked shot ages the bag 20 years: new pale vachetta turns honey-patina while the light outside cycles. Use Kling start frame = new, end frame = aged.
- **CASSARO, "Woven from afternoon light."** Venetian-blind shadows fall across a bare table, cross, and resolve into the bag's woven panel sitting in the same spot.

**(5) Recipe**
- Hero and macros: Flare + ref → Kling.
- Hands: Soul Cinema → Kling.
- Patina: two Flare stills (new and aged edit) → Kling start + end frames.
- Blinds-to-weave: Seedance with refs.

**STYLE HEADER:** "Atelier still life, shot on Kodak Vision3 500T, 65 mm at f/5.6. Single hard 4300 K window light raking at 15° across the surface, revealing pores and grain, deep negative fill, warm dust in the air. Bleached oak bench with knife scars, linen offcuts, waxed thread. Bag from @image_1 exact: stitch count, edge paint, hardware shape, no engraving. Off-centre on the left third. No logos, no monogram, no woven-strip pattern, no plastic sheen, no HDR."

Stills, each followed by its matching motion prompt:
1. **Still:** "[HEADER] Macro of the saddle stitch along the bag's edge, a thread tail, a pore field."
   - **Kling motion:** "Raking light slides 10 cm across the surface as if a cloud passes; the grain lights up in a wave. The camera is still with micro breathing."
2. **Still (Soul Cinema):** "Artisan's weathered hands, cracked knuckle skin, pulling waxed linen thread through leather on a stitching pony, warm window, apron texture."
   - **Kling motion:** "Two quick firm pulls, the thread tightens and the leather dimples. Real speed. No face."
3. **Still:** "[HEADER] Bag on the bench, flap closed, brass clasp catching one specular."
   - **Kling motion:** "A hand enters frame-right and opens the clasp with a click; the flap lifts 2 cm. Only the hand and flap move."
4. **Patina (Kling, start = new, end = aged):** "Locked-off time-lapse: daylight cycles across the room twice while the leather slowly deepens from pale to honey. The bag's shape never changes."
5. **Street (Seedance):** "@image_1 bag, @image_2 street. A woman walks away from camera, face never seen, the bag swinging naturally from her shoulder, strap swaying with her stride. Overcast flat light, 50 mm, handheld follow."

**ElevenLabs:**

## 4. EYEWEAR

**(1) Visual grammar**
- **Shots:**
  - hinge snap macro
  - a temple fold
  - the lens refracting the world (a water glass or a city grid bending)
  - the world reflected in the lenses (Oakley visor)
  - a dolly through the lens into the reflected world (Kling community)
  - glasses on an object or a statue rather than on a face
  - the case magnet clack
  - a profile wearing them with the eyes unseen
- **Light.** Backlight to show tint, or a hard sun so the lens throws a coloured shadow onto paper. Two specular lines per lens.
- **Colour.** The lens tint becomes the grade (Gentle Monster: one surreal set per drop; [Impression](https://theimpression.com/gentle-monster-2025-bold-collection-ad-campaign-review/)).
- **Pacing.** 1.5–2.5 s per shot.
- **Type.** Heebo Light, wide-tracked.
- **Sound.** Hinge click, case magnet, a wind gust, a city muffled through the lens.

**(2) Why they buy, and the traps**
- **Why they buy:** identity and face transformation, the way they see the world, and the cool reference (Gentle Monster's art-world retail; [Impression](https://theimpression.com/gentle-monster-fall-2025-ad-campaign-review/)).
- **Objection:** "will it suit me?" Answer with on-face crops or a try-on CTA.
- **Traps:**
  - "UV400", "polarized" and "blue-light" claims need substantiation; do not make health claims.
  - US sunglasses are medical devices that need impact-resistant lenses ([21 CFR 801.410](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-H/part-801/subpart-H/section-801.410)).
- **Trade dress.** No trapezoid-Wayfarer copy and no teardrop-aviator plus logo on the lens. Never put a wordmark on the lens corner unless it is ours, added in post.

## 5. Shared rules for this cluster
1. **Ban list on every motion prompt:** no slow motion, no rotation of the product, no text change, no logo, no morphing, no extra parts. Add "already moving at frame 1".
2. **Design the fictional product for AI.** Use dials without text, solitaire stones, a shoe sole without logos, leather without engraving and lenses without a logo. All branding goes in post.
3. **One material world per film** (moss / slate / oak / concrete). One product per shot. Faces averted.
4. **Glint in post, not in the prompt.** Star overlays, a light-slash wipe, or a 4% luma flicker on the hit.
5. **Label the piece** "Fictional brand. AI concept film." in the caption.
