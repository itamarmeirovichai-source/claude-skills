## 1. WATCHES & JEWELRY

**(1) Grammar**
- **Shots:** dial close-up, crown/clasp macro, cuff-pull wrist, neck macro, ring on a plinth, exploded view, box hinge, 10:10 hero.
- **Light:** metal needs one big reflected card plus one hard specular. Facets need a small hard point source.
- **Hands at 10:10, always.** The hands frame the logo, clear the subdials and read as a "smile" ([Gear Patrol](https://gearpatrol.com/watches/a42447598/why-are-watch-hands-set-to-1010-in-photos/)).
- **Colour worlds:** moss + gold, night teal, steel-blue (dive).
- **Pacing/type:** 1.5–3 s, glint on the beat; Cormorant small caps tracked +150.
- **Sound:** an 8-beat/s tick (28,800 vph), the crown ratchet, a clasp click, a hinge creak.

**(2) Why people buy, and the traps**
- **Purchase drivers:** milestones and gifting; heritage (Patek's "Generations": you only look after it for the next one); self-reward.
- **Objection** "is it real?": material macro plus spec.
- **FTC Jewelry Guides, 16 CFR 23** ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-23), [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/all-that-glitters-is-not-gold-ftc-updates-jewelry-guides)): Nothing under 10K may be called "gold", and the stated karat must be accurate. Disclose gold-filled, plated and vermeil. A lab-grown stone needs "laboratory-grown" with equal prominence if you say "cultured". Disclose treatments on pearls and gems.
- **Watch claims:** "Diver's" requires ISO 6425: at least 100 m plus further criteria ([ref](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_Resistant_mark)). Never say "waterproof". Say "water-resistant to X m". "Chronometer" and "Swiss Made" have legal definitions.
- **Trade dress — do not imitate:** a crown logo, a Tank-style rectangle with Roman numerals, a panther, the robin's-egg box, a serpent coil, screw-motif bangles. Screen outputs: the community divewatch leaked a real logo.

**(3) Formats (15–20 s)**
- **A. Light finds it.** 0–2 black frame, a slash of light sweeps in and a facet glints; 2–5 dial close-up at 10:10; 5–8 crown macro with fingers; 8–11 wrist or neck crop; 11–14 BIG IDEA; 14–18 off-centre hero, logo, CTA.
- **B. The gift.** 0–2 hands cup a box, the hinge creaks; 2–4 velvet glow; 4–7 the piece lifts out on a fingertip; 7–10 it is clasped on; 10–13 a hand rises to the collarbone (a reaction without a face); 13–20 line, then end card.
- **C. Exploded movement.** 0–2 hero; 2–6 parts float apart, casting shadows; 6–9 bezel/lume macro; 9–12 reassembly; 12–15 on the wrist; 15–18 spec super ("200 m. Sapphire.") and logo.

**(4) BIG IDEAS**
- **AUREN dive watch: "Silence at 200 m."** A split frame at the waterline: chop and noise above, still blue below, where only the seconds hand moves. The audio drops to nothing at the waterline.
- **SÉLAH gold chain: "Every link is a hand."** Three generations' hands interlock. Match-cut to three gold links in the same pose.

**(5) Recipe**
- **Models:** Dial, crown, ring and hero: Flare with the product ref, then Kling 3.0 Pro with start frame = end frame. Neck, hands, box POV: Soul Cinema, then a Flare edit to comp the piece in, then Kling. Exploded view and the split BIG IDEA: Seedance 2.5 at 720p, 5 s.
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Real tabletop insert, ARRI Alexa 35, 100 mm macro f/11, deep focus on metal, everything else falls to black. Single hard 3200 K key raking from frame-left at 15°, one large diffusion card reflected in polished surfaces, negative fill right. Product on the right third. Fine dust on sapphire, micro-scratches on the bezel, a faint fingerprint. Kodak Vision3 250D grain, gentle halation, muted grade. No HDR, no CGI gloss, no symmetry, no added text, logos or numerals beyond @image_1."
- **Still → motion pairs:**
  1. **Dial hero.** Still: "[HEADER] Watch from @image_1 on brushed slate, hands exactly 10:10, three-quarter view from 30° above." Kling: "Slider push of 3% over 5 s, already moving at frame 1. The dial, hands and indices are a fixed printed surface. Only light moves: a thin highlight crawls across the bezel."
  2. **Crown macro.** Still: "[HEADER] Crown knurling macro, two fingertips with visible ridges at frame-left, out of focus." Kling: "Fingers turn the crown a quarter-turn in two ratchet steps, at real speed. Camera locked to the case with micro handheld breathing."
  3. **Neck crop.** Still (Soul Cinema): "Neck and collarbone in three-quarter profile, cropped above the jaw, unretouched skin with fine down hair, 85 mm f/2.8, warm window light, dark teal wall." Then a Flare edit to add the chain. Kling: "Slow inhale. The collarbone rises and one link catches a glint. Handheld drift of 2 cm. No face."
  4. **Ring on moss.** Still: "[HEADER] Ring from @image_1 on a moss-covered stone ledge in the lower third, candle bokeh far behind." Kling: "5° arc plus a rack focus from moss to stone. Stone count and prongs stay fixed."
  5. **BIG IDEA** (Seedance; @image_1 = watch, @image_2 = split layout): "Half-underwater dome-port split frame: storm chop above the meniscus, still blue silence below, caustics on the case, the seconds hand ticking steadily. No bubbles near the dial; dial text never changes."
- **Workarounds for dial and text morphing:** (a) Design a wordless dial (baton indices plus a glyph) and add the wordmark in post with the same `zoompan` curve as the push. (b) Set Kling `last_image_url` to the same still. (c) Use only the first 3 s, because morphs arrive late. (d) Let the second hand move only in the BIG IDEA shot. (e) Choose solitaire or bezel-set stones and chunky links. Pavé melts. (f) Do sparkle as a post star PNG on 1–3 highlights with an 8-frame fade (the Dior convention). Never prompt "sparkle".
- **ElevenLabs:** Music: felt piano and low cello, 72 BPM. Room tone for 0–3 s, a low note on the first glint, a swell at 11 s, 0.5 s of silence before the logo. SFX: "mechanical watch ticking 8 beats per second, close-mic", "jewelry box hinge creak and felt thump". VO (v3): `[whispers] Silence… at two hundred metres.`
- **Edit:** 6–7 shots, punch-ins from 100% to 108%, glints on the hits. Film chain at half grain, because metal shows noise. 6 s cutdown: glint → dial → BIG IDEA → logo.
- **Budget:** refs $0.60 + 6 Flare $1.80 + 5 Kling $2.38 + 1 Seedance $2.30 + retakes $2.50 ≈ **$9.60**.

**(6) Weak spots**
- Numerals, logos and hands morph; stone/prong counts drift; bracelets fuse with skin; "sparkle" becomes glitter; chains float. Fix: the workarounds above plus "chain hangs straight and heavy".

## 2. SNEAKERS & STREETWEAR

**(1) Grammar**
- **Shots:** silhouette opener, box-lid lift, lace cinch, sole compression, shoe-locked snorricam, on-feet wide, mirrored-pair end card.
- **Framing:** ground-level 24 mm, worm's-eye, the shoe filling the lower half.
- **Light:** hard low sun, sodium night, or amber/blue duotone for the product-poem format.
- **Colour:** the colourway sets the grade. New Balance "Grey Days" builds a grey world (DP Norm Li; [AOTW](https://adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/grey-days)).
- **Pacing/type:** 0.8–1.7 s, cut on the kick; Heebo Black uppercase.
- **Sound:** squeak, lace zip, box cardboard, footsteps on concrete.

**(2) Why people buy, and the traps**
- **Purchase drivers:** identity and subculture (NB "We Got Now", [Campaign](https://campaignlive.com/article/ad-week-new-balances-we-now/1715921)), scarcity of the drop, visible comfort, the colourway.
- **Objection** fit/comfort: compression macro plus on-feet walk.
- **Claims:** Fake countdowns or a fake "sold out" are FTC deception. Performance percentages need tests. "Made in USA" must mean all or virtually all ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/made-usa-labeling-rule)).
- **Never generate:** three stripes, tick-like swooshes, a jumping silhouette, an "N", a side wave, a toe-cap-plus-midsole-stripe, red soles.
- Give our brand its own signature instead, e.g. an odd-colour heel tab or a sole notch.

**(3) Formats**
- **A. Drop reveal.** 0–1.5 the box slams into frame; 1.5–3 lid and tissue; 3–6 macros of suede, lace and heel tab; 6–9 lace cinch and step-in; 9–13 snorricam through the city; 13–16 BIG IDEA; 16–18 "Fri 10AM" and logo.
- **B. Product poem (Superfly).** 0–2 silhouette against a glow; 2–8 four macros with light streaks; 8–11 colour wipe; 11–14 sole compression; 14–17 mirrored pair and logo.
- **C. Neighbourhood portrait (Grey Days).** 0–2 grey street; 2–10 four vignettes of feet and hands; 10–14 shoe on a curb in rain; 14–17 line and logo.

**(4) BIG IDEAS (GRAYFIELD)**
- **"Concrete, softened."** At footstrike the asphalt ripples outward in rings like foam, then settles.
- **"Laced into the city."** One lace pull cinches the night street's light trails into parallel lines that resolve into the shoe's stitch line.

**(5) Recipe**
- **Models:** Macros: Flare with the shoe sheet (side and three-quarter views), then Kling. Snorricam and the ripple: Seedance 2.5 reference-to-video at 720p, with refs = shoe + a Soul Cinema location.
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Streetwear campaign on 35 mm, Kodak Portra 400 pushed one stop, 24 mm at ground level. Hard late sun 4800 K raking low from the left, long shadows, slight flare. A worn neighbourhood: cracked asphalt, chalk marks, chain-link, wet gutter. Shoe from @image_1 exact: panel count, stitching, lace count, sole pattern. Toe-box creases, heel scuff. No logos, no stripes, no text, no CGI gloss, no symmetric framing."
- **Still → motion pairs:**
  1. **Curb shot.** Still: "[HEADER] Shoe on a cracked curb, three-quarter rear view, heel tab on the left third, puddle reflection." Kling: "Low slider arc of 8°, real speed, a pigeon shadow crosses. The shoe is perfectly still."
  2. **Lace pull.** Still: "[HEADER] Macro of a hand pulling the laces, knuckles at frame-right, eyelets sharp." Kling: "One fast pull, the laces snap taut and the tongue flexes. No slow motion. Locked on the eyelets with micro shake."
  3. **Snorricam** (Seedance; @image_1 shoe, @image_2 street): "Camera rigidly bolted beside the right shoe at ankle height. The shoe stays fixed in frame while kerbs whip past with motion blur. The sole plants flat, no foot sliding."
  4. **BIG IDEA** (Seedance): "Ground-level 24 mm. The shoe lands at full speed and the asphalt ripples outward in three concentric rings like compressed foam, then settles. Dust lifts. Shoe geometry never changes. Documentary handheld."
- **ElevenLabs:** Music: dusty boom-bap at 92 BPM with a sub drop at 13 s, or UK garage for the drop format. SFX: "cardboard shoe box lid", "lace zip pull". VO: `[confident, low] Concrete. Softened.`
- **Edit:** whip-pans on kicks, one 2-frame colourway flash. Full grain and gate weave. 6 s cutdown: slam → lace → ripple → logo.
- **Budget:** refs $0.60 + 4 Flare $1.20 + 3 Kling $1.43 + 2 Seedance $4.60 + retakes $1.80 ≈ **$9.63**.

**(6) Weak spots**
- Feet slide or swap, eyelet counts change, sole text morphs, pairs mismatch. Fix: shoe-locked rigs, single-shoe shots, blank sidewall (logo in post), best 2 s per take.

## 3. LUXURY LEATHER GOODS & BAGS

**(1) Grammar**
- **Shots:** grain macro, stitch close-up, artisan hands, hardware glint, carried bag (face cropped), still-life tableau (Loewe), silhouette (Hermès).
- **Light:** hard raking light at 10–20° to show the pores, plus one gradient wrap.
- **Colour:** the leather tone sets the world (tan bag in a bleached-oak room).
- **Pacing/type:** 2–3 s; Cormorant italic or a tiny sans.

**(2) Why people buy, and the traps**
- **Purchase drivers:** craft as status, recognisable without a logo (Bottega); longevity and patina; quiet luxury; heritage (Loewe 180, [AOTW](https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/180-years-of-loewe)).
- **FTC Leather Guides (16 CFR 24):** Don't misrepresent "genuine", bonded or imitation leather. "Vegan leather" must make clear it is not leather. "Italian" and "handmade" must be true.
- **Trade dress:** Birkin-like shapes: Hermès won the MetaBirkins jury verdict ([Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2023/02/jury-finds-that-metabirkin-nfts-infringed-hermes-trademark-rights)). Woven-strip intrecciato-style panels, monogram canvases. Our signature is our own stitch colour or hardware shape.

**(3) Formats**
- **A. Made by hand.** 0–2 the awl pierces leather (ASMR); 2–5 thread pulled taut; 5–8 edge burnishing; 8–11 the bag on the bench; 11–14 carried down a sunlit street, face cropped; 14–17 BIG IDEA and logo.
- **B. Object in a world (Loewe).** 0–9 three absurd tableaux, each hiding the bag; 9–13 hero on panelling; 13–16 logo.
- **C. Silhouette walk (Hermès).** 0–3 sky through a foreground shape; 3–9 swinging bag in silhouette; 9–12 hardware macro; 12–15 horizon and type.

**(4) BIG IDEAS (CASSARO)**
- **"Better every year."** One locked shot ages the bag 20 years: pale vachetta turns honey while daylight cycles. Use Kling with start = new, end = aged.
- **"Woven from afternoon light."** Venetian-blind shadows cross on a bare table and resolve into the bag's woven panel in the same spot.

**(5) Recipe**
- **Models:** Macros and hero: Flare + ref, then Kling. Hands: Soul Cinema, then Kling. Patina: two Flare stills, then Kling with start and end frames. Blinds-to-weave and the street walk: Seedance with refs.
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Atelier still life, Kodak Vision3 500T, 65 mm f/5.6. Single hard 4300 K window light raking at 15° across the surface, revealing pores, deep negative fill, warm dust in the air. Bleached oak bench with knife scars, linen offcuts, waxed thread. Bag from @image_1 exact: stitch count, edge paint, hardware shape, no engraving. Left third. No logos, monogram or woven-strip pattern, no plastic sheen, no HDR."
- **Still → motion pairs:**
  1. **Stitch macro.** Still: "[HEADER] Macro of the saddle stitch on the edge, a thread tail, a pore field." Kling: "The raking light slides 10 cm as a cloud passes and the grain lights up in a wave. Camera still, micro breathing."
  2. **Artisan hands.** Still (Soul Cinema): "Weathered artisan hands with cracked knuckles pulling waxed linen through leather on a stitching pony, warm window, apron texture." Kling: "Two quick firm pulls, the thread tightens and the leather dimples. Real speed. No face."
  3. **Clasp.** Still: "[HEADER] Bag on the bench, flap closed, the brass clasp catching one specular." Kling: "A hand enters frame-right and opens the clasp with a click. The flap lifts 2 cm. Only the hand and the flap move."
  4. **Patina** (Kling, start + end frames): "Locked-off time-lapse. Daylight cycles across the room twice while the leather deepens from pale to honey. The bag's shape never changes."
  5. **Street** (Seedance; @image_1 bag, @image_2 street): "A woman walks away from camera, face never seen. The bag swings naturally and the strap stays outside her coat. Overcast flat light, 50 mm handheld follow."
- **ElevenLabs:** Music: nylon guitar and a string pad at 66 BPM, with silence under the awl. SFX: "awl piercing leather", "brass clasp click". VO: `[softly] Better… every year.`
- **Edit:** 2.5 s holds. Dissolve only inside the patina beat. Warm print LUT. 6 s cutdown: stitch → patina → logo.
- **Budget:** refs $0.60 + 6 Flare $1.80 + 5 Kling $2.38 + 1 Seedance $2.30 + retakes $2.50 ≈ **$9.58**.

**(6) Weak spots**
- Stitching drifts, engravings morph, straps clip through bodies, bags inflate. Fix: no engraving, short wide body shots, start = end frame.

## 4. EYEWEAR

**(1) Grammar**
- **Shots:** hinge snap, temple fold, lens refraction, world reflected in lenses (Oakley), dolly through the lens (Kling community), frames on a bust, case clack, profile with eyes hidden.
- **Light:** backlight shows the tint. A hard sun makes the lens throw a coloured shadow onto paper. Two specular lines per lens.
- **Colour:** the lens tint is the grade. Gentle Monster builds one surreal set per drop ([Impression](https://theimpression.com/gentle-monster-2025-bold-collection-ad-campaign-review/), [Fall 2025](https://theimpression.com/gentle-monster-fall-2025-ad-campaign-review/)).
- **Pacing/type:** 1.5–2.5 s; Heebo Light tracked wide.
- **Sound:** hinge click, case magnet, muffled city.

**(2) Why people buy, and the traps**
- **Drivers:** identity, "how I see the world", art-world cool.
- **Objection** "will it suit me?": face crops plus a try-on CTA.
- **Claims:** UV400, polarized and blue-light claims need substantiation. Make no health claims.
- **Regulation:** US sunglasses are medical devices that require impact-resistant lenses ([21 CFR 801.410](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-H/part-801/subpart-H/section-801.410)).
- **Trade dress:** no Wayfarer-trapezoid or teardrop-aviator copies, and no lens-corner wordmark unless it is ours, added in post.

**(3) Formats**
- **A. See it this way.** 0–2 grey street; 2–4 the frames lift into view, and only through the lens the world is golden; 4–8 hinge and temple macros; 8–12 profile; 12–15 BIG IDEA; 15–18 logo.
- **B. Art object (Gentle Monster).** 0–3 frames on a plaster bust in a brutalist room; 3–8 three surreal still lifes; 8–12 orbit; 12–15 logo.
- **C. Hinge ASMR.** 0–2 snap open; 2–6 lens shadow changes colour on paper; 6–10 case clack; 10–14 reflection; 14–16 logo.

**(4) BIG IDEAS (LUMEN NOIR)**
- **"Change the grade."** On a rainy grey street, only the view through the lenses is a warm golden afternoon. Grade the plate twice and mask by the lens shape in post. No AI needed for the effect.
- **"Every frame is a frame."** A dolly into the lens reflection of a coastline passes through the glass into that coast and keeps flying.

**(5) Recipe**
- **Models:** Product: Flare with refs (front and three-quarter, open and folded), then Kling. Wearer: Soul Cinema in profile, hair over the eye, then Kling. Dolly-through: Kling with start = lens close-up, end = coast.
- **STYLE HEADER:** "Eyewear campaign, 50 mm macro f/8, reversal-film look. Hard 5600 K sun from high left, the lenses throwing a tinted shadow onto textured paper. Frames from @image_1 exact: rim thickness, bridge, hinge, both lenses identical. Fingerprint smudge on one lens, dust on the paper. Right third. No text, no lens logo, no CGI chrome, no HDR."
- **Still → motion pairs:**
  1. **Bust.** Still: "[HEADER] Open frames on a plaster bust's nose in a concrete room, three-quarter view." Kling: "6° orbit right, one light slash crosses the lenses. The frames stay rigid."
  2. **Hinge.** Still: "[HEADER] Hinge macro, a temple half-folded, a fingertip at the left." Kling: "The fingertip snaps the temple open at real speed. The metal does not bend."
  3. **Pier profile.** Still (Soul Cinema): "Man's profile on a windy pier, hair over the eye, overcast." Comp frames in with Flare. Kling: "Wind moves his hair. He turns his head 10° toward the sea. The frames stay on his nose."
  4. **Dolly** (Kling, start + end frames): "Continuous push into the right lens. The reflected coast grows until we pass through the glass into it on the same trajectory, no cut, then handheld forward over the water."
- **ElevenLabs:** Music: analog synth pulse at 84 BPM, opening into warm Rhodes at the lens reveal. SFX: "eyeglass hinge snap", "magnetic case clack". VO: `[calm] Change the grade.`
- **Edit:** The golden-lens mask: a +2-stop warm grade inside the lens shape, on a short near-static shot. Heebo Light tracked +200. 6 s cutdown: grey → golden lens → hinge → logo.
- **Budget:** refs $0.60 + 5 Flare $1.50 + 5 Kling $2.38 + retakes $1.43 ≈ **$6** (room for a Seedance try).

**(6) Weak spots**
- Lenses differ, frames warp on head turns, reflections mismatch, refraction is ignored, faces go waxy. Fix: objects and profiles, refraction/tint in post, "both lenses identical", head turns under 15°.

## 5. Shared rules
1. **Every motion prompt carries a ban list:** no slow motion, no product rotation, no text change, no logos, no morphing, no extra parts. Add "already moving at frame 1".
2. **Design the fictional product for AI.** Wordless dials, solitaire stones, logo-free soles, unengraved hardware, clean lenses. All branding is added in post.
3. **Glint in post**, never "sparkle" in a prompt. 4. **Caption:** "Fictional brand. AI concept film."
