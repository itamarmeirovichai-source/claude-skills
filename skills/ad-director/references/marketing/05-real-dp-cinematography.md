## Lighting glass and liquid
- **Backlight does most of the work.** Hard tungsten behind the bottle makes the juice glow from within; move it shot to shot [L]. Gridded soft backlights trace only the edges, with the front kept dark until a final fill [B]. A small hidden light sits low behind the bottle, angled up [E].
- **Gradient wrap.** A 6×6 diffusion frame right beside the product, with the light behind it almost facing camera, so light falls off across the glass [D].
- **The moving sliver.** A snooted tungsten slash, hand-panned across the bottle, hands or rock. It became the film's signature [L]. A light slid past the bottle draws streaks [A][B].
- **Reflections.** Keep one clean specular per surface and avoid blotchy hotspots [K]. A 45° reflection is made with diffusion cut on a diagonal by black card [B].
- **Contrast.** Use floppies and negative fill, and keep the back wall dark, or all the contrast is lost [L][F]. The "dark-side" key comes from the side away from camera, with the camera on the shadow side [J].
- **Black-set recipe** [E]: pull the product far from the backdrop, light it with a top-down key, add a foam-core bounce underneath and a small backlight, expose for the product, and crush the background to black in the grade.
- **Background separation.** Raking hard light across dark panels [K], a gobo on the back wall [K], a white card in the top-left corner [L], or a spherical gradient backboard [C].
- **Colour.** Use ONE colour pair everywhere [L]. Mix colour temperatures (2700 K against daylight) [F]. Save the one white highlight for the hero [C].

## Lenses, camera, motion
- Macro inserts at f/16–f/32 for deep focus [B]. Faces at f/2.8 [F]. Lenses: 25 mm Zeiss Supreme, 24 mm probe, a 50 mm "nifty fifty", and a 16 mm inside a glass tube [F][K].
- Frame rates: 240 fps at 1/500 for flying ingredients [E], 600 fps for pours [K], and 60 fps slowed 20–30% [G]. Use a 180° shutter for natural motion blur.
- Moves:
  - motion-control pull-back while the product rotates into the reveal [B]
  - a flag sliding off the key so the light "grows" onto the bottle [K][B]
  - top-down push/pull [K]
  - turntable spins of 2–3 rotations [I]
  - Steadicam orbit [L]
  - slider angled to the table so lines converge [J]
- Composition: never flat-on; use angled lines and light–dark–light depth layers [J]. Keep the product off-centre (thirds) to leave room for type [G]. Fabric fills the dark third of the frame [L]. Mostly tight shots; wide shots only to establish.

## Sets and practical effects
- Sets: black leather, wainscot panels [K]; mirror board, black powder, dark rock [L]; real ingredients such as halved citrus and vanilla pods [A].
- The bottle dropped into black powder, which bursts gold where it crosses a ground-level tungsten beam [L]. Haze, but "everybody overcooks it" [J]. Rain on the window as a foreground layer [J]. Ingredients launched from below frame, with no hands [E].

## What makes footage read as REAL
- Visible light falloff, and large controlled black areas.
- Haze, and windows blown out but still showing detail.
- Practicals in frame that don't actually light the scene.
- Mixed colour temperatures, and a non-zero shadow side on skin.
- Hotspots only partly removed.
- Focus that lands a beat late, a slight miss, faint dust or micro-scratches.

## Prompt vocabulary for a real shoot
**Light**
- "single hard tungsten backlight through the bottle, liquid glowing from within"
- "thin sliver of snooted tungsten light hand-panned across the glass, rest of frame falling to black"
- "gridded soft backlights tracing only the bottle's edges and shoulders"
- "wraparound gradient from a large diffusion frame beside the product, visible light falloff across the glass"
- "keyed from the far side, camera on the shadow side, black negative fill"
- "white foam-core bounce lifting the base"
- "one clean vertical specular highlight, a few small uneven hotspots left in"
- "diagonal reflection cut by black card"
- "hard raking light across dark panelled backdrop"
- "out-of-focus gobo pattern on the back wall"
- "mixed colour temperature: 2700K tungsten against cool daylight"
- "practicals visible but not lighting the scene"

**Lens**
- "macro insert at f/16, deep focus"
- "25mm Zeiss Supreme Prime at T2.8"
- "50mm full-frame, gentle compression"
- "24mm probe lens skimming the tabletop"
- "240fps, 1/500 shutter, crisp droplets"
- "natural 180-degree shutter motion blur"
- "slight focus breathing, focus lands a beat late"

**Move**
- "motion-control pull-back while the bottle rotates into reveal"
- "flag slides off the key light, light growing across the bottle"
- "slow top-down push-in"
- "locked-off turntable spin at product height"
- "slider move at an angle to the table, lines converging"
- "light source slides past the bottle drawing a line along its edge"

**Set and realism**
- "product pulled far forward from black seamless, crushed shadows"
- "bottle dropped into black powder bursting gold in a ground-level tungsten beam"
- "textured black leather, wainscot panels"
- "clean mirror board reflection"
- "draped dark fabric filling a third of the frame"
- "atmospheric haze catching the backlight"
- "rain-streaked window foreground layer"
- "fine dust specks and micro-scratches on the glass, not CG-perfect"
- "off-centre composition, negative space for type"
- "rich blacks, controlled highlights, subtle film grain, true-to-camera grade"
