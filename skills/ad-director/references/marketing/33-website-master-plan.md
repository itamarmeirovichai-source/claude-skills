## 1. Concept

{{STUDIO}} sells premium 15–25 s product films to founder-led DTC brands whose product sells through the senses: "the tabletop commercial without the shoot". The home page opens on a show. Otto, an openly AI director with a moustache the size of his shoulders, stands centre screen inside a thin film frame, and our films orbit him. The visitor picks one; Otto points, the film enlarges and plays with sound, and Otto asks, "Pick another, or continue?" On Continue he reaches out past the frame and hauls the site up from below. Below is a calm, honest landing page: free frames on your product, labelled spec work, a one-week process, a risk reversal, three clear prices. Otto reappears six times, one line each, and never sells. Headline, offer and both CTAs are visible from the first frame, so the show never blocks the offer.

## 4. Mascot recommendation

**Pick: Otto, the weird director** (`30` §5A). A tall, lean man in his mid-50s with real skin and an **enormous charcoal handlebar moustache, 1.6× shoulder width, fully covering the mouth**. Black turtleneck, tan waxed-canvas vest, silver viewfinder on a lanyard, a red tally-light pin. Deadpan, generous, exacting; finger-frames the viewer.

Why: he plays the studio's job; the moustache reads at 64 px; a hidden mouth removes lip-sync, our biggest risk; deadpan absurd is the tone AI carries best. Voice: ElevenLabs Voice Design, dry baritone (`30` §5A), one `voice_id` forever.

**Rules:** labelled AI everywhere; a host, never a reviewer (FTC); no praise of results, no clients, no claimed senses or credits, never says he makes client films. Off pricing, tiles and deliverables. ≤8 words a line. **No spoken line contains his name or the studio's**, so a rename costs no audio.

**Alternatives:**
- **Hopper** (frog head, perfect suit): non-human fallback; lip-sync errors read as character.
- **Margo** (producer, beehive wig): visible mouth, highest lip-sync risk. Not for v1.
- With Still Life Club, Otto doubles as "the Club's host" unchanged.

## 5. Stack and architecture

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Framework | **Astro (static) + vanilla TS modules**, content collections for films, FAQ and `pricing.json` (the single price source, also used by proposals) |
| Motion | **GSAP 3** (ScrollTrigger, Flip) + **Lenis** on one ticker; `gsap.matchMedia()` for reduced motion |
| Ring | **CSS 3D** `preserve-3d`, 6 cards; Flip a flat 2D clone to enlarge |
| Otto | **`<stacked-alpha-video>`**: stacked **AV1** primary, stacked **HEVC** fallback, two A/B players. 540×960 mobile / 720×1280 desktop. No VP9 alpha shipped |
| Hero logic | Hand-rolled typed state machine (`boot → intro → idle → pointing → enlarging → playing → asking → reaching → revealed`), pure reducer + one effects runner, a timeout on every media wait |
| Clip switching | A/B players with a **4–6-frame crossfade on the hub pose** (T01: generated clips land at ≈0.91–0.93 SSIM of their end keyframe, so cuts are never pixel-exact) |
| Voice | ElevenLabs TTS `with-timestamps` **at build time only**; `.m4a` + `.vtt`; T01 E1 measured word timing at 0.04 s mean error, so the alignment-built captions are reliable |
| Hosting | **New Cloudflare Pages project `studio-site`**, root `site/`, watch paths `site/**`; PeakForm (`peakform-qtzvm`) untouched and excluded from `site/**` |
| Media | On Pages while ≤40 MB total; then R2 at `media.<domain>` (needs the domain). No Cloudflare Stream |
| Form | Pages Functions `start → PUT photo → finish`, shared zod schema, Turnstile + honeypot + rate limit, private R2 `studio-uploads`, 90-day retention |
| Email | **Resend** on the verified domain: founder alert + auto-reply to the lead. Before the domain exists: leads in R2, `/admin` behind PeakForm's gate pattern, no auto-reply |
| Analytics | Cloudflare Web Analytics + beacon → Analytics Engine; no cookies. One event list: `31` §8 plus `29`'s `skip_click`, `pop_seen`, `character_hide`, offline stages |
| Quality | Strict TS + ESLint, Vitest, Playwright (WebKit iPhone, Chromium Pixel), axe, Lighthouse CI (LCP ≤2.5 s, JS ≤90 kB gz), `check-dist` |

Budgets: ≤1.5 MB before the first gesture on mobile; ring loops ≤400 kB; `char_idle` ≤350/600 kB; full films ≤4/8 MB.
