# Every portfolio piece is a real ad, not a demo

A clip without a point is a demo. A portfolio of ads must **sell something**: one product, one promise, one reason to believe, one action. The viewer must finish knowing **what it is, why they want it, and what to do**.

## 1. The ad brief (fill in before any image)
- **Product + one-line promise:** "TIDEWATER: electrolyte water that cools you down in Florida heat" (benefit, not features; no health claims we can't back).
- **Audience + moment:** "Floridians at 3 PM, sun-drained".
- **Single-minded message (≤ 7 words):** "Beat the heat, one sip."
- **Reason to believe (visual proof):** frost spreading from the can; condensation; the person visibly reviving.
- **Tone:** cinematic and cool / funny / premium / ASMR.
- **CTA:** "Find it cold at your corner store" / "Shop link in bio" / "Comment HEAT".
- **Brand moments:** logo or pack visible by 0:03, packshot + tagline in the last 2 s, sonic logo.

## 2. Story arcs that fit 10–20 s (pick one per ad)
1. **Problem → turn → payoff (classic DR):** pain (heat), product enters, transformation, packshot + CTA.
2. **Before / after world:** the world without it vs. with it (visual metaphor), packshot.
3. **Hook → reveal:** an impossible image first ("snow on a Miami street?"), then the explanation is the product.
4. **Ritual:** a satisfying sequence (open → pour → sip → sigh) shot like ASMR. Ends with the tagline.
5. **Mini-story with a twist:** a character wants X, obstacle, the product saves it, a funny button at the end.
6. **Demo-as-spectacle:** prove one true attribute in an extreme way (cold → freezes the street).

## 3. Script template (VO via ElevenLabs, supers in reel-studio)
| Time | Picture | VO (≤ 2.5 words/s) | Super | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:02 | Hook image | — or a 3–5 word hook | Hook text ≤ 6 words | Hit / ambient |
| 0:02-0:06 | Problem / setup | 1 sentence | — | Foley |
| 0:06-0:10 | Product turn + proof | 1 sentence | Key benefit | Pop / fizz / whoosh |
| 0:10-0:13 | Payoff | 1 short line | — | Music lift |
| 0:13-0:15 | Packshot + logo + CTA | Tagline | Tagline + CTA | Sonic logo |

## 4. Edit standard (every portfolio piece)
- **Word-timed captions** from the ElevenLabs VO timestamps (big, 2–4 words per card, inside safe zones).
- **Music:** ElevenLabs Music bed, instrumental, ducked under the VO. Cut the visuals on the beat.
- **SFX layer:** ElevenLabs SFX per action (pop, fizz, whoosh on cuts, an impact on the reveal).
- **Packshot end card:** the real (or approved AI) product image, logo, tagline, CTA, 2 s.
- **Versions:** 15 s master + 6 s cutdown + 3 hook variants. Optionally a "Prompt / Original vs AI" breakdown for the studio's own channel (faceless content that drives comments).
- **QC:** `director.py qc`, then the visual rubric ≥ 80 + realism checks R1–R4.

## 5. Legal guardrails
- Fictional brands only for the public portfolio.
- No health or performance claims we can't substantiate ("hydrates" is fine for a water; "boosts metabolism" is not).
- No fake testimonials. AI disclosure label on posts.
