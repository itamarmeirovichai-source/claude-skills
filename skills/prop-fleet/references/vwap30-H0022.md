# H0022 — VWAP trend with half-hour decisions: FAIL

**Verdict:** FAIL. On the decisive NQ data the rule made +5.04 bp a day after costs, with t +1.68. The bar is t ≥ 3.4808. The unseen SPXUSD-TWAP check was positive (+2.19 bp a day). SPY lost 0.75 bp a day after costs, so it failed too.

Every number below comes from `agents/daily/vwap30-H0022/vwap30.py`. The full output is in `agents/daily/vwap30-H0022/run.txt`, and the unit-test output is in `agents/daily/vwap30-H0022/pytest.txt`. The H0021 figures used for comparison come from `agents/daily/vwap-H0021/run.txt`.

## What was tested (as registered in `agents/ledger.csv`, row H0022)

The rules are H0021's intraday VWAP trend (Zarattini & Aziz 2023, SSRN 4631351) with one change: the decision cadence. Decisions are made only every 30 minutes, the cadence used in Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon's "Beat the Market" (2024). The rule was run exactly as written, with no tuning.

- **Same as H0021:**
  - The session runs on weekdays, 09:30 to 15:59 New York time.
  - VWAP starts at 09:30. It is the running sum of typical price × volume divided by the running sum of volume, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
  - A position change fills at the next bar's open.
  - The position goes flat at the close of the session's last bar.
  - There are no stops, targets or filters, and size is 1.
  - A flip counts as 2 units; an open or a close counts as 1.
  - Cost per unit: NQ 0.375 index point; SPY 0.5 bp of price.
- **What changed:** the wanted position is set **only** at the closes of the 12 bars at 09:59, 10:29, 10:59, 11:29, 11:59, 12:29, 12:59, 13:29, 13:59, 14:29, 14:59 and 15:29:
  - long if close > VWAP;
  - short if close < VWAP;
  - unchanged if they are equal (flat before the first decision that isn't equal).
  - If a decision bar is missing, the last existing bar before it in that half hour is used. If the half hour has no bars at all, that decision is skipped. The position is held until the next decision or the flat exit.
- **Unseen check (SPXUSD):** this file has no volume, so it uses the session TWAP instead of VWAP. TWAP is the running mean of the typical price, which is the same formula with every bar's volume set to 1. The cost is 0.5 bp of price per unit.
- **Unit:** net daily return in bp of notional = the day's P&L divided by that day's first entry price.
- **Decision rule:** PASS only if all three hold:
  1. NQ net daily mean > 0 with one-sided t ≥ z(0.05/N);
  2. SPXUSD-TWAP net daily mean > 0;
  3. SPY net daily mean > 0.
- **Threshold:** the ledger N is **200** (the script asserts this), so alpha is 0.05/200 = 2.5e-4 and the bar is **z = 3.4808**.

### Choices fixed before any result was computed (also in the script docstring)

All of H0021's implementation choices carry over unchanged. These include the session definition, "next bar" meaning the next bar that exists, flat at the last bar's close, the P&L and cost accounting, the NQ roll-week exclusion, and the statistics definitions. The choices specific to H0022 are:

- **Half-hour slot:** a bar's slot is (minutes since 09:30) ÷ 30, rounded down, for bars 09:30–15:29. That gives slots 0 to 11. Bars from 15:30 to 15:59 belong to no slot.
- **Decision bar:** the last bar of a slot that exists in the session. Normally that is the :59 or :29 bar.
  - You only know a bar was the last of its slot when the next bar arrives with a timestamp outside the slot. That next bar's open is exactly when the decision fills, so no future price is used.
  - If a slot has no bars, its decision is skipped.
- **Half days:** a decision bar that is also the session's last bar is never acted on, because the position goes flat at that close.
- **SPXUSD:** there is no roll exclusion, because it is a cash-index CFD. The file's volume column is all 0, so v = 1 on every bar.
- **Code change:** only the decision cadence changed. `positions()` and the streaming reference `stream_day()` were edited. `daily_pnl()`, the statistics and the H0021 loaders are unchanged, and `load_spx()` was added.

## Look-ahead checks (all passed)

- **(a) Truncation test.** For 250 random sessions per dataset (seed 0), the day was cut after every bar in turn, and the positions were recomputed from only the remaining bars. Every position was identical. That is 95,730 cut points on NQ, 89,047 on SPXUSD and 97,425 on SPY.
- **(b) Full streaming check.** A separate bar-by-bar loop sees each bar's timestamp at its open and its prices only at its close. It decides when a half hour has closed and keeps cash accounts. Across every session of all three datasets, it reproduced every bar's position, the units traded, the first entry price and the daily gross and cost. The largest P&L differences were 0 on NQ, 2.2e-16 on SPXUSD and 5.7e-14 price units on SPY.
- **Unit tests: 12 pass** (`pytest.txt`). They cover:
  - the slot mapping, and that a full session has exactly the 12 registered decision bars;
  - VWAP arithmetic;
  - signals between decision bars being ignored;
  - a short held until the next decision even when price crosses VWAP;
  - the **missing-decision-bar fallback** (09:57 stands in for missing 09:58 and 09:59 bars, filled at the 10:00 open, and the streaming loop agrees);
  - a **half hour with no bars** being skipped, with the position held through it;
  - an equal close at a decision keeping the position, and flat before the first decision;
  - no decisions in 15:30–15:59, with flat at the close;
  - a decision on a half day's last bar not being acted on;
  - flip = 2 units and the percent cost;
  - random sessions with dropped bars and dropped half hours, matching the streaming loop and the truncation test;
  - a no-position day.

## What it means (not part of the decision)

- **The turnover cut worked, but the gross edge went with it.**
  - Units traded fell from 32.6 to 5.9 a day on NQ, and the cost fell from 8.0 to 1.4 bp a day.
  - But the NQ gross fell from +12.4 bp (t +4.00) to +6.5 bp (t +2.18).
  - Net improved only slightly, from +4.40 to +5.04 bp, and t from +1.38 to +1.68. That is still far below 3.48.
- **Recent NQ is flat.** From 2023-11 on, the gross is +0.3 bp a day and the net is negative. 2025 was −9.4 bp a day net. The worst day, 2025-04-07, lost 856 bp.
- **The unseen SPXUSD result is weak, not confirming.**
  - The gross is clearly positive (+5.4 bp, t +3.94), but the net of +2.2 bp has t +1.57 and a bootstrap p5 below zero.
  - Four of nine years were negative after costs, and the net turns negative at cost ×2.
- **SPY gross is only +2.4 bp a day** (t +1.17). That is below its 3.2 bp cost.
- **The Sharpe is far too low.** At about +0.5 to +0.8 even where it is positive, this is nowhere near the Sharpe of about 3 that a 21-day evaluation needs. The ledger note on H0022 already said it was the last index-intraday lead before changing data class.
- **Do not rescue it.** Other cadences, bands or time filters would each be a new hypothesis and one more count in N.

**One-line verdict:** H0022 FAILS. Deciding every 30 minutes cuts NQ costs by about 80%, but the gross edge halves with it: net +5.0 bp/day, t 1.68 against a bar of 3.48, flat since 2023-11, and SPY is negative after costs.
