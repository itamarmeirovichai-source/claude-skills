# H0020 — 5-minute opening range breakout: FAIL

**Verdict:** FAIL. On the decisive NQ data the rule made −0.005R net per trade (t −0.08), nowhere near the t ≥ 3.48 it needs. SPY was also negative after costs. Only the SPXUSD gross check passed.

Every number below comes from `agents/daily/orb-H0020/orb.py`. The full output is in `agents/daily/orb-H0020/run.txt`, and the unit-test output is in `agents/daily/orb-H0020/pytest.txt`.

## What was tested (as registered in `agents/ledger.csv`, row H0020)

This is the rule from Zarattini & Aziz (2023), "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?" (SSRN 4416622). It was run exactly as written, with no tuning and no filters.

- **First 5-minute bar:** the 1-minute bars from 09:30 to 09:34 New York time. All five must exist, or the day is skipped.
- **Direction:** long if that bar closed above its open, short if it closed below. No trade if they are equal.
- **Entry:** the open of the 09:35 1-minute bar.
- **Stop:** the low of the first 5-minute bar for longs, the high for shorts. R = |entry − stop|. Skip the day if R ≤ 0.
- **Target:** entry ± 10R.
- **Exit:** the stop, the target, or the close of the last bar at or before 15:59, whichever comes first.
  - If one bar touches both stop and target, the stop counts.
  - A bar that opens beyond the stop fills at its open.
- **Trades:** one a day. No sizing.
- **Cost:** 2 bp of entry round trip, subtracted in R.
- **Unit:** net R per trade.
- **Decision rule:** PASS only if all three hold:
  1. NQ net mean R > 0 with one-sided t ≥ z(0.05/N);
  2. SPY net mean R > 0;
  3. SPXUSD gross mean R > 0.
- **Threshold:** the ledger N is **198**, so alpha is 0.05/198 = 2.53e-4 and the bar is **z = 3.4781**.

### Choices fixed before any result was computed (also in the script docstring)

- **Session:** a weekday with at least one bar between 09:30 and 15:59 New York time. This is the same definition `gapfade-H0012` and `gamma-H0019` use.
- **09:35 bar:** it must exist, because the entry is its open. If it is missing, there is no trade.
- **Exit path:** the bars from 09:35 to 15:59, starting with the entry bar itself, since its high and low come after its open.
- **Stop fill:** for a long, the lower of the stop and the bar's open; for a short, the higher. If the 09:35 open were already past the stop, the trade would exit at that open. This never happened in any dataset (the count is printed as "entry already beyond stop: 0").
- **Target fill:** at the target price, with no price improvement.
- **NQ roll weeks:** days from `roll_excluded` (copied from `gapfade-H0012`) are never traded. That covers Monday to Friday of the week holding the 3rd Friday of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec, plus the following Monday.
- **t-statistic:** mean divided by sd/√n, one-sided.
- **Bootstrap p5:** the 5th percentile of the mean over 10,000 iid resamples, seed 0.
- **Trades per year:** trades divided by the calendar span of the sessions in the file.
- **NQ post-publication subsample:** trade dates from 2023-05-01 on.

### Future-truncation test

For **every** tradeable day (NQ 1,223, SPY 1,645, SPXUSD 2,090), the script recomputed the signal with all bars after 09:35 deleted. The signal means whether the day trades, the direction, the entry and the stop. In every case the result was identical to the full-data run.

The decision function is the same `signal()` the backtest uses. It reads only the 09:30–09:35 bars.

### Unit tests (`test_orb.py`, 8 passed)

- long signal and R;
- short mirror;
- no trade on a doji, a missing 09:30–09:34 bar, or a missing 09:35 bar;
- stop first when a bar touches both stop and target;
- a gap through the stop fills at the open (long and short);
- target fill and 15:59 close exit, with bars after 15:59 ignored;
- entry open already beyond the stop exits at entry;
- net R arithmetic and roll-day exclusion.

## Decision (pre-registered)

- **Condition 1, NQ net mean R > 0 with t ≥ 3.4781:** the net mean is −0.0052R and t is −0.08. **Not met.**
- **Condition 2, SPY net mean R > 0:** −0.0865R. **Not met.**
- **Condition 3, SPXUSD gross mean R > 0:** +0.0810R. Met.

**Result: FAIL.**

## Reading it plainly

- **Before costs:** there is a small positive drift in all three datasets, about +0.08 to +0.11R per trade. None of it is close to significant: gross t is 1.38 to 1.63, against a bar of 3.48.
- **After costs:** the 2 bp cost takes 0.12–0.23R per trade, because R is small (13–27 bp of price). That cost wipes out the drift everywhere.
- **Cost assumption does not decide it:** NQ's gross t (+1.63) also fails the bar. A cheaper futures cost would not change the verdict.
- **Post-publication NQ:** +0.015R net with t +0.15. That is no edge.
- **Shape of the returns:** the win rate is about 20–24%, with 75–80% of trades stopped out. Results swing year to year with no sign that one stays steady.
- **For prop use:** the fleet needs about +0.28R a trade at 3 trades a day, and more at 1 a day. This rule delivers roughly zero.

The family is closed under the pre-registration. Re-testing a variant would need a new ledger row, and with it a higher N.
