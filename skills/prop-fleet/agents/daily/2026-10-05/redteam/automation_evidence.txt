Red team 2026-10-05: evidence against "Bulenox, Lucid (Tier A) and Topstep XFA (Tier B) allow full automation on funded accounts".
All items are WebSearch summaries (search engine paraphrase), not fetched pages. Fetch attempts EGRESS_BLOCKED:
www.lucidtrading.com, www.mql5.com, blog.pickmytrade.io, pipback.com, www.track360.io.

1. Lucid: contract text requires PRIOR WRITTEN APPROVAL, at Lucid's sole discretion, for "any custom, algorithmic, or other
   automated trading software". Domain-restricted search (lucidtrading.com) attributed it once to the LucidDirect Account
   Agreement PDF (lucidtrading.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/LucidDirect-Account-Agreement-5-19.pdf, May 2025) and once to
   lucidtrading.com/lucid-trader-agreement/ and lucidtrading.com/terms-of-use/. Support page 11404728 still says automated
   systems are permitted. Conflict: the FAQ permits, the agreement requires approval. Which document governs LucidFlex is unknown.
   Lucid ToU also bans coordinated trading "between unconnected accounts and third parties, to pool or hedge risk".
   Lucid moves funded traders to LucidLive after 5 payouts (rules_matrix); automation terms for LucidLive not found.
2. Bulenox (bulenox.com, domain-restricted search): prohibits "scalping algorithms, DTC Protocol Bridge API or automated
   discretional trading in any trading environments"; "if abuse is suspected, Bulenox reserves the right to refuse to claim
   any profit". Third-party algorithms need management approval. After 3 Master payouts, move to Funded is at Bulenox's
   discretion, requires a separate Funded Account Agreement, and if the trader does not consent "the Master Account will be
   closed and no additional Reward will be issued" (bulenox.com/help-center/funded, /master). Automation terms of the
   Funded Account Agreement not found. Third-party reports (trustpilot.com reviews via search) of payouts denied on
   rules not in public docs ("Section 5.6 of the Master Agreement").
3. Topstep (help.topstep.com, domain-restricted search): call-up to Live is at the Risk team's discretion; the trader cannot
   decline and keep the XFA (move to Live or close); on call-up ALL XFAs close; the API is not available on Live, so automation
   is "not possible" there (articles 13747178, 10657969). Prohibited Conduct bans coordinated trading "including unconnected
   accounts or third parties ... to trade the same or opposite strategy simultaneously" (article 10296582).
4. Cross-firm: Tradeify bans the same bot at other firms (rules_matrix 2026-10-05). Industry tools for shared cross-firm
   detection exist (Centroid PropShield, tradeinformer.com; QuantSentry, fundedtrading.com). July 2026 ban waves at Tradeify
   and Lucid (pipback.com via search, single source).
