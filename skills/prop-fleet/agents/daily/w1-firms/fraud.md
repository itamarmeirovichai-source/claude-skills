### A2. 2024 MetaQuotes withdrawal (US-serving prop firms)
- **Early Feb 2024:** True Forex Funds said MetaQuotes terminated its MT4/MT5 licences, and it halted services (FM, 6 Feb 2024, page: financemagnates.com/forex/after-the-my-forex-funds-fiasco-true-forex-funds-stalls-operations/). CEO Richard Nagy blamed a third-party "equity synchronization" provider (snippet: fundedtrading.com/?p=8868 ⚠).
- **14 Feb 2024:** Funding Pips halted. CEO: "MetaQuotes decided to abruptly halt services due to active US accounts, with no prior notice". BlackBull was "forced to immediately shut down Funding Pips as a client" (FM, page: financemagnates.com/forex/exclusive-ftmo-halts-us-client-acquisition-amid-metaquotes-crackdown/).
- **18 Feb 2024:** FTMO stopped onboarding US clients, moved US accounts to DXtrade, and had open positions closed by the Friday close (same FM article, page). **MetaQuotes never publicly confirmed the policy** (same).
- **Effects:** a mass migration to cTrader, DXtrade and Match-Trader. Only 3 of 122 firms had a non-MT platform beforehand (same FM article, page). The platform shock then cascaded:
  - SurgeTrader lost Match-Trader as well and shut down on 24 May 2024 (FM, page: financemagnates.com/forex/prop-firm-surgetrader-shuts-down-a-week-after-losing-match-trader-license).
  - Purple Trading ended prop services, and Eightcap stopped MT4/MT5 for prop, which led to the closure of Funds For Traders (snippet; FM page via A4).
  - Many firms later re-added MetaTrader for non-US clients (snippet: financemagnates.com/forex/prop-trading-firms-renaissance-cutting-us-clients-integrating-new-trading-platforms).
- **Lesson:** platform or broker dependence is a single point of failure. A firm can be killed in days by a vendor decision, independent of its solvency.

## TASK B — Red-flag checklist (distilled from the cases above)
Each flag lists the case(s) where it showed up before the failure. Score a firm 1 point per flag. **≥3 = do not deploy a bot fleet; ≥5 = do not buy at all.**

**Payout and enforcement behaviour (strongest predictors)**
1. **Spike in payout denials citing vague "gambling", "windfall", "erratic", "one-sided" or "behavioural" reasons with no trade-level evidence.** Seen at TFT before its audit and pause (Mar 2024), at Apex 2024–25, and in the MFF allegation of "pretextual" terminations.
2. **"Internal audit" or "manual review" pause of all payouts.** TFT, 13 Mar 2024, followed by a full pause 2 weeks later.
3. **Retroactive rule changes that reduce already-earned profit.** FundingTicks, Dec 2025, then wind-down in Jan 2026. The Apex "No DCA" reinterpretation.
4. **Sudden "prohibited strategy" sweeps** (hedging, group trading, HFT, device/CID match) that hit many accounts at once. Seen in Maven's alleged 50,000-account "group trading" report (Aug 2025 ⚠), FundingPips and Blue Guardian CID bans, and Tradeify hedging bans. **This is the #1 bot-fleet killer: multi-account plus automation looks like "group trading" or "copy trading".**
5. **Interview or "verification call" gate added before payout**, including AI interviews. Seen at The5ers, Blue Guardian, Maven and FundedNext (user reports).
6. **Payout delays creeping past the advertised SLA** and KYC re-requests at payout time. Seen at TFT, SFT, MyFlashFunding and Seacrest's final payouts.
7. **Refunds limited to fees or vouchers on closure, with earned profits voided.** Alpha Futures (Jul 2026, activation-fee refunds only). Seacrest (3-week claim window).

**Business-model and financing signs**
8. **Aggressive discounts (≥50–90%) and "unlimited accounts" promotions.** The firm is running on fee inflow, which is a Ponzi-like dependence on new buyers (FSMA/CONSOB critique; Karma Prop Traders lasted 2 months).
9. **B-book with no disclosed LP, while marketing "we profit when you profit".** This was the core MFF allegation.
10. **Self-reported, unaudited "$X paid out" totals used as the main trust signal.** TFT claimed $150M; FundingTicks claimed $220M.
11. **Unpaid vendors and liquidity talk.** FPFX sued TFT over unpaid dues, after the firm had cited "liquidity issues". NinjaTrader vs Alpha Futures over an overdue balance.

**Corporate and platform**
12. **Single platform or broker dependency, especially MT white or grey label via an offshore broker.** TFF, Funding Pips, SurgeTrader and Funds For Traders were all killed by vendors.
13. **Opaque or shifting ownership, offshore re-domiciling mid-crisis, or no named beneficial owner.** TFT moved to the Cayman Islands. Ownership of TFT "remains unclear". Founder disputes at TFT.
14. **Affiliated "sister brands" under one holding company.** If one fails, the others often follow: Easton's TFT and SFT failed together; Funding Pips' sister FundingTicks wound down.
15. **CEO attacks complainants publicly** instead of producing evidence ("propaganda", TFT).
16. **Delisting by PropFirmMatch or a Trustpilot profile suspension.** TFT (PFM suspension), Alpha Futures (PFM delist), Funding Pips (Trustpilot, Jun 2024).
17. **Platform migration announced at short notice with "positions will be closed by Friday".** FTMO US, Feb 2024. This is operational rather than fraud, but a fleet killer.
18. **Regulatory exposure without a licence.** Any regulator action freezes everything for years whatever the merits: MFF assets were frozen from Aug 2023 to Dec 2025.
19. **Rapid serial rule changes (more than one material change per quarter),** especially to profit split and payout caps. MFFU cut its split from 90% to 80% (Feb 2026); E8 makes "frequent rule changes" (Propvator ⚠).
20. **Discretion clauses.** ToS language giving the firm unilateral discretion over "toxic", "unreasonable" or "non-commercial" strategies, applied after the fact. FundingTraders, The5ers ("uncommercial trading behavior") and FTMO ("overall trading behaviour").

**Operational rules for the client (derived)**
- Withdraw at every eligible payout; never let profit accumulate on any firm.
- Cap exposure per firm and per holding-company group.
- Archive ToS, FAQ and rule pages (web.archive.org) on the purchase date to fight retroactive changes.
- Keep a written pre-clearance from support for automation and multi-account use, by email, not chat.
