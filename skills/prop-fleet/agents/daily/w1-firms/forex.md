# D5: Due diligence on forex/CFD prop firms (worker: forex/CFD)
Access date for every source: **2026-10-11**. "page" means I read the page itself (WebFetch or curl). "snippet" means I saw only a search-engine or summary snippet. ⚠ marks a claim I could not confirm. Nothing was bought, no account was opened, and no firm was contacted.

## Method, blocks and gaps (read first)
- **Blocked hosts** (not routed around): IOSCO I-SCAN `iosco.org/i-scan` returned 403 for every firm name. These also returned 403: `e8markets.com` terms and help center, `alphacapitalgroup.uk` terms, and `fxify.com` FAQ and terms. `fundingpips.com/terms-and-conditions` returned 429. `web.archive.org` reset the connection. FundedNext's terms URL returned 404. For these firms the evidence comes from search snippets.
- **The session's WebSearch budget ran out** (200 calls, shared by all agents) before every gap was closed. The open gaps are listed under each firm. A follow-up run can close them.
- **Regulatory check, same for all firms:** I searched the general web for CFTC, FCA, ASIC, CNMV, CONSOB, SC Malaysia, FSMA and AMF actions against each firm, and ran an FCA warning-list search on fca.org.uk. Neither turned up an entry or enforcement action for any of the 13 firms (snippet). I could not check I-SCAN (403). **Industry context:** the CFTC sued My Forex Funds (a different firm) in 2023 over the "simulated prop firm" model. The case was dismissed in 2025 with sanctions against the CFTC, so the legality of the model is still untested ([financemagnates MFF](https://financemagnates.com/forex/mff-case-misconduct-embarrassment-for-the-cftc-but-not-yet-a-win-for-prop-trading), snippet; [barlaw.co.il](https://barlaw.co.il/wxte), snippet). In June 2026 Finance Magnates reported that up to about 100 prop firms closed between early 2024 and late 2025 after MetaQuotes revoked MT4/MT5 access ([FM 26 Jun 2026](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/funding-pips-pushes-traders-to-new-account-causing-online-grievance/), page; the URL serves an industry-overview article).
- **Every firm below runs simulated (demo) funded accounts.** The firm, or its affiliate, pays "rewards" out of its own revenue. Your counterparty is never a market.

## 1. FTMO: Grade A
**1. Regulatory and legal:** None found against FTMO. **Australia, 2024:** FTMO stopped serving Australians directly from 30 Sep 2024 and moved them to ASIC-licensed partner VRGK Tech Pty Ltd. Clients had to accept new terms or have their accounts closed (snippets: fundedtrading.com, proptraderedge). FTMO itself now **owns a regulated broker group**: it completed its purchase of OANDA from CVC on 1 Dec 2025, reportedly for about $422M with a $250M UniCredit-led loan (snippets: financemagnates, brokerchooser, ftmo.oanda.com press release).
**2. Payouts:** FTMO says it has paid "more than $450 million" (FM, 29 Sep 2025, page). Trustpilot is about 4.8/5 from about 42k reviews (snippet: trustpilot/propfirmmap). Complaints follow one pattern: a trader is labelled for "gambling" or "one-sided betting", is limited to 1% risk per trade, and is terminated after a payout request (snippets: Trustpilot, propfirmmatch). FTMO's own policy says traders are warned first and that ignoring warnings means "the reward will not be paid" (ftmo.oanda.com, snippet). I found no paused-payout period. PropFirmMap logged a change in payout frequency to bi-weekly / 14 trading days, undated ⚠, possibly April 2026.
**3. Corporate:** Founded 2015 in Prague by Otakar Šuffner (CEO) and Marek Vašíček (CTO) (FM, page). Legal entity: "FTMO s.r.o.", tax ID 03136752, from the ToS page JSON-LD. The ToS page carries "dateModified 2026-08-26". The full ToS is a linked PDF I did not read ⚠. Parent company: OHM (snippet). Platforms: MT4/MT5, cTrader, DXtrade. **MetaQuotes 2024:** FTMO cut MT4/MT5 for US residents from 19 Feb 2024 and moved them to DXtrade. It later returned to the US on MT5 through OANDA and is "the only prop firm currently offering MT5 to US traders" (FM, snippets).
**4. EA, accounts, swing:** "we have no reasons for limiting or restricting your trading strategy, whether it's discretionary trading, algorithmic trading, EAs, etc." (FAQ, page). On third-party EAs: "you potentially run a risk of being denied the FTMO Account if you exceed the maximum capital allocation rule" (page). Hard limit: EAs causing "more than 2,000 server requests per day" are banned (Forbidden Trading Practices, page). Accounts: "There is no limit to the number of accounts… the total capital allocation across all accounts is limited to $400,000 per trader or strategy". If "identically traded strategies are detected across multiple FTMO accounts" above the cap, the accounts may be suspended (FAQ, page). The cap rises to $600k with Prime and $1M with Premium, and to $2M through the Scaling Plan of +25% every 4 months (snippets, ftmo.com). **A swing account exists:** it has no restrictions on news or "holding positions over…" (FAQ, page).
**5. Rule changes 2024–26:** US move to DXtrade in Feb 2024. Forced Australian migration in Sep 2024. Payout cycle changed to 14 days ⚠ (2026). A 1-Step product was added, sharing the same $400k cap.
**6. Simulated?** Yes: "all accounts we provide to our clients are demo accounts with fictitious funds" (ToS page). Payouts are "Rewards" ("simulated trading experience with real money Rewards", page metadata). Counterparty: an FTMO entity ⚠ (inside the PDF).
**Grade A because:** 10+ year record; named owners; now owns the regulated OANDA group; EAs explicitly allowed on funded accounts; a swing account exists. **Main risk for the bot plan:** the $400k-per-strategy cap means one bot across many accounts is capped at $400k unless it scales, and "inappropriate behaviour" terminations are discretionary.

## 2. The5ers: Grade B
**1. Regulatory and legal:** None found (searched the web and FCA; snippet). The5ers temporarily blocked US traders in Feb 2024 (FM, snippet).
**2. Payouts:** Trustpilot is about 4.8/5 from about 23.8k reviews (propfirmmap, Apr 2026, snippet). Complaints are mostly about slow payouts (one case of 16 days after an admitted "system error") and trades not showing on the platform (July 2026) (Trustpilot snippets). In April 2026 payouts moved to bi-weekly / on demand (snippet).
**3. Corporate:** Founded 2016 in Israel; CEO Gil Ben Hur (snippets). ToS entities: "FIVE PERCENT ONLINE LTD, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 12553363" and "FIVE PERCENT ONLINE LTD, a company registered in Israel with company number 515864007" (ToS, page). Platforms: MT5, cTrader (added 2025), plus a separate futures arm.
**4. EA, accounts, swing:** The FAQ says any EA is allowed except listed categories, and the trader must own the source code (snippet). **But the T&C (page) say:** "User shall notify the Company in writing and obtain written approval from the Company prior to using any Automated Trading Software… approval shall be at the Company's sole discretion", "the Company may require advance testing", and "the Company prohibits use of any Automated Trading Software owned or developed by any third party". The Prohibited Practices page (updated 28 Jul 2026, page) bans "Trade coordination or copy trading with other traders **or accounts**" and EAs "that cause… an excessive number of server requests". Running one bot on many accounts may therefore count as "copy trading… with accounts" ⚠. Caps: Bootcamp allows max 4 active accounts. Futures has "$500K total per user across all programs". I found no firm-wide forex cap ⚠. High Stakes scales to $500k; Bootcamp and Hyper Growth to $4M (snippets). Swing: ⚠ not confirmed.
**5. Rule changes:** US exit Feb 2024; cTrader added 2025; payout schedule change Apr 2026. The T&C say rules "are subject to change at any time… with or without notice" (page).
**6. Simulated?** Yes: "any trading that You perform through the Evaluation is entirely simulated… the 'funds'… are fictitious". Payouts are a "Reward Balance" that can be forfeited. Rewards can be converted to non-refundable "Hub Credits" (ToS, page).
**Grade B because:** about 10-year record; named UK and Israeli entities; payouts mostly paid. But bots need written approval at the firm's discretion, and cross-account copying is banned.

## 3. FundedNext: Grade C
**1. Regulatory and legal:** None found (snippet). One trader on FPA says they complained to "Dubai authorities" about a refund (May 2026, snippet; unverified).
**2. Payouts:** Reported cumulative payouts are about $250M–$325M; figures vary by source (snippets). Complaints on PropFirmMatch and FPA cite HFT reclassification after a payout was approved, "one-sided betting" profit deductions, and copy-trading "similarity" terminations. Some reviewers report fast payouts (snippets). TheTrustedProp scores FundedNext Futures 60/100 (snippet).
**3. Corporate:** Founded 2022; CEO Abdullah Jayed (NEXT Ventures) (snippets). Entity is reported as GrowthNext F.Z.E., UAE; another source names "Funded Next Ltd" ⚠. Platforms: MT4, MT5, cTrader, Match-Trader. US CFDs were relaunched in 2026 (snippet ⚠).
**4. EA, accounts, swing:** Help center, last updated 8 Sep 2026 (page): EAs are allowed on MT4/MT5 **only below $50k**. "Traders on accounts of $50,000 and above must trade fully manually." No EAs at all on cTrader or Match-Trader. EAs need a paid add-on: "this fee is non-refundable". "There is a maximum allocation limit of $300,000 for each EA or trading bot's strategy." EAs "designed specifically to pass Prop firm challenges" are banned. Swing: ⚠.
**5. Rule changes:** The $50k manual-only rule and the EA add-on fee (2025–26). The firm says "Existing users remain unaffected" by the add-on change (snippet ⚠). Existing cTrader accounts are grandfathered (snippet).
**6. Simulated?** Yes ⚠. I could not read the T&C (404); the snippets describe simulated accounts and "rewards".
**Grade C because:** short record (2022); offshore UAE entity that is not clearly identified; a bot fleet would be stuck on sub-$50k accounts behind a paid add-on; recurring HFT and copy-trading payout disputes.

**2. Payouts:** Trustpilot about 3.5/5 from about 605 reviews (fi.trustpilot, snippet). **5. Rule changes:** Platform churn (moved off MetaTrader, then back) ⚠. Consistency rules on Instant and Flex.
**6. Simulated?** ⚠ (terms page 404). Treated as simulated.
**Grade D because:** fully automated entry EAs are not clearly allowed; a hard cap of 3 forex accounts that must each use a different strategy and asset; post-hoc risk-rule payout cuts.

## 8. Funding Pips: Grade C
**1. Regulatory and legal:** None found (snippet). Its futures arm, FundingTicks, "applied retroactive rule changes including a one-minute minimum trade hold to existing accounts" in late Dec 2025 (FM, snippet).
**2. Payouts:** Reported totals are $167M–$273M (snippets). Complaints include a "shared CID" denial after one paid payout, device-ID bans later reversed on manual review, and KYC refused because a third party (Rise) declined verification (FPA, snippets). **Paused payouts:** in Feb 2024 broker Blackbull cut off MT access under MetaQuotes pressure. Trading stopped on 14 Feb 2024 and payouts were pushed back about a week during the blackout (FM/fxnewsgroup, snippet). There was also a separate DDoS disruption (FM, snippet).
**3. Corporate:** Founded 2022; owner, CEO and chair Khaled Ayesh; Dubai (snippets). The legal entity is **unclear**. Sources name Funding Pips Services Ltd (Mwali/Comoros), Funding Pips LLC, Ankh Prop FZCO, FundingPips Education FZCO and a Cyprus company HE 450941 ⚠. Platforms: Match-Trader (Feb 2024), then cTrader, then MT5 back about 2025. Has its own regulated broker "Tradin" (FM, page).
**4. EA, accounts, swing:** "If the EA is your own, developed by you, full automation is permitted with proof of ownership". The proof is source code, version control or a live call, and "The Responsible Trading Team has discretion". "A compiled binary on its own is not proof". Third-party EAs may only manage trades or risk (help center, snippet). Cap: all accounts share "a single Max Allocation of $400K" (up to $600k via PRIME) (snippet).
**5. Rule changes (serious):** **The PRIME migration (2026).** The T&Cs effective 15 May 2026 made PRIME "mandatory" when the firm invites a trader, although the CEO had said on 5 Jun 2026 it "is and will remain optional". About 70% of the about-100-trader pool was migrated. The pending payout becomes the PRIME starting balance (×12.5), and "the reward becomes the maximum loss limit" (FM 22 Sep 2026, page). Rules removed for **new** purchases from 28 Sep 2026; legacy accounts keep the old rules (snippet).

## Cross-firm red flags for a fully automatic multi-account bot fleet
1. **Per-strategy and per-person caps defeat "many accounts, one bot".** FTMO caps at $400k per *strategy*, FundedNext at $300k per EA strategy, E8 at $500k per market per *household*, Funding Pips at $400k across all accounts, FXIFY at about $785k. FTUK allows 3 accounts with different assets; Blue Guardian allows 1 per level. The same bot on many accounts is treated as one strategy or as "copy trading".
2. **Bots are banned or gated in the fine print:** Maven, Alpha, FundingTraders, FTUK (risk-management EAs only) and FundedNext (accounts of $50k and above). The5ers' T&C require **prior written approval**. Funding Pips requires proof of ownership judged at the firm's discretion.
3. **Every firm here runs simulated accounts and pays discretionary rewards.** There is no profit-share claim on real P&L. Firms such as The5ers, Maven and Audacity can change rules "with or without notice", and accrued rewards can be forfeited.
4. **Retroactive or forced changes have happened:** the FundingPips PRIME mandatory migration (2026), FundingTicks retroactive rules (Dec 2025), FTMO's forced Australian move (2024), and the 2024 MetaQuotes cut-off that took Funding Pips and others offline.
5. **Weak jurisdictions:** most firms contract through UAE free-zone, Saint Lucia or Comoros/Anjouan entities. The only regulated broker groups found are FTMO (owns OANDA) and, partly, Funding Pips (Tradin) and FXIFY (FXPIG).
