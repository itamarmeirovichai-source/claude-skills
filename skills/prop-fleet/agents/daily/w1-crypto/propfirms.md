## 3. Notes for a bot fleet
- **Multiple accounts are the main constraint, more than bot permission.** HyroTrader allows one account per program type (T&C §4.5). FTMO caps $400k per *strategy*, so identical bots are aggregated. FundedNext caps $300k per EA strategy. CFT caps $300k per user. Propr caps $300k aggregate. Breakout caps $200k combined.
- **Copying the same bot across your own accounts** is explicitly allowed at Propr, Velotrade (3p), The5ers, GFT and Blue Guardian. It is restricted at CFT (only Break evaluation accounts) and HyroTrader (no mirroring). Cross-account *hedging* is banned everywhere.
- **Funding rates are real costs** at Propr (Hyperliquid funding passed through), CFT-Bybit, Breakout and Velotrade (overnight funding), and per HyroTrader's T&C. CFD firms charge swaps instead.
- **"Own strategy" clauses** (HyroTrader, The5ers, GFT, Fintokei, Alpha) can include source-code ownership or disclosure demands (The5ers, GFT, Alpha).
- Funded crypto at FTMO Standard cannot be held over the weekend. Use Swing, or the Evaluation phase, where weekend holding is allowed.
