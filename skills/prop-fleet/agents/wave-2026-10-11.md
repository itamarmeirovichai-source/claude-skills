# Wave 1 (from 11 Oct 2026): out of the box, four departments in parallel

Itamar asked for this on 11 Oct: run research without stopping, build a hierarchy
(department heads, team leads, workers, verifiers), and aim for at least 20% a year,
more if possible. The two rules in ORG.md still cannot change: the system must be
fully automatic, and many funded accounts must be buyable on it.

## Why the frame changes

From H0001 to H0022, every index-intraday futures idea we tested on S&P, SPY and NQ
failed. The best of them had a gross edge of about Sharpe 1 to 2, and costs ate it.
Three assumptions held us back. Each one can be changed:

1. **Futures only.** Forex, CFD and crypto prop firms (FTMO, The5ers, FundedNext,
   crypto firms and others) often allow expert advisors and bots. They also allow
   large total allocations.
2. **No overnight holding.** Swing-type accounts at forex and CFD firms allow
   overnight and weekend holding. That opens multi-day trend-following and carry, the
   only edge we have measured (trend-following on 54 futures, Sharpe 1.17). Static
   drawdown and no evaluation clock at some firms may make a Sharpe of about 1 enough.
3. **The denominator.** 20% a year can be measured on the money Itamar actually
   spends (fees plus any capital), not on the notional size of the accounts. At most
   firms the only money at risk is the fee.

## Shared rules (same as ORG.md, plus these)

- **Pre-registration.** Before any backtest, write the exact rules as rows in
  `agents/prereg/<dept>.csv`, using the same columns as `ledger.csv`, with ids
  `<DEPT>-01` and so on. Commit and push that file **before** running anything; the
  commit order is the proof. Count every variant you run.
- **Budget.** Each department may count at most **15** variants in this wave.
- **The bar for the whole wave is fixed now.** N = 200 (the ledger as of 11 Oct) + 60
  (the wave budget) = 260. That gives a one-sided z(0.05/260) of **3.5504**. A result
  passes only if the out-of-sample net mean has t ≥ 3.5504 **and** the out-of-sample
  bootstrap p5 is > 0 **and** the look-ahead and truncation tests pass. A
  department that uses fewer variants still uses this bar.
- **Out of sample** means a period, or a set of markets, that was never used to
  choose rules. Write that split down in the pre-registration.
- **Costs.** Use realistic costs (spread plus commission plus swap for forex/CFDs;
  taker fees plus spread for crypto) and repeat at 2× cost. The worse of the two is
  reported next to the main result.
- **Network blocks.** If a host is blocked, report it and move on. Never route around
  a block.
- **Data.** Never commit data files over 1 MB.
- **Firm contact.** Never buy, sign up, contact a firm, or move money.
- **Every number** must trace to code that was run or to a cited source page.
- **Output.**
  - `references/w1-<dept>.md` in plain English, with a one-line verdict at the top.
  - Scripts in `agents/daily/w1-<dept>/`.
  - Your prereg csv, filled with results.
  - Push to your branch. No pull request.

## Itamar's decision, 11 Oct: the denominator

The 20% is measured on the **size of the funded account**. A $100,000 funded account
must pay out at least $20,000 a year, net to Itamar, after the profit split, all fees
and resets. Item 3 under "Why the frame changes" is replaced by this. Every department
reports its result against this target: the yearly payout per funded account, divided
by the account size, averaged across a fleet that includes the accounts that fail.

## D5 Firm due diligence and fraud risk (added 11 Oct)

Branch: claude/w1-firms.

Many prop firms have:

- refused payouts;
- changed rules after the fact;
- closed down (MyForexFunds after the 2023 CFTC case, True Forex Funds and others);
- banned traders for "prohibited strategies" that appear only in fine print.

D5 checks, for every candidate firm:

- regulatory actions;
- payout proof (independent, not marketing);
- Trustpilot and Reddit complaint patterns, especially payout denials and account
  closures;
- years in business, owner and jurisdiction;
- the exact clauses on bots/EAs, copy trading, "gambling", news trading, consistency
  rules and IP/VPS;
- what happens to funded accounts if the firm changes its broker or platform.

The output is a risk grade (A to D) for each firm with citations, and a final list of
firms safe enough to run a fleet on.

# Wave 2 (started 11 Oct, 22:55 UTC, while D1 finishes)

## What wave 1 taught us

- **D2.** Forex/CFD trend and carry were dead out of sample from 2013 to 2026.
- **D3.** Crypto trend was mostly beta.
- **D4.** Intraday rules across 19 markets lose after costs, and those markets act like only about 8.
- **D5.** The safe list is FTMO, MyFundedFutures, Lucid, Topstep and TradeDay: about 20 to 25 futures accounts, plus one FTMO strategy capped at $400k.

## The bar for wave 2

N = 270: 225 rows in the ledger, D1's rows, and a budget of 15 for each of the 2 departments, rounded up. That gives a one-sided z(0.05/270) of **3.5612**. Every other rule is unchanged from wave 1.
