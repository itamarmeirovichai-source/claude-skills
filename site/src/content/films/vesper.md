**After dusk.** A gentle push-in on the bottle while the sun goes down behind it. Our first attempt orbited the bottle, and the glass shape drifted. So we kept the camera to a slow push and let the sky do the moving.

- **Frames:** start and end keyframes fix the light, the palette and the camera path.
- **The take:** one shot, start frame to end frame, 30 seconds. No cuts.
- **Finish:** music and sound effects, the spec label, then the 9:16 and 4:5 versions.
