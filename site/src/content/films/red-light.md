**Red Light.** A cold open on a hand passing a gold can between two cars, then the chase that led there: a burnout, a handbrake turn, a cruiser on the getaway car's tail. At the red light the getaway car stops, and so does the cruiser. The driver hands the officer an AURUM. Green light, and they pull away together. "Some things are worth stopping for."

- **The hook:** motion in frame 0 (the hand-off, a 4x punch-in) and a line on screen from 0.08 s: "Worth stopping for."
- **The turn:** a car chase that stops for a red light.
- **The punchline:** the can ends the chase. Without the product there is no joke.
- **The edit:** 9 shots with hard cuts, plus a cold open, a packshot and an offer card. 30 s, 9:16, with 15 s and 4:5 cuts.

### What our QC caught

- **Fixed in the re-cut (v4, editing only):** a 2.9 s frozen frame in the middle of the chase; the product first readable at 19.5 s and only 4 % of the frame (now 0.67 s, label from the product photo); subtitles sitting under the platform buttons (moved into the safe area); no packshot at the end.
- **Still in the film, and we say so:** in the interior shot Vee has a second stopwatch on her lapel; the cruiser stops beside the car, not behind it; the chase shots are 480p detail, upscaled. Fixing them means new renders, so they are noted, not hidden.
- **Policy:** no weapons. The police imagery is fine for organic posts; a paid-ads cut would need a version without it (Meta and TikTok rules).

### QC sheet

- **Sound:** −14.0 LUFS integrated, true peak −1.3 dBTP, measured on the H.264 file this page plays (two-pass loudness, then measured again after encoding).
- **Sync:** the chase sound was generated with the picture, so each hit stays on its own frame. Each flagged onset was checked by eye on an 8 fps strip; one tyre screech lags its stop by about 6 frames, a generator limit we note rather than hide.
- **Frozen frames:** none longer than 6 frames.
- **Lips:** no on-screen dialogue; the tagline is a voice-over over a frame with no visible mouth.
- **Text:** every super at most 7 words, inside the 9:16 safe area, on screen for at least (words ÷ 3) + 1 s.
- **Disclosure:** "AI-generated film" on screen at the open and on the end card.
