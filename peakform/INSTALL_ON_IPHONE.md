# Install PeakForm on iPhone

Address: <https://itamarmeirovichai-source.github.io/claude-skills/peakform/>

A QR code for the same address is in `docs/install-qr.png`.

1. Open the address in **Safari** on the iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open **PeakForm** from the new Home Screen icon.
5. Tap **Restore a backup or private setup file**, choose `peakform-private-setup.json`, keep **Use file** selected, and tap **Import**. Or tap **Set up** and fill in the short first run instead.
6. In **More, Schedule and reminders**, check the times.
7. In **More, Calendar export**, tap **Create calendar file**, then **Add to Calendar**, then **Add All**.
8. Leave notifications off. PeakForm does not use push notifications. Apple Calendar gives the alarms.
9. In **More, Backup and data**, create your first backup and save it to Files or iCloud Drive.

## What has to happen on the phone

These steps need the phone itself and cannot be done from a computer: steps 1 to 5, adding the calendar in step 7, and saving the backup in step 9.

## Good to know

- Use PeakForm from the Home Screen icon, not a Safari tab. Safari can clear a tab's data after about a week without a visit.
- The first open needs internet. After that, PeakForm works offline.
- When a new version is ready, a small **Update** bar appears at the top. Tap it.
