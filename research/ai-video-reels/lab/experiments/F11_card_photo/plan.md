## Brand and product (invented)
- **Brand:** HOLLOW & HOUND. A web search on 2026-10-11 found no pet brand by that name (only an unrelated 2018 novel, *The Haunting of Hounds Hollow*).
- **Product:** the "First Frost" embroidered bow-tie dog collar.
  - Deep forest-green cotton twill bow tie: two puffy wings and a wrapped centre knot.
  - Matching forest-green twill collar strap, polished gold-tone D-ring and gold-tone metal side-release buckle.
  - **Motif (locked, identical in every frame):** each wing carries exactly one small gold-thread pine tree in the middle, flanked by two small ivory six-point snowflakes. The centre knot is plain green, with no embroidery. Total: 2 trees and 4 snowflakes.
- **Dog (locked):** one adult Pembroke Welsh Corgi, red-and-white coat, white chest bib and white blaze, upright ears. One dog in every frame.

## Step 2: creative thinking
1. **Selling truth:** a holiday collar is bought to be photographed. This one makes your dog the picture everyone keeps.
2. **Hook in second 1, sound-first:** a camera flash pops (charge whine, then a hard *pop-clack*) on a tight frame of the dog's chest and bow tie. This is a ≤1.2 s flash-forward of the product beat. The product is moving (the dog's head tilts and the bow shifts), and it fills ≥30 % of the frame height.
3. **Turn and punchline, caused by the product:** at a family holiday-card shoot, the handheld photographer keeps zooming in until every human is cropped out and only the dog in the bow tie remains. The printed card on the fridge is just the dog. **Without the bow tie, the photographer has no reason to crop the family out, so the joke dies. The product test passes.**
4. **Format:** comedy, multi-shot with 7 hard cuts. The joke escalates through cuts (doc 44), and the held punchline is the joke (doc 43 §7.5).
5. **Policy:** no weapons, no police, no claims. The dog is never presented as evidence ("dogs love it"); it is a gag (doc 52 §0.6). No AI faces: humans are hands, laps and legs only. The film carries an AI-disclosure super.

Alternatives considered (owner already picked "Card Photo"):
- **"Group Chat":** phones buzz as the card is shared and everyone crops the family out. Rejected because it needs phone UI (doc 62 rule).
- **"Re-gift":** the bow tie is the only gift nobody returns. Rejected because the product doesn't cause the joke strongly enough.
