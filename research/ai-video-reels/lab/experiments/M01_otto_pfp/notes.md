## Pick

Why: hair-textured (not rubber) moustache, centred, and the plain charcoal background with one oxblood glow gives the cleanest silhouette at 110 px. f1_velvet is the runner-up; its curtain is busier behind the moustache tips.
Exploration: v6_oxblood_velvet won (real strand texture). v1/v3/v4/v8 had plastic or rubber moustaches, v2 a prop-like one, and v5 had a blond wig and cartoon hands. v7 was good but has a stray bulb.

## Learnings
- Soul Cinema honoured "centred, square frame" for portraits (it usually goes off-centre).
- Moustache material is the main failure: 5/8 Soul takes looked like moulded rubber. Writing "real hair: individual waxed strands, stray hairs" in the Flare pass fixed it.
- Flare with one Soul still as identity ref kept the face and wardrobe and improved the skin and hair texture.
