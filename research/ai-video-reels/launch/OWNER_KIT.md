**How to write the comment** (it must be genuine; it's never a pitch):
- About **the post itself**: something visible in it, with a point of view or a real question. 8–20 words.

- One comment per account. Don't reply-chain or tag anyone.
- Shapes to adapt to what the post actually shows (don't paste these as they are):
  - Product detail: "The embroidery on the cuff reads even at thumbnail size. Is that thread or a woven patch?"
  - Behind the scenes: "Shooting the whole flavour line on one counter is a smart way to keep the colours honest."
  - Launch: "Didn't expect strawberry and rose to work in a can. Which one sells out first?"
- If you can't say something true and specific about a post, skip the comment and just follow. A fake comment is worse than none.

## 4. Send-day checklist (per lead, before the owner presses send)

Tick every line. One "no" means don't send that lead today.

**The lead (doc 63 §7.1)**
- [ ] The Meta Ad Library (US, active, the brand's page) shows active ads. 0 → drop the lead to WARM.
- [ ] The product named in the message is public: it's in the site navigation, on IG or in press, and in stock. It isn't a hidden or unreleased SKU.
- [ ] No back-end or stale data in the message: no ad counts, no JSON dates, no job posts, no revenue.

**The message (doc 63 §3)**
- [ ] It's the approved text for this lead, word for word (Foggy: doc 63 §4.1; Moment: §4.2 with no coupe, no hangover, calm, mood or health words; the others: the new 20-lead file).
- [ ] It names the sender ("Itamar from VXO"), has one visible observation, the idea with a product-caused turn, "AI" once next to what stays real (when the message mentions AI), "5 free stills", and a two-yes CTA.
- [ ] 35–55 words for a DM. No link, no image, no price, no superlatives.
- [ ] It doesn't share three sentences with any other message sent this week.

**Timing and volume**
- [ ] Tue–Thu, 8–10 am in the founder's time zone.
- [ ] This is DM number ≤ 10 today, at least 5 minutes after the previous one.
- [ ] Emails only with the real postal-address footer (doc 63 §4.0). Otherwise no email.

**Right after sending**
