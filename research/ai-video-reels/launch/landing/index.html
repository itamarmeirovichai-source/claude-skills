FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you use AI?

Yes. AI is part of our production, the way a post house uses CGI: it lets us build new scenes, worlds and variations around your product in days instead of weeks. Strategy, scripting, editing and QA are done by a person. Your real product is locked from your own photos and label files, and any AI-generated people or scenes are disclosed using Meta’s and TikTok’s AI labels. We never hide it.

Will my product look right?

That is our first QA gate. We work from your packaging photos and label files, check every frame of every hero shot for label accuracy, and composite your real logo or packshot in the edit where needed. If a label is wrong, the ad doesn’t ship.

Do you make fake testimonials or AI “customers”?

No. We don’t create reviews or testimonials from people who don’t exist, and we don’t present AI people as real customers. That is both a trust problem and a legal one (FTC rule 16 CFR Part 465). If an ad uses a presenter, it’s a disclosed presenter, not a “happy customer.” Real customer quotes come only from you, with their consent. You also won’t find invented client logos or reviews on this site.

What do you need from us?

Product photos (front, back, 45°), your label or packaging files if you have them, your logo, a list of approved claims, 10–20 of your best reviews, and access to (or screenshots of) your current top ads. That’s it.

Who owns the ads?

You do, once paid. You get full usage rights for paid and organic use. We may show the work privately to other prospects only if you say yes in writing.

Can you make claims about results, before/after, or health benefits?

Only claims you supply and can substantiate. We don’t generate before/after results, body transformations, or medical claims. Supplements, skincare and health products get an extra claims check against your approved language.

Do you run the ads?

The sprint is creative only; your team or media buyer runs it. If you don’t have one, ask us about a launch add-on with a partner media buyer.

Why not just use an AI ad tool ourselves?

You can, and for some brands that’s the right call. Tools give you clips. What moves numbers is the angle, the hook, the edit, and a product that looks exactly like yours. That’s the part we sell.

Where are you based?

Boca Raton, Florida. We work with brands across the US, remotely, and we speak English and Hebrew.
