# HOT_NEW_30: 30 new leads ranked by heat score

Prepared 2026-10-08. **Nothing has been sent.** None of these brands appear in `PROSPECTS_50.md` or `HEAT_50.md`.

**Scoring.** Same 100-point rubric as HEAT_50: ads 30, creative need 20, hiring 15, momentum 15, budget proxy 10, reach 10.
- **Ads.** Meta Ad Library, US, active ads, exact-phrase search, scanned with a headless browser on 2026-10-08. Data: `hot/ads*.jsonl` and `hot/adsrun*.txt`.
  - "Results" means the Library's keyword total. "Cards" means the cards whose advertiser name matched the brand. "Video" means the same search filtered to video.
  - The video/all ratio is how creative need was scored.
  - Where the scan showed nothing, the entry says "ads: unverified" and no claim is made.
- **Hiring.** Not re-checked for this batch, so every brand scores 0–3 here. These scores are slightly conservative next to HEAT_50.
- **Momentum.** Uses `/products.json` publish and create dates from each brand's own Shopify store.
- **Budget proxy.** An estimate from assortment, price points and ad volume. It is not a reported revenue figure.
- **Fit flags.** Brands that may sit above the $1–20M band, or that aren't clearly founder-led, are flagged. Vet them before sending.

**Sending rules** (as in EMAILS_50 v2 and the 28/34 playbooks):
- Email 1 carries no link.
- `{PORTFOLIO_URL}` goes on the Day 3 reply and `{CONCEPT_FRAME_LINK}` on the Day 7 reply. Templates are at the bottom.
- Every email ends with the `{BUSINESS_ADDRESS}` footer.
- The CTA is always a two-option yes/yes. There is no "if you're not interested" exit.
- Don't make health or performance claims about any product. Kosterina's and Bask & Lather's own ads use health language, so stay on taste and texture for them.
- Email sending is on hold, so use the DM version first wherever an IG handle exists.
