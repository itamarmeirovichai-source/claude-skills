## Scoring rubric (transparent)

| Signal | Max | How points were given |
|---|---|---|
| Ads running now | 30 | Ad Library verified 2026-10-08: ≥15 cards scores 26–28, 8–14 scores 20–22, 3–7 scores 14–18. A stale brandmov sample showing ≥1 live ad scores 10–15, plus 3 if one ad has run ≥90 days (fatigue). Unverified ads score by pixels: Meta+TikTok 5–8, Meta only 4–5, no pixel found 1–2. |
| Creative need | 20 | Verified static or no video scores 16–19. Mixed, or long cuts not built for hooks, 11–14. Already video- or creator-heavy, 8–9. Unverified ads score 6–12, based on site imagery and stated needs. |
| Hiring / content need | 15 | An open content, social or marketing role scores 12–15. A creator or ambassador program, an influencers@ inbox, a creator campaign or a role seen but unverified scores 5–8. Open careers pages with only non-content roles score 2–4. Nothing found scores 0–1. |
| Momentum | 15 | New SKUs in the last 90 days (created after 2026-07-10 in the Shopify feed), Q4 sets, launches, new doors, press and raises. |
| Budget proxy | 10 | Revenue band from `PROSPECTS_50.md` × price × assortment: $15M+ scores 10, $10–20M 9, $5–15M 8, $3–8M 7, $1–5M 5, under $1M 1–3. |
| Reachability | 10 | A direct founder or marketing email plus a founder who is publicly active scores 8–9. A generic inbox plus a named founder 5–7. A contact form, phone or DM only 3–4. |

## Sources and limits (what could NOT be verified)

- **Meta Ad Library (direct).** `facebook.com/ads/library` returned **HTTP 403** with a JS bot challenge on 2026-10-08, both by curl and by WebFetch. We did not try to get around the challenge. The GraphQL and async endpoints sit behind the same wall. Counts for 9 brands come from the 2026-10-08 Ad Library checks recorded in `PROSPECTS_50.md` (#2, 3, 14, 15, 16, 33, 34, 35, 49).
- **Brandmov.** The route from `23-what-actually-sells.md` still works for its public `/watch/<brand>/` pages, but it covers only brands it tracks. 8 of our 50 had pages: Mingle, Plum Deluxe, Maison Louis Marie, Copper Cow, Cusa, Sanzo, Bones and Weezie. Each page holds a **1-ad sample** with a brandmov fetch date of May–Jul 2026, so it is stale and not a full count. Its free "Competitor Ads Lookup" API (`api.brandmov.com/api/v1/tools/competitor-ads-lookup/search`) returns **401 without an account**, so it was not used. The other 42 brands return 404 on brandmov.
- **TikTok.** Creative Center top-ads API returned `40101 no permission`. The TikTok Ad Library API rejected our requests (HTTP 421 "params error"), and it covers EU-served ads only. **No TikTok ad data for any brand.**
- **adlibrary.com and Motion.** adlibrary.com brand pages show placeholder ads with no dates or counts. The Motion Creative Analytics connector has no workspace for this account.
- **Video vs static split.** Verified only for the 9 Ad Library brands above. For everyone else it is unknown.
- **Hiring.** Hiring was checked on company careers pages and with web search. We did not use logged-in LinkedIn, Indeed or Wellfound. No brand had a verified open content, video, social or paid-social role on 2026-10-08. The AZL Digital Marketing Manager and the Bones eCommerce Manager listings are unverified aggregator or snippet hits. The Brooklyn Candle Studio social media coordinator page is now 404.
- **UGC platforms.** We found no public Billo or Insense brand listings and no "#ad creator wanted" posts for any of the 50. This was not searchable without logins.
- **IG growth and founder DM responsiveness.** Not measurable from public pages without logging in. Reachability is scored on published emails and on how publicly active the founder is.
- **Momentum dates** come from each store's Shopify `products.json` `created_at` (or `published_at` where noted). These are reliable for "new SKU" but are not launch announcements.
