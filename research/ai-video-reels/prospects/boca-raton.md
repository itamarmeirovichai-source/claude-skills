## 0. How to read this

- **Score (0–100)** estimates how likely the business is to *pay a local solo creator within 30–60 days*. It combines budget (20%), urgency and timing (25%), decision speed / single decision-maker (20%), fit with hybrid or cinematic AI (20%), and low regulatory risk plus few entrenched vendors (15%).
- **Follower counts:** Instagram blocks automated reads, so counts are **not verified**. Handles are marked **(confirmed)** when they were linked from the business's own website, otherwise **(verify)**. Spend 2 minutes on each before outreach. Also check the Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library, search the page name, country = US) to see whether they run paid ads right now.
- **Public business info only.** Names are taken from the business's own site or press coverage.
