## How to read this

**Scoring (0-5 per signal, max 40):**

| Code | Signal |
|---|---|
| Ads | Actively running Meta/TikTok ads |
| Vol | High creative volume need (DTC/Amazon/Shopify, new SKUs, retail launches) |
| Fund | Recent funding or retail expansion (2025-2026) |
| Size | Small/mid size (5 = small and reachable, 1 = near-enterprise) |
| Gap | Weak or repetitive current video creative = room for you |
| UGC | Active on IG/TikTok with UGC/creators |
| Hire | Hiring creative strategist / UGC / content / creative roles |
| AI | Openness to AI content |

**Tie-break:** Size + Gap first (a solo creator is likelier to win there), then Hire, then alphabetical.

**Evidence quality, read this before outreach:**
- **Ad volume:** Motion's public library pages (`motionapp.com/library/<brand>`) and the US Meta Ad Library, read 2026-10-05. Where neither had data, the Ads score is marked *(unv.)* = unverified/inferred. Open the Meta Ad Library for the brand before sending anything.
- **AI openness:** none of these brands showed public evidence of AI-generated creative. Every **AI** score is an inference from creative velocity and team culture, not a confirmed fact.
- **Hiring:** confirmed only where a posting URL is given. Many careers pages could not be read.
- **TikTok Creative Center** could not be checked; TikTok ad volume is unverified for all brands.

**Positioning warning:** AI ads draw public backlash (e.g., Coca-Cola holiday spots). Zevia runs explicitly anti-AI ads ("real soda for real humans"), so it was excluded. Lead with **"more tested variations per week, faster launch creative"**, not "AI ads". Disclose the AI process in the proposal; don't make it the headline.

## Outreach playbook (applies to all)

1. **Verify before sending:** open the brand's Meta Ad Library page and note 2-3 real ads, so the email references something specific and current.
2. **Make the spec first:** a 15s 9:16 spec built from the brand's real can or pack photo. Use only claims printed on the brand's own site and label. Don't use a celebrity's likeness (De Soi, Bero, UPDATE).
3. **Offer a small paid pilot:** e.g., 10 variations for a flat fee, and they keep the winners. That's easier to approve than a retainer.
4. **Disclose the AI method in the proposal** (rights, likeness and platform-policy safety), but lead with business outcomes: speed, volume, testing.
5. **Alcohol and NA-spirits brands:** keep spec ads 21+ compliant (no minors, no implied intoxication or performance claims).
6. **Follow-up cadence:** day 0 email, day 3 IG DM with the spec clip, day 7 LinkedIn note to the named exec, then stop.
