## 0. How to read this

**Scoring (0–5 each, max 40):**

| Code | Signal |
|---|---|
| PS | Paid-social volume (active Meta ads, TikTok Shop activity) |
| CV | Creative-volume need (new creatives shipped per week vs. team size) |
| FL | Fresh money / launch in 2025–26 (funding, PE deal, new category, retail rollout) |
| SZ | Size fit for a solo creator (higher = small/mid, lean team, no huge in-house studio) |
| WK | Weak / repetitive creative (higher = more obvious gap AI can fill) |
| HC | Hiring creative / content / motion roles (2025–26 evidence) |
| AI | Openness to AI / suitability of the category to AI visuals (pets, devices, sensory products score high; claim-heavy categories score lower) |
| MG | Price point & margins that support constant ad spend |

**Main evidence source for ad volume:** Motion's public "Inspo Library" brand pages (`motionapp.com/library/<brand>`). Each page states the number of active Meta ads, approximate new creatives per week, and format mix. Pages say "refreshed 4 months ago", so treat the numbers as a **mid-2026 snapshot**. Re-check the live [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) before every pitch.

**Honesty notes**
- Scores are analyst judgement built on the cited evidence, not measured performance data.
- "(unverified)" means I could not confirm the item from a primary source.
- Named people appear only where press or company pages publicly name them in that role. Confirm on LinkedIn before outreach.
- Contact emails are **not** verified unless marked. The default path is LinkedIn plus the brand's site contact, partnerships or careers page.

**Excluded after checking** (too big, acquired, or not US):
- **Grüns:** acquired by Unilever, completed June 1, 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%BCns)).
- **Hiya:** sold for $260M in September 2025 ([Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/hiya-health-sold-for-260-million)).
- **Kitsch:** over $500M revenue and fully self-funded, so very large ([search summary via Fortune profile](https://www.fortune.com/2024/07/01/kitsch-viral-mutigenerational-haircare-brand-founder-ceo-cassandra-thurswell/)).
- **Wonderskin:** HQ in London ([BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/beauty-brand-wonderskin-lands-50-million-series-a)).
- **Bioma:** Lithuania ([Kilo case](https://kilo.co/?p=935)).
- **PetLab Co:** over $200M and majority-owned by BC Partners.
- **Quince, Medicube, Tarte:** giants or non-US.
- **Primal Queen:** 2K+ Meta ads but HQ unverified. Keep on a watchlist.

## 5. Execution notes

1. **Order of attack:**
   - Brands: Arrae, Create, Native Pet, Solawave, Oak & Luna, Snif, Dossier, Other Half.
   - Agencies: TubeScience, Collier Simon, inBeat.
   - Ten specs at about 2 hours each is a realistic week.
2. **Before each email:** open the live Meta Ad Library for the brand and screenshot 3 current ads, then reference one in the email ("your 'X' ad…").
3. **Compliance rules for every spec:**
   - **Product accuracy:** real packaging must come from reference images.
   - **No AI before/afters, bodies or "results".**
   - **No AI children's faces.**
   - **No competitor trademarks.**
   - Supplements: use only the brand's own claim language.
   - Disclose AI use to the client, and follow Meta/TikTok AI-label rules.
4. **Re-verify flagged items:** HQs marked "unverified", named executives, and Motion numbers (mid-2026 snapshot) before outreach.
