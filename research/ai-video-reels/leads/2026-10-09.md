## What verification taught the lead process (for the next run)
1. **Score from the live Ad Library, never from Motion.** All four Motion counts were 4 months stale and wrong by 40–180 %. Two leads had far more video than the snapshot showed, which flips the "creative need" points. Page-scoped URL and retry rule: see "Live verification" above.
2. **Filter `products.json` for hidden SKUs before counting momentum.** Drop anything tagged `redirect-to-404`, `hidden` or `draft`, or with every variant `available: false`. Fishwife's whole pitch, and its 83 score, rested on 8 hidden products.
3. **Re-open every "why now" source on the day.** Job posts disappear (Foggy's Art Director), and sale banners change the story (AUrate's 40 % off).
4. **Read the brand's live ad copy, not only the summary.** Moment's live copy says "No hangover", a claim our films refuse. That is a deal-risk to settle before the free stills, not after.
5. **Check stock on every SKU named in a message.** A CTA option that's sold out in half the sizes invites a "we're out of that" reply.
6. **Contact check:** an email on the brand's site can sit only in page metadata (AUrate hello@). Prefer the address a visitor actually sees, and note which one it is.
