## Method and data limits (read before sending)
- **Candidate sourcing:**
  - Motion's public library index (`motionapp.com/library/brands/`, 2,846 brands). I fetched 1,424 brand pages in relevant industries (F&B, home, kitchen, accessories, personal care, wellness, jewellery, pets, beauty) and parsed active ads, new ads per week, and the video/image split of the 20 newest ads.
  - I filtered for 15–300 active ads, ≤ 20 new a week, and ≤ 35 % video, which left 266 brands. I removed big, corporate, non-US and already-listed brands, and added open-window brands that aren't on Motion (NA drinks, cleaning).
  - **32 candidates were screened** (table below).
- **Launch dates:** live Shopify `/products.json?limit=250` pulled 2026-10-09 (`created_at`/`published_at`).
- **Limits:**
  - **Every Motion page reads "Refreshed 4 months ago"**, so ad counts are a ~June 2026 snapshot. Ad points are scored from that snapshot and discounted to a 25-point maximum (28 for very high volume).
  - **The Meta Ad Library returned HTTP 403**, so current activity is unconfirmed. **Before any send, the owner opens the Ad Library by hand (US, active, brand name) and checks that ads are still running.**
  - **TikTok via yt-dlp:** @fishwife reports private; the other accounts returned empty JSON. No TikTok metrics were used.
  - Revenue figures are only those published (cited); everything else is marked [inf].
- **Exclusions:** every brand in `prospects/HOT_NEW_30.md`, `HEAT_50.md`, `us-*.md`, `boca-raton.md` and `launch/dtc-100.csv` was skipped. None of today's leads appear in those lists.
- **Timing used:**
  - BFCM is ~7 weeks out, and holiday gifting is open now.
  - The Dry January pitch window is Oct → late Nov (doc 57).
  - The January cleaning reset window is now → mid-Nov (doc 59).
  - Health and sleep apps should be approached Oct–Nov (doc 62).
