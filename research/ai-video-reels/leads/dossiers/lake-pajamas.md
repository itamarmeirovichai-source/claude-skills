## 1. Why they are hot (one sentence)

## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** Peruvian pima cotton pajama sets (CloudPima®), DreamModal® kimono sets, nightgowns, and since August a daywear/travel loungewear line (Tour, Route, Departure, Scout sets). Women, men, kids, maternity, bridal, corporate gifting.
- **Hero products and prices (live 2026-10-09):** Tour Pullover Jogger Pants Set $268 (published 8/18, PDP 200); Poplin Piped Long-Long Set $148 (8/11); Pointelle Long-Long Set $128 (8/11); Maternity DreamModal Kimono sets $158–228 (9/4). Catalogue 380 products, median ~$124.
- **Size and channels:** DTC plus boutiques; bridal and corporate gifting desks. Revenue private; the price points, 221 ads and dedicated bridal/corporate inboxes suggest ~$10–30M [inf].
- **Where they advertise:** Meta (FB/IG). 29 of the top 30 cards are dynamic (DCO) units; newest 10/7.
- **Current creative:** good lines ("Gang's all here", "Let the light in", "Exploring the English countryside", "Your closet's starting lineup"), but delivered as catalogue stills and DCO mixes. Weakness: the soft-hand and drape of pima can't be felt in a still, and the new travel line (pajama comfort you can wear out) is described, never shown doing its job.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** co-founders **Cassandra Cannon** (creative vision) and **Anne Read Lattimore** (business side). Founded in Savannah in 2014 after both became mothers (brand About page; Southern Lady; The Everymom career profile; UGA alumni magazine 2024).
- **Public voice:** Southern, family, gifting, timeless prints; they speak about functionality and style for real women.
- **Cares about:** taste and print fidelity, gifting moments, not looking trendy or cheap.
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** stripes and piping drawn wrong, fabric that looks plastic, AI models that don't look like their customer.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** stills-heavy library at scale, a brand-new daywear line that needs a "use case" film, a gifting calendar (Dec), bridal (Jan–Jun) and Valentine's.
- **Red flags:** (a) only general inboxes (info@ on the FAQ page); (b) kids' and maternity lines exist: the film never shows a child or a pregnancy; (c) stripes/piping must come from their product photos (no generated pattern); (d) no claims issues found in live ads.

- **Physics:** one outfit, same creases logic across cuts; no magic outfit changes. Camera never passes through doors or glass.
- **AI vs real:** AI builds the places and the body from behind; the set comes from their product photos (stripe count checked against the PDP); no faces, no children.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills with the real Tour set placed true to colour and stripe; a promise that patterns are never generated.
**Re-buy path:** Dec 1–20 gifting refresh, January travel, Valentine's prints, bridal season, every new print drop (they ship new prints monthly).
**Suggested tier:** Premiere $2,500 (two cuts: travel + gifting), then Season $3,500/mo for drops.
**Timing:** pitch the **Dec 1–20 refresh** (gifting ads tire after ~3 weeks) and **January travel**; never "holiday hero".
