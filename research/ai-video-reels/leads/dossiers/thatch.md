- **What they sell:** demi-fine and fine-look jewellery, the "Anywhere" charm system (charms that move between necklaces, bag clips and straps).
- **Hero products (live):** Wood Anywhere Shell (Coconut, Driftwood, Cacao) $115, Sand Lustre $138; Large Bag Clip for Anywhere Charms $50; Rya Leather Tassel Necklace $95. Median ~$125; 635 SKUs.
- **Size and channels:** DTC + Liberty Station showroom (opened 2023-11-02) + wholesale (JOOR, Faire). Revenue private [inf: low single-digit $M].
- **Current creative:** fall-collection and charm stills. The charm *system* (one charm, many places) needs motion and isn't shown moving.

## 3. The person
