## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** organic cotton and Eco Satin pajamas in original hand-painted prints (XXS–6X), plus robes, boxers, kitchen textiles, pouches, pet bandanas. Made with artisan partners in Jaipur.
- **Hero products and prices (live 2026-10-09):** Holiday Hens Men's Long PJ Set $178, Short PJ Set $148, PJ Pants $84, Pet Bandana $28 (all created 9/15, PDP 200); Holiday Hens Eco Satin Tribeca Pants $168 (9/15); Winter Holly Bagheera bundles (9/29). Catalogue 1,000+ products, median ~$84.
- **Size and channels:** DTC first, some wholesale. ICIC Inner City 100 (2023 and 2024) reported 5,280 % revenue growth 2018–2022; ~$1M in 2019 (Inquirer). Likely ~$10–25M now [inf].
- **Where they advertise:** Meta; 336 active, 11 started ≤30 d, 7 running 60+ d, newest 10/3.
- **Current creative:** single-print stills and DCO with "Limited Edition", "Selling Fast", "Join the herd". Licensed drops (Gilmore Girls, Grateful Dead) sit next to originals. Weakness: the hand-painted origin of every print, their real difference, is in the copy but never on screen; satin drape is invisible in stills.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** founder **Amy Voloshin** (RISD textiles, 10+ years in fashion), co-founded with **Leo Voloshin** in 2016 (ICIC 2024; Philadelphia Inquirer 2019; 6abc Localish 2021; brand "Why Printfresh" page).
- **Public voice:** whimsical creatures, sustainability, size inclusivity (XXS–6X), majority woman-owned.
- **Cares about:** the art staying hers, inclusivity, scarcity-driven drops.
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** AI redrawing her hand-painted prints (the core of the brand), AI bodies that erase size range.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** stills-heavy library at very high volume, constant print drops (each needs creative), holiday matching sets, a pet line.
- **Red flags:** (a) **licensed prints** (Gilmore Girls, Grateful Dead "Stealie & Dancing Bears") are off-limits for any film or still; use originals only; (b) "Matching Family Pajamas in Holiday Hens" (created 10/5) is not available ($0, no stock): never mention it; (c) prints must come from product photos, never generated; (d) size-inclusive casting is expected: show a range of bodies from the shoulders down; (e) no claims issues in live ads ("Scary-Cute" is Halloween copy, not a fear claim).

## 5. The buying path

| 8.0–11.0 | Same frame | **Punchline caused by the product:** the dog jumps up wearing the Holiday Hens bandana and settles next to Mom, the only exact match. |
| 11.0–13.0 | **Proof slot** | Their real footage of Amy's hand-painted original becoming the print (2 s). |
| 13.0–15.0 | End card | VO: "Every print starts as a painting." Logo + swappable offer slot. |

- **Physics:** a dog jumps, circles once and lies down; fabric folds follow the body; prints never move or morph.
- **AI vs real:** AI builds the room and bodies from the shoulders down; every garment and the bandana from their product photos; no faces; the dog is the only "face" and is generic (not a real pet without consent).

**Proof they need:** 5 stills with Holiday Hens and Bagheera placed with the print unchanged (pattern check against the PDP); a written promise that prints are never redrawn.
**Re-buy path:** every print drop (monthly), Valentine's, Mother's Day, spring florals, Halloween; Season retainer fits the cadence.
**Suggested tier:** Premiere $2,500 first, then Season $3,500/mo (drop cadence).
**Timing:** **Dec 1–20 refresh** for Holiday Hens (holiday ads tire after ~20 days), then Valentine's prints.
