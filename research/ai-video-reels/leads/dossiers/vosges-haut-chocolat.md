## 1. Why they are hot (one sentence)
A Chicago luxury chocolatier running ~73 active US Meta ads (22 of the top 30 are stills) that published a 15-SKU holiday collection called **"Snowbound"** on 10/6 (Snowbound Perfect Gift $95, Snowbound Little Gift $32, Alpine Chocolate Ensemble $130, The Holiday Toast $36), with Valentine's, its other peak, next.

## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** exotic-flavour truffles, bars, bonbons, drinking chocolate, gift towers ($125–355), the Advent Calendar ($385, "It sold out last year" per their ad), cheese-and-chocolate pairings. Iconic purple box. Tagline "TRAVEL THE WORLD THROUGH CHOCOLATE®" (Our Story page).
- **Size [inf]:** founded 1998; long-running national brand with retail and DTC; plausibly $20–40M, at or above the top of our band.
- **Current creative:** elegant still cards with story copy ("Welcome back to the Grand Cacao Lodge… 25 new doors"), a few advent videos. **Gap:** the Snowbound collection is a story (a snowed-in lodge) told only in a headline; nothing in the library *happens*.

## 3. The person
- **Founder:** **Katrina Markoff** founded Vosges in 1998 from her Chicago apartment (Entrepreneur). **Ownership changed:** Wikipedia, citing Crain's Chicago Business (2024-10-25), says the company "is owned by La Montagne Holdings", and the same Crain's piece is headlined "After making a splash in luxury chocolate, Vosges' founder preps her next act." **The current decision-maker (CEO or marketing lead) is not public in our sources [unverified].** The first message therefore goes to the brand inbox addressed to "the Vosges team", asking who runs creative.
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** (1) the purple box, foil and truffle finishes looking cheap or wrong; (2) luxury tone broken by slapstick; (3) AI people in a luxury ad.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** a heavy still library, a collection with a built-in story, two peaks (holiday, Valentine's) and constant drops (Halloween, pairings, advent).
- **Red flags:** (a) **not founder-led any more**; the buyer is unknown and may be a corporate marketing team with an agency [unverified]. (b) Likely at the top of our revenue band. (c) "The Holiday Toast" set and "Eggnog Elixir Drinking Chocolate" are non-alcoholic, but the word "toast" and eggnog can read as alcohol cues on Meta: no glasses raised, no bottles in frame. (d) Claims scan clean.
