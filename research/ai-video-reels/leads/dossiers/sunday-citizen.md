## 1. Why they are hot (one sentence)
Sunday Citizen published the **Ciel Love Seat Sofa ($1,800, ships compressed, "no tools") on 9/22**, new throw pillows on 10/8 and Cloud Jersey loungewear on 9/24, and is running **~304 active US Meta ads, all 30 top cards started in the last 30 days, 9 of 30 with video**.

## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** "the world's softest things": Snug and bamboo-viscose bedding, comforters, throws, loungewear, and since 2026 compressed modular furniture (Sunday Lounge Chair 3/30, Ciel and Sunday chair sets 6/30, Ciel Love Seat Sofa 9/22).
- **Hero products and prices (live 2026-10-09):** Snug Comforter from $171; Snug Cooling Comforter $360; Snug Bed Blanket $160; Ciel Love Seat Sofa $1,800 (PDP 200: "one-person assembly, no delivery appointments or tools", "wait up to 72 hours for full expansion"); new throw pillows $95–125 (10/8). 431 products, median ~$112.
- **Size and channels:** DTC plus a SoHo store (42 Crosby St, opened Dec 2022). Eight-figure revenue reported by 2022 (Mann Report). Likely ~$20–40M now [inf], at the top of our band.
- **Where they advertise:** Meta; 304 active (confirmed); formats DCO 15 / DPA 10 / VIDEO 4.
- **Current creative:** review-quote statics ("8,000 five-star reviews"), DPA product feeds, a few furniture videos ("No Tools. No Stress. Just Sit."). Weakness: the sofa's unbox-and-expand moment is told in copy; the bedding is sold with adjectives ("insanely soft") rather than a scene.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** co-founders **Mike Abadi** (co-founder and CEO) and **Michelle Familier** (co-founder and CMO) per the Mann Report store-opening item (Nov 2022). 2026 roles not re-confirmed. The brand's About page is written in a founder's first person.
- **Public voice:** comfort as the whole point, hotel-origin story (a blanket guests kept taking home), "designed in Miami".
- **Cares about:** softness as the brand, review volume, the new furniture category landing.
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** fabric that looks synthetic or stiff; an expansion shown faster or bigger than real (a returns problem).

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** a new category (furniture) with a visible mechanism (compressed → full sofa), heavy static/DPA library, gifting (Dec) and a "new year, new apartment" January.
- **Red flags:** (a) ads use "naturally cooling", "hypoallergenic" and "Tones of Cool™" language: the film makes no temperature or allergy claim; (b) expansion takes **up to 72 hours** (their PDP): never show it as instant or put a clock on screen; use a hard cut to "later"; (c) only hello@ (contact and FAQ pages); (d) possibly above the $20M band, so a creative team may exist: pitch as the scenario layer their team can't shoot (LESSONS 2026-10-09).

## 5. The buying path
**The ONE film: "Fourth Floor" (15 s, 9:16 + 4:5 + 16:9 for the PDP) for the Ciel Love Seat Sofa.**

| t (s) | Shot (real rig) | Action / sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Handheld, stairwell, low angle | **Sound-first:** a mover shouting "pivot" and a scrape. Frame 0: a neighbour's full-size sofa wedged in a walk-up stairwell, two movers' gloved hands straining. |
| 1.0–4.0 | Same stairwell, hard cut | A single Sunday Citizen box slides past them into a tiny cage elevator (hands only). |
| 4.0–8.0 | Tripod, small apartment | Box cut open; the compressed sofa unrolls and starts to rise. Hard cut "later" (no clock): full Ciel sofa, pillows on it. |
| 8.0–11.0 | Same tripod | **Punchline caused by the product:** she sits with a coffee; through the open door, the movers are still stuck on the landing. |
| 11.0–13.0 | **Proof slot** | Their real expansion footage or fabric macro (2 s). |
| 13.0–15.0 | End card | VO: "Arrives in a box. Stays a sofa." Logo + swappable offer slot. |

- **Physics:** the neighbour's sofa is stuck at a real stairwell turn; the box goes up by elevator (no one-person stair carry); expansion shown in two states only, matching the PDP's "up to 72 hours".
- **AI vs real:** AI builds the building, movers' gloved hands and the apartment; the sofa, fabric and pillows come from their product photos; no faces.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills of the Ciel sofa (boxed, half-risen, finished) true to colour and proportion; an accuracy promise against the PDP dimensions.
**Re-buy path:** each new furniture SKU, bedding drops, Dec gifting, January "new place", Valentine's (couch-for-two cut).
**Suggested tier:** Premiere $2,500 (furniture + bedding cut), then Season.
**Timing:** **Dec 1–20 refresh** and the **January moving/reset** window.
