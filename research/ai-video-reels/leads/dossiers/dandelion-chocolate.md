## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** single-origin bars made from "just two ingredients: cocoa beans and sugar" (Todd Masonis, About page), $13–18; gift sets (70 % Three-Bar $42, Six-Bar $80); bonbon collections ($76–100); the Advent Calendar ($205, "sells out well before December" per their ads); subscriptions ($70–800); a book; cafés in San Francisco (7 locations listed), Las Vegas (The Venetian) and Tokyo.
- **Size [inf]:** 2 SF factories + Tokyo (2019 coverage), multiple cafés, wholesale; plausibly $10–25M.
- **Current creative:** long, literate copy over product stills ("A gift that says you paid attention — without you having to say it"; "the only one in the world with 25 days of treats from 24 different chocolatiers"). 4 videos in the top 30 (Tokyo gâteau, hojicha hot chocolate). **Gap:** the two-ingredient story, the most distinctive fact they own, is told only in text.
- **Ad longevity:** 15 of the top 30 cards have run 60+ days (food creatives can run long, doc 49 N5), so a strong film earns a long run.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** **Todd Masonis**, co-founder and CEO (About page signs the welcome note; book bio: "in 2010, he and Cameron founded Dandelion Chocolate"). Co-founder **Cameron Ring** reportedly sits on the board, not day to day [c]. Both previously built Plaxo (sold to Comcast) [c].
- **Public voice:** precise, process-proud, a former engineer's taste for "doing one thing right": "Since 2010, we've been crafting chocolate with just two ingredients: cocoa beans and sugar."
- **What he cares about:** craft truth (bean origin, harvest year, batch), education (classes, the book), restraint.
- **What he'd fear from an AI studio:** (1) chocolate that looks wrong (bloom, snap, temper, wrapper art); (2) anything that implies extra ingredients or misstates an origin; (3) generic luxury-ad gloss that contradicts their minimalism.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** founder-led, heavy still-ad volume, a distinctive fact that makes a visual joke, two large seasonal peaks and monthly collections (Season plan).
- **Red flags:** (a) many bars are sold out on any given day: CTA products are re-checked on the send day (today: Maya Mountain Belize 70 % and Kokoa Kamili 70 % in stock; 70 % Three-Bar Gift Set $42 in stock; The Classics Collection 2026 is **sold out**, so it is never named). (b) The wrapper art and origin text must come from their real photos (Kling i2v), never generated. (c) "Two ingredients" is shown only for the bars, never for bonbons or confections (which have more). (d) Claims scan clean: no health, fear or rival claims; "only one in the world" is their own advent claim, not repeated by us.

## 5. The buying path
**The ONE film: "Two Ingredients" (15 s, 9:16 + 4:5, cut to 8 s).**
Logic: a kitchen keeps trying to "improve" the chocolate. The product causes the punchline: the bar's ingredient list stops every addition.

| t (s) | Shot (real rig) | Action / sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Overhead tripod above a steel counter | **Frame 0 in motion, sound first:** a scoop of cocoa beans already pouring onto the counter (rattle on the measured frame), a cup of sugar beside it. Super: "Ingredients:" |
| 1.0–3.5 | Same overhead | A hand slides in a bottle of vanilla. A second hand slides it straight back out of frame. |
| 3.5–6.0 | Same overhead, hard cuts | Milk powder in, out. A jar of something else in, out. Faster each time (one prop per cut). |
| 6.0–9.0 | Insert, macro on a slider | **Proof slot:** the client's real bar: wrapper, origin, and the printed ingredients line, from their product photo or phone footage. |
| 9.0–12.0 | Back to overhead | The last hand hesitates over the counter holding a cinnamon stick, then sets it down outside the frame line. Beat. |
| 12.0–15.0 | End card | The 70 % Three-Bar Gift Set. **VO (2 lines, locked voice, no lips):** "Cocoa beans. Sugar. That's the whole list. Dandelion Chocolate." Logo + swappable offer slot. |

- **Rigs:** overhead tripod, slider insert; hands only. **Food truth:** beans and bars from real photos; no melt or pour generated (a real phone insert if needed). **Sound:** bean rattle and the slide-outs on measured frames; −14 LUFS / ≤ −1 dBTP.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills built from their real bar and gift-set photos, wrapper art unchanged and colour matched (ΔE ≤ 3); a claims sheet showing "two ingredients" only on two-ingredient bars.
**Re-buy path:** every harvest, origin and collection (Raven, Japanese Tea, Classics), Valentine's, Lunar New Year (their Red Envelope Bar) → the "Two Ingredients" skeleton re-shot per origin; Season plan.
**Suggested tier:** Premiere $2,500 for a Valentine's film (approve by early January); Season at the first reply.
