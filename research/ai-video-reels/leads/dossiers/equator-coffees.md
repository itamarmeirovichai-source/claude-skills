## 1. Why they are hot (one sentence)
A founder-owned Bay Area roaster (12 cafés plus national wholesale) running ~65 active US Meta ads, two-thirds of the top 30 stills, that launched an **Office Coffee Subscription (5 lb)** on 9/30 and four new coffees since mid-September, going into the January back-to-office and resolution weeks.

## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** specialty coffee roasted to order (single origins $20–29, blends, espresso), subscriptions, the new 5 lb Office Coffee Subscription ($96), bundles (Equator Cafe Bundle $69), merch. 12 cafés, wholesale "coast to coast" (About page).
- **Size [inf]:** founded 1995; 12 cafés + wholesale + DTC suggests the $15–30M band, at the top of our range.
- **Current creative:** stills with offer headlines ("Claim 30% Off All Coffee", "Brand New Coffee Arrivals", "The Finest Coffee, Roasted to Order") and some short video. **Gap:** no idea-led film; the "roasted to order" story is told in captions, never shown as a joke.
- **Ad lifespan:** coffee creatives average ~38 days (doc 49 N5), so the case for a monthly Season cadence is strong.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** **Helen Russell and Brooke McDonnell**, co-founders ("partners in life and business", About page). Equator was California's first B Corp roaster (2011) and the first certified LGBTQ-owned business to win the SBA National Small Business of the Year (2016) (About page).
- **Public voice:** values-led and precise: Brooke on her first origin trip (1997): it "filled me with a sense of wonder, gratitude and privilege to be a small part of the coffee world."
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** (1) coffee that looks generic or wrong (crema, roast colour, a bag that isn't theirs); (2) anything that cheapens the farmer and sourcing story; (3) AI people standing in for their real café staff.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** a mixed but still-led library, steady new coffees, a brand-new B2B product (office subscription) with an obvious January moment, and monthly creative churn.
- **Red flags:** (a) a larger, established company may have a marketing team or agency [unverified]; position VXO as the staged scene they can't shoot, never a replacement. (b) Their café baristas are real people: no AI staff, no AI café interior presented as an Equator café. (c) Claims scan clean (no health, fear or rival claims); "The Finest Coffee" is their own puffery, not repeated by us. (d) The press contact is an outside PR agency (equator@quotedpr.com on /pages/press); the first email goes to info@.

- **Rigs:** tripod lock-offs, slider insert, handheld; never through glass. **People:** backs and hands only. **Sound:** bag thud, chair squeaks on measured frames; −14 LUFS / ≤ −1 dBTP. **Coffee truth:** pour and crema only from a real phone insert.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills from their real bag photos (label and roast colour matched, ΔE ≤ 3); a variant plan with a 16:9 cut for office buyers.
**Re-buy path:** a new single origin every few weeks + seasonal blends + office/wholesale pushes → the Season plan; coffee ads tire in ~5 weeks.
**Suggested tier:** Season $3,500/mo pitched at the first reply; Premiere $2,500 as the entry film for January.
