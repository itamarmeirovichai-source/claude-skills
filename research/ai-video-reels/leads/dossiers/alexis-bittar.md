## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** Lucite and molten-metal statement jewellery (since the 1990s) and, since the 2021 relaunch, handbags.
- **Hero products (live):** Twisted Gold Side Handle Clutch Purse, Moss Green, $675 (published 9/21, in stock); Silverlake Lucite pieces ($345–1,195, published 8/18); new FW26 earrings $70–148.
- **Size and channels:** DTC + ~15 US stores (Wikipedia) + wholesale. Revenue private; likely above the $20M band [inf; flag].
- **Current creative:** stills and catalogue carousels; launch copy ("The next edit of our FW26 collection", "Discover all new sculptural handbag designs"). Since **10/8**: "Mini Vault Sale… up to 60% off… new drops at 9AM, 3PM and 9PM".

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** **Alexis Bittar**, founder; he bought the brand back from Brooks Brothers in 2021 (Business of Fashion; Wikipedia; Design Scene 2025) and relaunched DTC with handbags. A marketing team exists (Press/Marketing inbox).
- **Public voice:** art and fashion, sculpture, New York; strong personal brand.
- **Fears from an AI studio:** hardware or Lucite rendered as generic plastic; "AI art" that cheapens the hand-sculpted claim.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** "hand-sculpted… at the intersection of art + fashion" is their own language: a museum gag dramatises it without overstating anything.
- **Red flags:** (a) probably above the band; slower, team-led buying path; (b) heavy discounting (60 % vault sale): pitch a **full-price** handbag and a January/Valentine's slot; (c) do not show any artist's real work or a real museum (IP); (d) no claims issues.
