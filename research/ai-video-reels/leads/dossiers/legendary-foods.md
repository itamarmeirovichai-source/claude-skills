- **Rigs:** tripod lock-off, slider insert, low tripod; never through walls. **Food truth:** the donut from the client's real photo (Kling i2v), one donut size throughout, icing never drifts, no bite on camera. **People:** hands only. **Sound:** shriek and beep on measured frames; −14 LUFS / ≤ −1 dBTP. **Claims:** only the label's numbers, with units, from the pack.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills from their own pack photos with the label crisp; a claims sheet listing every on-screen number against their nutrition panel.
