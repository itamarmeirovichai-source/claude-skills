## 1. Why they are hot (one sentence)
On **10/1** Brackish published its **Slim Collection** (four slim feather bow ties, $225, tagged fall 2026) and is running **~60 active US Meta ads, all started in the last 30 days, with only 1 of the top 30 carrying video**, so a product sold on "an iridescence found only in nature" is being advertised in stills.

## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** handmade feather bow ties (pheasant, quail, goose, turkey), pocket squares, cummerbunds, cufflinks, pins; a women's line (feather earrings, cuffs) since 2019. Made in a Charleston atelier.
- **Hero products and prices (live 2026-10-09):** Edisto / Rice / Slate / Jeffery **Slim Bow Ties** $225 each (published 10/1, in stock); Savannah Statement Earring $185 and Lillian cuffs $175–225 (created 9/2). Catalogue ~448 SKUs, median ~$175.
- **Size and channels:** DTC plus department-store wholesale (Saks and Neiman Marcus per older press); weddings are the core use case. Revenue private; likely low-to-mid single-digit $M [inf].
- **Where they advertise:** Meta (FB/IG); every top card started 9/11–10/8.
- **Current creative:** wedding and fall-collection stills, DPA carousels, one video. Copy is good ("Hand-set quail feathers", "A keepsake, for the day you'll remember most"). Weakness: feathers shift colour with light and angle, which a still can't show; the gift/wedding moment is described, never staged.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** co-founders **Ben Ross** and **Jeff Plotner** (brand's own "Here's our story"; Charleston Magazine; Hemispheres; SCETV). Ross made turkey-feather ties for his groomsmen in 2007; they launched in 2012. Current 2026 roles not verified.
- **Public voice:** Southern craft, sustainability of feathers, groomsmen and family stories.
- **Cares about:** craft authenticity, the wedding party, not looking costume-y.
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** fake-looking feathers (a cheap render of their craft), AI groomsmen faces, being made to look like a novelty brand.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** stills-only library, a launch this month, a product whose sales argument is motion and light, and an annual wedding calendar that needs fresh creative (engagements peak in December; planning in January).
- **Red flags:** (a) founders' current roles unverified; (b) only a general inbox (info@); (c) feathers must never be generated: every tie comes from their real product photos (Kling i2v), colour checked against the PDP; (d) no claims issues found.
