## 1. Why they are hot (one sentence)

## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** handcrafted acetate and titanium sunglasses and optical frames named after New Orleans streets and landmarks; lifetime guarantee.
- **Hero products and prices (live):** MUSE | Sauvage + 18K sunglasses $335 (published 9/8); ST. LOUIS CLASSICS | Venezia 18K $335; ZANDER | Gelato $295; ~410 SKUs, median ~$335.
- **Size and channels:** DTC + own stores (New Orleans, NYC and others per press) + wholesale. Revenue private [inf: mid single-digit to low double-digit $M].
- **Current creative:** product-on-white stills and DPA, a few launch videos (MUSE runway). Since **10/8** they run "Black Friday is coming early. Up to 70% off… our biggest (and last) sale of the year."

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** founder and CEO **Stirling Barrett** (started KREWE in 2013 at 23; Cool Hunting, Madewell interview, Good Grit, myneworleans.com). No 2026 confirmation of role found.
- **Public voice:** "100 % Bayou State" inspiration, Italian manufacturing, culture over trend.
- **Fears from an AI studio:** a frame rendered wrong (hinge, acetate pattern), a fake-looking New Orleans, Carnival cliché.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** stills-heavy, launches monthly, and an owned seasonal moment.
- **Red flags:**
  - (a) **Health claim on eyeglasses PDPs:** the Blue Light Lens add-on says it "filter[s] out harmful blue light". Not in their ads. We film **sunglasses only** and never mention or show blue-light lenses (doc 58 −10 applied).
  - (b) **Deep discounting** (up to 70 % from 10/8) may signal inventory pressure; keep a swappable offer card and don't comment on the sale.
  - (c) Carnival imagery: masks are worn by float riders by law, which conveniently means no faces; avoid drink cups (alcohol cue) and any real krewe's name or float design (IP).
  - (d) Generic inbox only (krewe@krewe.com).
