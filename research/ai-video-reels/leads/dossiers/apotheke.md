## 1. Why they are hot (one sentence)
APOTHEKE released **Churro Classico, a collaboration candle with a Brooklyn restaurant ("Two Brooklyn houses. One candle.", published 9/30, ad from 10/5)**, a seasonal mini reed-diffuser line (9/8) and advent sets, after landing on Target shelves this summer, and runs **~134 active US Meta ads**.

## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** hand-poured candles (Classic, Petite, 3-Wick in coloured glass with gold interiors), reed diffusers, soaps, fragrance classes and scent-design parties at the Red Hook studio; collaborations (Jones Road Beauty, PlantShed, VATO).
- **Hero products and prices (live 2026-10-09):** VATO x APOTHEKE Churro Classico Candle $25 (PDP 200); seasonal 3-Wick candles $84 (Cardamom Chestnut, Bitter Cherry, Juniper Birch); mini reed diffusers $36 (9/8); 12- and 25-piece advent sets $140/$250.
- **Size and channels:** DTC, own stores (Red Hook flagship, Rockefeller Center), Nordstrom, West Elm, Bloomingdale's, Crate & Barrel, and Target (founder post dated 6/1/2026). 16,000 sq ft Brooklyn factory. Likely ~$15–30M [inf].
- **Where they advertise:** Meta; formats VIDEO 16 / DCO 6 / DPA 4 / IMAGE 3; newest 10/7.
- **Current creative:** creator UGC (classes, "POV: girls' night"), scent-note descriptions over product stills ("Cardamom Chestnut is the first fire of the year, in a candle"). Weakness: scent is described, never made visible; the collab's "two houses, one candle" line has no story on screen.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** founder **Chrissy Fichtl** (launched 2011 from her kitchen; Brooklyn Flea; factory 2012), co-founder husband **Sebastian Picasso** (brand journal; noissue profile; Red Hook Waterfront; J.Crew "Studio Hours").
- **Public voice:** Brooklyn-made, hands-on, "look how far we've come", team pride.
- **Cares about:** craft and Brooklyn identity, retail growth, experiences.
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** losing the handmade, made-in-Brooklyn truth; a fake flame or fake pour.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** collab launches every season, retail expansion, a gifting peak, and a library that is UGC rather than concept-led.
- **Red flags:** (a) **creative need is low** (19 of 30 top cards are video); (b) Churro Classico is a co-brand: the partner's name and logo appear only as on the real label, no partner premises shown; (c) the pour and the flame must be real footage or physics-checked (flame direction, wax pool); (d) generic info@ (contact and FAQ pages); (e) no claims issues ("wellness can be as simple as slowing down" is a creator's caption, not a health claim).

- **Physics:** flame stays upright indoors, wax pool grows only across cuts that read as time passing, doors open inward realistically; camera never passes through the door.
- **AI vs real:** AI builds the hallway, hands, dog and kitchen; the candle and label from product photos; the pour is their footage; no faces, no children.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills with the real Churro Classico glass and label; a note that the flame and pour are physics-checked or real.
**Re-buy path:** every seasonal scent and collab (they launch several a quarter), Valentine's, Mother's Day, store openings, Target resets.
**Suggested tier:** Short $1,200 first (one collab film), then Season for the collab cadence.
**Timing:** **Dec 1–20 refresh** (gifting) and **Valentine's** scents.
