## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** design-led home goods made with artisan partners: stonewashed linen bedding, lighting, tabletop, kitchen tools, bath, storage, rugs. Brand + retail stores (Hudson, NYC, LA) + large wholesale.
- **Hero products and prices (live 2026-10-09):** Pleat Table Lamp $450 (Bone, Peach; 9/21–23, PDP 200); Colorstory Stripe Lamp $360 (9/21, PDP 200); Pillar Table Lamp $400; Curve butter dish $84 (Natural, Moss, Azure; PDP 200); Woven Stripe kitchen towel $28; Malabar and Blanket Stitch Bouclé throws $148–158.
- **Size and channels:** DTC + wholesale (press@, trade@, wholesale@ inboxes); likely ~$20–40M [inf].
- **Where they advertise:** Meta; formats DPA 19 / CAROUSEL 3 / DCO 2.
- **Current creative:** clean copy ("A well-set table changes the whole vibe", "Something good is in the oven") on product-feed stills; spring/summer collections (Sky, outdoor dining, farmer's market) still running in October. Weakness: no scene, no light. A $450 lamp is sold without ever being switched on.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** founders **Paul Denoly** and **Nick Blaine**, who started the brand and shop in 2013 from their home in Hudson NY (Remodelista; Sight Unseen; Wikipedia stub). Current roles and the marketing lead not verified; press@ is the marketing-facing inbox.
- **Public voice:** considered, craft-and-colour, "designed in Hudson, New York for everyday use".
- **Cares about:** taste above all, artisan respect, colour stories by collection.
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** kitsch, glossy CGI that cheapens handmade pieces, wrong glaze colours.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** stale static library + big new collection + lamps (light is a film's native material) + a January "reset the room" moment.
- **Red flags:** (a) founders' 2026 roles unverified; (b) taste bar is high: the stills must look like their catalogue, not like AI; (c) wholesale partners may run their own creative; (d) no claims issues found ("Kitchen accessories designed to work beautifully and last" is the strongest line).

## 5. The buying path
**The ONE film: "The Last Piece" (15 s, 9:16 + 4:5 + 16:9) for the Pleat Table Lamp.**

|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Tripod, locked wide on a dim January living room | **Sound-first:** a lamp switch click that does nothing (no bulb yet). Frame 0: a bare side table. |

| 8.0–11.0 | Same frame | **Punchline caused by the product:** a hand sets the Pleat lamp on the side table and clicks it. Warm light fills the room; everything placed before finally shows its colour. |

| 13.0–15.0 | End card | VO: "Start the year with the light on." Logo + swappable offer slot. |

- **Physics:** one light source; shadows and colour shift consistently when it turns on; window light stays winter-low.
- **AI vs real:** AI builds the room and hands; every Hawkins object from their product photos with glaze/stripe colours checked against the PDP; no faces.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills of the room in their palette with real pieces, lit by the Pleat lamp; a promise that colours match the PDP.
**Re-buy path:** seasonal collections (they ship spring/summer/fall colour stories), Dec 1–20 hosting refresh, January reset, Mother's Day.
**Suggested tier:** Premiere $2,500 (lamp film + 2 cutdowns), then Season for each collection.
**Timing:** **Dec 1–20 hosting refresh** (tabletop cut) and the **January room reset** (lamp film).
