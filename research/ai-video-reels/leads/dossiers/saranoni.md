## 1. Why they are hot (one sentence)
Saranoni launched the **Double Ruched throw in a new XXL 80" x 100" size (ad from 9/24: "More blanket for staying close")** while **~229 active US Meta ads keep running, only 3 of the top 30 started in the last 30 days and 22 have run 60+ days**, going into its biggest gifting season.

## 2. The business
- **What they sell:** faux-fur and minky blankets and throws (Double Ruched, Lush, Bamboni®), baby and toddler blankets, licensed university blankets/snugglers, gift sets.
- **Hero products and prices (live 2026-10-09):** Double Ruched Faux Fur XL Throw, XXL 80x100 variant $289 (PDP 200); Lush XL; University Snuggler $75 (8/26, licensed: off-limits for film).
- **Size and channels:** DTC, Perigold, affiliates (Awin). Likely ~$10–30M [inf].
- **Where they advertise:** Meta; formats VIDEO 15 / DCO 14 / DPA 1; oldest card 305 days.
- **Current creative:** soft-focus lifestyle video and DCO ("The second your hands touch it… everything slows down"). Weakness: the same set of spots has run for months, and size (their new selling point) is never dramatised.

## 3. The person
- **Who decides:** **Emily Peterson**, founder and CEO (Saranoni About page). She and her late husband Nathan started the company to fund their daughter's microtia surgery; Nathan died in 2022 (USU Entrepreneur series; About page).
- **Public voice:** family, comfort, giving back (they donate with purchases), mother of six.
- **Cares about:** warmth and connection, product quality, family-safe imagery.
- **What they'd fear from an AI studio:** fake-looking faux fur, cold or cynical tone, anything unsafe around babies.

## 4. Fit and red flags
- **Fit:** stale library, a new size to sell, peak gifting, Valentine's ("staying close").
- **Red flags:** (a) baby/toddler line: **never** show an infant with a blanket (safe-sleep norms); film adults and a pet only; (b) licensed university products are off-limits; (c) 53 % of top cards are video already, so the pitch is fresh scenes, not "you need video"; (d) only service@ (contact page); (e) a family story with loss: keep the tone light, never reference it.

## 5. The buying path
**The ONE film: "Tug" (15 s, 9:16 + 4:5) for the Double Ruched XXL 80x100.**

| t (s) | Shot (real rig) | Action / sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Tripod, low, sofa end, feet only | **Sound-first:** a sharp tug of fabric. Frame 0: four bare feet fighting over a small throw. |
| 1.0–5.0 | Same frame, hard cuts | The throw slides left, right; a cold foot pokes out each time. Movie light flickers. |
| 5.0–9.0 | Handheld, standing | A pair of hands unfolds the XXL Double Ruched: fold after fold, it covers the sofa, both people and the arm rests. |
| 9.0–12.0 | Tripod, low | **Punchline caused by the product:** the dog climbs in too, and there's still blanket hanging to the floor. |
| 12.0–13.5 | **Proof slot** | Their real macro of the ruched faux fur (1.5 s). |
| 13.5–15.0 | End card | VO: "Room for everyone." Logo + swappable offer slot. |

- **Physics:** 80" x 100" covers a 3-seat sofa and two adults, not more; folds and weight drape realistically; no impossible unfolding.
- **AI vs real:** AI builds room, feet, hands and dog; the blanket's texture and colour from their product photos; no faces, no children.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills of the XXL on a real-scale sofa, colour-true (Flax, Midnight, Chocolate Blend); a promise that the fur texture comes from their photos.
**Re-buy path:** Dec 1–20 gift refresh, Valentine's, Mother's Day, new colourways each season.
**Suggested tier:** Short $1,200 first (one film, two cuts), then Premiere for the gifting flight.
**Timing:** **Dec 1–20 refresh** (their current spots are months old), then **Valentine's**.
