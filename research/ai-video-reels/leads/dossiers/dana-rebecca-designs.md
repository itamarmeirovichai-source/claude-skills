- **Where they advertise:** Meta (FB, IG, Messenger, Threads, WhatsApp placements).
- **Current creative (live Ad Library read):**
  - **What works:** warm, specific copy ("She was wearing a tennis necklace at pickup and you noticed it immediately", "Skip the candle"). Retargeting DPA cards.
  - **What's weak:** almost everything is a dynamic product card or a still; the best lines describe scenes (school pickup, the airport, "you put it on for dinner and forget to take it off") that are never shown moving.

- **Red flags:**
  - (a) Name discrepancy in old press (one JCK article says "Dana Levy"); her own newsletter uses Dana Rebecca Gordon. Use "Dana" only.
  - (b) The only general inbox is customer service (info@). jen@ is the **wholesale** contact (Jen Wainstein) and margo@ is external PR: never pitch those.

  - (d) No in-house AI team seen; content team size unknown. Pitch as the scenario layer, never as a replacement.

## 5. The buying path
**The ONE film: "The Dish" (15 s, 9:16 + 4:5, plus an 8 s cut) for the Ava Bea Interval Tennis Bracelet.**

| t (s) | Shot (real rig) | Action / sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Locked-off tripod, overhead 3/4 on a nightstand, lamp on | **Sound-first, frame 0 in motion:** a car key already dropping into a small ceramic dish painted "JEWELRY". Clatter on the measured contact frame. |
| 1.0–7.0 | Same lock-off, 6 hard cuts (one per "night") | Hair tie, AirPods case, a receipt, a guitar pick, a coat button, a second key. The dish fills. Never jewellery. Lamp light shifts slightly per night. |

| 9.5–12.0 | Macro, slider (insert) | **Punchline caused by the product:** a hand enters, wearing the Ava Bea bracelet, and switches off the lamp. The bracelet stays on. Super: "The one you never take off." (their own line) |
| 12.0–13.5 | **Proof slot** | The client's real product footage or a real-photo i2v of the bracelet clasp and stone line. |
| 13.5–15.0 | End card | VO (2 lines, locked voice): "Made to stay on. Designed by Dana." Logo + swappable offer slot. |

- **Physics:** objects fall with real weight and bounce once; lamp light constant except the off; the bracelet doesn't swing like a chain on a still wrist.
- **AI vs real:** AI builds the nightstand world and the props; the bracelet is a Kling i2v from her product photo, stone count checked against the PDP; the hand is a licensed hand model or her own footage. No faces.

**Proof they need:** 5 stills with the real Ava Bea from her photos, stone count matched; a short note on how the bracelet is protected (real photo → i2v, checked frame by frame).
**Re-buy path:** the gag is a franchise skeleton (lock the dish, swap the piece): huggies, birthstone studs each month, Valentine's, Mother's Day. A new birthstone launches monthly, which suits Season.
**Suggested tier:** Premiere $2,500, then Season $3,500/mo (monthly birthstone drop + ad fatigue at her volume).
**Timing:** holiday ads are live; pitch the film as the **Dec 1–20 refresh** and the **Valentine's** flight (ads live mid-January).
