## 5. The buying path
**The ONE film that would make them buy now: "Pace Yourself" (15 s, 9:16 + 4:5) for the 12 Days Advent Calendar.**
The punchline is *caused* by the product: it's too good to ration, so the 12-day calendar is finished on day 4.

| t (s) | Shot (real rig) | Action / sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Overhead, locked-off on a C-stand over a kitchen table | **Sound-first hook:** the crisp peel of a tin's pull-tab on frame 0 as a hand opens Advent door **1**. Super: "Day 1." |
| 1.0–2.5 | Same overhead | Hard cut. Door 2 opens; a tin on toast (**real insert**, the client's phone footage). Super: "Day 2." |
| 2.5–4.0 | Same overhead | Hard cut. Door 3; a neat little plate, cracker and lemon. Super: "Day 3." The crumbs only fall, never regrow. |
| 4.0–7.0 | Same overhead | **Turn:** a hard cut to "Day 4." All 12 doors are ripped open and 12 empty tins are stacked in a tower, with one fork. A beat of silence, then a single fork clink. |
| 7.0–10.0 | Side angle, slider push-in at table height | The empty calendar. One hand (no face) slides **a second, unopened calendar** into frame. |
| 10.0–12.5 | Macro insert, Kling i2v from the real product photo | **Proof slot:** the real calendar and tin art, label crisp, sourcing line legible as printed. |
| 12.5–15.0 | End card | **VO (2 lines, locked voice, no lips):** "Twelve days of Fishwife. Pace yourself. Or buy two." Logo plus a swappable offer slot ("ships by …", the client's real date only). |

- **Camera:** every position is a C-stand overhead, a slider or a macro; nothing passes through objects.
- **Object fates:**
  - ONE calendar until the second calendar appears.
  - Every tin opens exactly once and the lids stay open.
  - Food matches the real product, with no new garnish (allergens).
- **Sound:** the pull-tab peels and the clink sit on their measured frames. Master to −14 LUFS, ≤ −1 dBTP.

**The proof they need:**
- 5 free frames built from their real tin and calendar photos, tin art colour-checked against their files.
- One frame in their illustration world (Danny Miller style used *as a reference*, not imitated without permission; ask first).
- The asset maths: one 15 s master gives a 6 s cut, 3 hooks and 8 stills.
- Turnaround: "frames this week, film live by Nov 1".

**Re-buy path:**
- Collabs nearly every month (Dieux, Bag Balm, Sweetgreen, musicians), each needing a reveal.
- New flavours and packs.
- Seasonal moments: Father's Day box, summer "tinned fish date night", Valentine's.
- Retail "now at Costco" versions.
- Fatigue every ~4 weeks.
- This becomes the Season plan.

**Suggested tier:** Premiere $2,500 (advent plus gift pack). Then Season $3,500/mo for the collab cadence.
