## 1. סרטון מלא, multi-shot / single take מבוים (Seedance 2.5)

### V2-01 · "מעורפל" מול "מבוים": מרדף בסמטה [מתוך מדריך] [CON, YT:oei5JgHcDBk]
**כלי:** ‏Seedance 2.5 דרך OpenArt, ‏1080p, ‏16:9. **הפרומפט המעורפל (לא להשתמש, זו ההשוואה):**
```
A woman runs through a rainy alley at night while something chases her. Make it cinematic, realistic, tense, and dramatic.
```
**המבוים:**
```
A tense nighttime chase through a narrow rain-soaked city alley. A woman in her late 20s wearing a dark charcoal jacket runs away from camera toward the far end of the alley, repeatedly glancing over her left shoulder at an unseen pursuer behind her.
CAMERA, Camera starts roughly 4 meters behind her at waist height, slightly offset camera-right. Natural 35mm documentary lens character. Handheld tracking forward at her running pace, with restrained operator shake caused by footsteps, never smooth or floating. She remains slightly left of center so the alley depth stays visible beside her.
LIGHT, Cold overhead street lamps mixed with a single red neon sign halfway down the alley. Wet brick, metal pipes and puddles catch broken reflections. Light falls off quickly between fixtures, leaving sections of the alley close to black. No soft studio fill.
PERFORMANCE, She runs with real effort rather than a perfect sprint: shoulders rising with hard breathing, jacket fabric pulling and bouncing, wet hair sticking unevenly to her face, shoes splashing through shallow puddles. She looks back once, almost loses her footing, catches herself against the wall with one hand, then keeps moving.
ENVIRONMENT, Heavy rain, overflowing gutter water, steam leaking from a vent, scattered trash shifting in the wind. The unseen pursuer is suggested only by fast approaching footsteps and a moving shadow briefly crossing the wall behind her. Never reveal the pursuer.
SOUND, Native sound only. Her breathing closest in the mix, shoes striking wet pavement and splashing water beneath it, rain on metal fire escapes, distant traffic, then the pursuer's footsteps growing louder behind her. No dialogue. No music.
Photoreal documentary thriller. Visible skin texture, wet fabric weave, imperfect hair movement, realistic water weight and splash behavior, natural motion blur, subtle film grain. Photographed, not rendered.
End with her reaching the brighter red-neon section of the alley while the shadow behind her grows larger.
```
**למה זה עובד:** כל בלוק נפתח ב-`CAMERA,` / `LIGHT,` וכן הלאה, ולכן קל להחליף בלוק אחד בלי לגעת בשאר. המצלמה מתוארת כעובדות פיזיות: מרחק במטרים, גובה, צד, עדשה. האיום **לא נראה לעולם** ("Never reveal"), וזה חוסך כשלים של יצורים. יש **שורת סיום** ("End with…") שקובעת את המצב הסופי. ובסוף מופיעה הנוסחה החוזרת `Photographed, not rendered`.

### V2-03 · מצלמה כעובדות פיזיות: רציף רכבת [מתוך מדריך] [CON]
```
A woman stands alone on an empty train platform at night while a train approaches in the distance. The platform is wet from recent rain, with reflections from overhead station lights. She watches the train coming toward her.
CAMERA, Start 6 meters away from her at chest height, positioned slightly behind and camera-left so we see her three-quarter rear profile and the railway line extending deep into frame. Natural 50mm lens character with moderate background compression and no wide-angle distortion. Begin with her occupying the right third of frame. The camera performs one slow lateral track camera-right across the full shot while moving only slightly closer. No orbit, no crane, no sudden push-in, no handheld shake. Her body stays oriented toward the approaching train and she never turns toward camera.
As the train gets closer, keep her in the right third while the headlights grow larger in the left background. The camera movement remains slow and physically grounded, as if operated on a short dolly track.
End with the camera roughly 4 meters from her, still three-quarter rear, with her profile edge readable and the train headlights filling more of the left side of frame.
LIGHT, Cool overhead platform lights as the primary motivated source, with the approaching train headlights gradually adding stronger frontal spill from the background. Wet concrete reflects both sources. Deep blacks between fixtures, no soft beauty fill.
PERFORMANCE, She remains still except for subtle breathing, coat fabric moving slightly in the wind, one small shift of weight, and hair lifting naturally as the train pushes air toward the platform.
SOUND, Native sound only: distant rail vibration growing louder, wind along the platform, soft electrical hum from the lights, then increasing wheel noise as the train approaches. No dialogue. No music.
Photoreal documentary realism, visible skin texture, fabric weave, realistic reflections, natural motion blur, subtle film grain. Photographed rather than rendered.
```
**למה:** הפרומפט מגדיר גם **מצב התחלה** (6 מ', השליש הימני) וגם **מצב סוף** (4 מ', פרופיל קריא), עם **תנועה אחת** בלבד. אחריה באה רשימת שלילות של תנועות מצלמה ("No orbit, no crane, no sudden push-in"), כי בלעדיה המודל בוחר תנועה אקראית. וגם "she never turns toward camera".

### V2-04 · פיזיקה לפי סדר הכשל: מדף מתמוטט [מתוך מדריך] [CON]
```
Inside a dim industrial warehouse, a heavy steel drum rolls across the concrete floor toward a tall loaded storage rack.
CAMERA, Camera sits roughly 6 meters away at chest height, positioned at a three-quarter angle so both the rolling drum and the full height of the rack remain visible. Natural 35mm documentary lens character. One restrained handheld shot with subtle operator movement. No cuts, no orbit, no slow motion.
ACTION & PHYSICS, The drum rolls with visible weight and momentum across the concrete floor and strikes the lower front support on the left side of the rack.
The collapse begins specifically at that impact point. The lower steel support bends inward first. The left side of the rack starts to lean, while the right side initially remains standing.
As the frame tilts, boxes on the upper shelves begin sliding toward the lower side before falling. They do not explode outward. Gravity pulls them downward as the shelf angle increases.
The upper shelves then lose support progressively from left to right. Steel cross-bracing twists and bends while remaining physically connected to the rack. The structure collapses in sequence rather than disappearing or breaking apart instantly.
Heavy boxes hit the concrete with weight, bounce once or twice depending on size, then skid or settle. Smaller loose items scatter farther. Dust rises only from floor impacts and disturbed cardboard.
The steel drum continues a short distance after impact, slows from the collision, then rolls onto its side and stops near the collapsed rack.
LIGHT, Hard overhead warehouse fixtures provide the only main light source, creating bright pools beneath each fixture with darker gaps between them. Dust becomes visible only when it passes through those light beams.
SOUND, Native sound only: steel drum rolling across concrete, the hard metallic impact, steel bending and groaning, boxes sliding across shelves, heavy objects hitting the floor, smaller debris scattering, then settling creaks. No dialogue. No music.
Photoreal industrial documentary style. Real steel deformation, believable object weight, realistic cardboard compression, gravity-driven motion, natural dust behavior, restrained motion blur, subtle film grain. Photographed rather than rendered.
```
(הגרסה המעורפלת שלו: `A heavy steel drum rolls into a warehouse storage rack and makes it collapse. Make it cinematic and realistic.`)
**למה:** "Give it the order and the order is the physics". כותבים מה נפגע ראשון, מה מתכופף, מה נשאר עומד בהתחלה, ומה קורה לחפץ אחרי ההתנגשות. יש שלילות שמכוונות לכשל הידוע: "They do not explode outward", ‏"rather than disappearing".

### V2-05 · רגש כהתנהגות: מטבח [מתוך מדריך] [CON]
```
A woman stands alone in a dim kitchen at night, checking her phone beside the counter. Her phone vibrates once.
She looks down and reads the message. Her expression does not instantly become exaggerated. Instead, her hand stops halfway toward the counter, her shoulders become slightly rigid, and her breathing becomes shallow.
Her eyes move toward the dark kitchen window first. A moment later, her head slowly follows.
She tightens her grip around the phone with one hand and takes one quiet step backward from the window.
Outside, the curtain shifts slightly.
A faint crunch of gravel is heard from somewhere beyond the window.
She freezes and keeps staring toward the sound. Never reveal another person or creature.
CAMERA, Single continuous medium shot from slightly behind and camera-left, roughly 4 meters away at chest height. Natural 50mm lens character. Very slow push forward throughout the shot. No orbit, no sudden zoom and no angle change.
LIGHT, One warm kitchen light above the counter provides the main motivated source. The window side of her face falls into deeper shadow. No beauty fill.
SOUND, Native sound only: phone vibration, refrigerator hum, subtle room tone, faint wind outside, one gravel crunch, and her increasingly audible breathing. No dialogue. No music.
Photoreal documentary suspense, natural skin texture, realistic hand movement, restrained facial performance, subtle fabric movement and natural motion blur. Photographed rather than rendered.
```
**למה:** המילה "scared" לא מופיעה אף פעם. במקומה יש פעולות פיזיות קטנות: יד שנעצרת באמצע, **העיניים זזות לפני הראש**, הנשימה נעשית רדודה, צעד אחד אחורה. אם כותבים רגש במילה, המודל משאיר את הפנים ריקות או מגזים.

## 9. דקדוק פרומפט לכל פורמט (מזוקק)

הסימון: `<...>` = חובה. `[...]` = לפי הצורך. סדר השורות הוא הסדר שהמקורות משתמשים בו.

### 9.1 סרטון מלא, multi-shot (‏Seedance 2.5, ‏10–15 ש')
```
[REF ROLES]   @Image1 is <product/person/place>; preserve <exact features>. @Image2 is <...>. All references are ingredients, never displayed as cards.
[PREMISE]     <one sentence: genre + core event>. [already in motion from the first frame.]
<t0>s to <t1>s: <shot size>, <camera rig + height + move + speed/distance>, <~NN-degree view>. <subject action, physical>. <motivated cut cue>.
Hard cut.
... (2–5s per window; one camera move per window)
Continuity: <count of people/objects>, <wardrobe/product lock>, <light direction>, <the only intentional transformation is ...>; no <shape morphing, duplicate, text overlays, logos>.
Sound: <music style or "no music">, <SFX per beat>, <dialogue rule: "the single quoted sentence is the only speech" / "No dialogue, narrator, voiceover, subtitles">.
Last frame: <exact final composition>, <still moving / camera easing out>.
```
**גרסת "LLM skill"** (‏SKA): ‏`SHOT n (a–bs)` ואחריו Subject / Action (sub-beats) / Environment / Camera / Style (זהה בכל השוטים) / Constraints, ובסוף `Negative:` אחד: כשלי AI כלליים ועוד כשלי ז'אנר.

### 9.2 שוט בודד מבוים (‏Seedance 2.5, ‏8–12 ש')
```
<Scene + subject + situation in 1–2 sentences>.
[<0:00, 0:03> beat ... (only for action beats)]
CAMERA, <start distance m>, <height>, <side>, <lens mm character>. <ONE move> <speed>. No <orbit, crane, push-in, shake...>. <subject never turns to camera>. End with <camera distance + framing>.

PERFORMANCE, <emotion written as physical behavior: eyes before head, breath, one step>.
ENVIRONMENT, <moving elements>. Never reveal <threat>.

<Photoreal style line>. Photographed rather than rendered.

### 9.3 תמונה / keyframe (‏GPT Image 2.5, ‏NB2)
```
SUBJECT / SETTING AND BACKGROUND / CAMERA (body, lens mm, f/, height m, distance m) / LIGHT (source, K, direction, stops) / SURFACE AND TEXTURE / DELIBERATE IMPERFECTIONS / COLOUR PALETTE (HEX) / COMPOSITION (thirds + room for the later camera move) + "A single normal full-frame cinematic photograph, not a contact sheet, grid, diptych…; no written text anywhere in frame."
```
עריכה, זווית או וריאנט של תמונה קיימת: `Using the reference image, <change>. Maintain the same character appearance, environment, composition, and lighting as the reference image.`

### 9.4 גיליון דמות / מוצר (רפרנס)
- **דמות (2.5):** ‏`three-panel … LEFT headless front / CENTER back / RIGHT tight face` ‏+ 18% gray ‏+ "Relight from scratch… completely flat shadowless" ‏+ "One invented person, not a recognizable public figure" ‏+ "Photographed not generated".
- **דמות ל-UGC:** ‏4 פאנלים (חזית, ¾ ימין, ¾ שמאל, מותניים), לבן ‎#FFFFFF, רשימת פגמי עור, ו-"Do not make her unusually beautiful".
- **מוצר:** מידות בס"מ, ספירה ("exactly four"), טקסט מאויית אות-אות, ‏HEX, ‏5000K key + fill one stop lower, "Every extremity inside the frame", ‏"not a dark dramatic ad frame".
- **בווידאו, שורת binding חובה:** ‏`@ImageN is a three-panel character sheet of one person: … Render one normal complete human …; never reproduce panels, gray backdrop or headless anatomy.`
- **Kling 4.0 / Elements:** ‏"assign each character and location reference a clear role", ‏~10 שניות לשוט [YT:QKBwA8VWMAY תיאור]. התבנית בסעיף 9.5 תואמת.

### 9.5 רפרנסים מתויגים (‏@Image / @Video / @Audio)
```
@Image1 is used for <who>'s <appearance/clothing/structure>; do not use <background/person/composition>.
@Video1 is used for <action/camera/pacing>; do not use <identity/clothing/scene>.
@Audio1 is used for <who>'s <voice/dialogue/ambience/music>.

```
כללים: תפקיד אחד לכל טוקן, ‏**ולכל אחד גם "do not use"**. אין טוקן בלי נכס, ואין נכס בלי תפקיד או בלי שורת Unused. אפשר לתת שמות (`@Nova`, ‏`@bike`) בממשקים שתומכים בזה. ‏Higgsfield: הטוקן שהממשק מכניס (פרק 07 §0.1).

### 9.6 ‏I2V מול Element-to-Video
- **I2V:** `Use the supplied image as the exact first frame.` ואחריו Preserve עם **camera side and framing**, ואחריו פעולה קטנה, ואחריו `Camera performs one extremely slow push… Do not change camera side, rebuild the environment or introduce a new angle.`
- **Element:** `Use the supplied image as the identity and wardrobe reference for <X> only.` ואחריו `Create a completely new shot <where>`, ואחריו CAMERA מלא.
- **First+Last:** `Use @Image1 as the first frame.` / `Use @Image2 as the last frame.` (כל אחד משפט עצמאי, מילה במילה), ואחריהם "This … frame defines …", ובסוף "naturally arrives at the last frame defined by @Image2".

```

### 9.8 ‏Negative constraints: ארבע שכבות
1. **כשלי AI כלליים** (שורה אחת): `warped faces, extra limbs, fused fingers, watermarks, subtitles, plastic CGI sheen, uncanny plastic skin, teleporting bodies, frozen background extras`.
2. **כשלי ז'אנר או סצנה** (2–3 פריטים בלבד): `horses with extra legs`, ‏`no daylight outside the windows`, ‏`don't duplicate the labels on the instrument panel`.
